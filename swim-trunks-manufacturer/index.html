Custom Swim Trunks Built Around Your Range

We customise men's swim trunks in the fabric, print and colour you choose, so you can build a range that suits your market.

Our classic trunks have an elastic waist with a drawcord and come with 5 inch, 7 inch or 9 inch inseams. Our retro short trunk has a 3 inch inseam. Shorter cuts are the norm in European markets, so we make square-leg and trunk-brief styles for them.

Quick-Dry Shell Fabrics and Liner Options

  • Fast-drying polyester

    If fast drying matters most, go for our 85/15 polyester spandex. It has around 0.4% moisture regain, dries in about 20–30 minutes in the sun and clings less when wet.

  • Softer recycled nylon

    Our recycled nylon absorbs around 4%, dries about 10 minutes slower and sits closer to the body when wet. You give up some drying speed and get a softer feel and stronger stretch recovery in return. Its partner mill uses water-saving dyes.

  • Print compatibility

    Nylon doesn't take sublimation printing, so for busy all-over sublimation prints we use polyester, not recycled nylon.

  • Movement and shape

    For longer, boardshort-style cuts, tightly woven non-stretch quick-dry polyester holds a crisp shape. For paddling and hard swimming, 4-way stretch with spandex moves with you. Short-inseam trunks use a lighter quick-dry fabric with a built-in liner.

  • Liner choices

    Our standard lining is a soft polyester mesh. To suit your market, you can switch to a soft knit brief-style lining or a compression liner, or have the trunks made unlined.

  • Specialist fabric sourcing

    Our fabric sourcing covers nylon and polyester blended with spandex, including 80% nylon 20% spandex with matching linings. On request, we also source chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL recycled nylon, REPREVE recycled polyester and Italian Carvico.

  • Production credentials

    Our sustainability provisions cover regenerated waste fibres, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

  • Water-repellent finish

    With our trunk and boardshort DWR finish, water beads up and runs off. The chemistry is fluorine-free, PFC-free and PFAS-free, so the finish works for PFAS-restricted markets, and we can send you a declaration from the finish supplier on request.

Waistband, Seam and Pocket Construction

  • Waistband components

    We enclose 35–40 mm elastic in the waistband and fit adjustable nylon drawcords with metal or moulded tips. Metal eyelets always sit on reinforced backing and never on raw fabric.

  • Lining attachment

    We anchor the mesh at the waistband and tack it at the side seams. Between those points it hangs free, so it drains, dries on its own and doesn't twist.

  • Seams and hems

    We flatlock the inner seams to stop thigh chafing and finish the leg hems with soft elastic or a 1 cm silicone gripper.

  • Sewing cost trade-off

    Tacked linings and flatlock seams take more sewing time than overlocked, fully bagged linings, and that raises your unit cost.

  • Pocket closure choices

    For your trunk design, you can specify corrosion-resistant zips, marine-grade hook-and-loop or mesh-lined zip pockets.

  • Fixed-waist fly options

    You can choose a lace-up fly with hook-and-loop or a waistband snap, a corrosion-resistant zip with a metal snap or moulded button, or a stitched-down faux fly.

  • Hybrid fly details

    Hybrid trunks take a topstitched faux fly or a working zip, snap or hook-and-loop fly. We offer five 1.2 cm belt loops around the waist.

  • Walk-short construction

    For trunks that go from the water to the street, we build a 4 cm flat-front waistband with a button or snap closure, elastic at the back and quick-dry stretch fabric.

Private Label Swim Trunks: Prints and Branding

Private-label branding

On volume orders, we offer silicone patches at the hem or waistband, heat-transfer logos, embroidered marks, centre-back woven labels, logo drawcord tips and branded eyelet hardware.

Pocket pattern matching

Before cutting, we map your print across the patch pockets and the panels underneath. Then we check on a pre-production sample that it lines up to within 3–5 mm.

Curves and stretch zones shift motifs, so we keep your key artwork clear of them.

Branding cost trade-offs

Pattern-matched pockets create more cutting waste, and every extra branding point raises the unit price. On a simple style, a single woven label or patch keeps your branding costs lower.

Style-specific pricing

Liners, zip pockets, colour-block panels and branded hardware all raise unit prices. We quote from your specification, fabric, trims, logos and quantity, and we confirm bulk pricing after review.

Taking details out or swapping them for others brings the cost down.

Logo finish durability

Our Custom Printing service offers three logo finishes. Thin, flexible transfers suit low-wear areas. Raised silicone holds its shape. Embroidery is durable but stretches the least, so we give it a soft backing and use it on low-stretch areas.

Embroidery and label minimums

Our Embroidery service offers machine or hand work in standard, 3D, reflective or stone finishes, with thread colours from our available library. Embroidery and screen printing need 50 pieces per design. Custom woven labels need 1,000 pieces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you manufacture trunks using shell and lining fabric that we supply, and what material limitations would prevent you from accepting them?

Yes. We cut and sew shell and lining fabrics you supply, or we import them from your mill using its supplier name and fabric code. Sublimation printing needs polyester, so on nylon we use digital pigment printing instead.

If I order light or white colours, how do you stop the trunks from going see-through when wet?

We use a dense 190–220 GSM warp-knit tricot for the shell and add a lining, a double layer of self fabric or a lined front panel. When there's a separate lining, we make it from 140 GSM stretch tricot.

Is the drawcord secured inside the waistband, or can it slip out through the eyelets in use or in the wash?

We secure it with a 1 cm bar-tack at centre back inside the waistband, so it can't pull through when the trunks are worn or washed. The metal or moulded tips are bigger than the eyelet opening, which gives you a second stop.

What minimum order do you need before you can put my logo on the drawcord tips and make branded eyelets?

Logo hardware starts at 1,000 pieces per item. Before we add a logo to the tips, we review your artwork and the tip material. Branded eyelets need their own tooling, so we quote once we've confirmed the tooling, finish, size and quantity.

Can you add vents or other drainage features to release trapped air and water from loose-fitting trunks?

Yes. We build drainage into loose-fitting trunks so trapped air and water can get out, which stops ballooning and drag. You can choose two reinforced 6 mm metal grommets at the rear rise, mesh panels up to 8 cm high in each side seam, or mesh-lined pockets, on their own or together. Open-mesh side and back pocket bags have eyelets at the base, so they drain even when the zip or hook-and-loop is closed. We mark the vent positions on your sample for you to approve before bulk production starts. Visible vents and mesh change the look and let light through, so they suit light-coloured or close-fitting styles less well.
