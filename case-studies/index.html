About Strandmill: Our Own Swim Factory in Guangzhou

We make your swimwear ourselves, in a single factory of our own in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Your order goes from your brief and sampling through approval, bulk production and final inspection, and then on to shipment. Because of our factory ownership and company structure, production and export sit inside one company, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control.

The factory has been running since 2011.

How a typical order moves through our factory

  1. Brief or tech pack

    You send us your brief or tech pack.

  2. Sample

    A sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

  3. Approval and deposit

    You approve the sample and we receive the deposit.

  4. Bulk production

    35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

  5. Final inspection

    A full final inspection of the finished goods.

  6. Shipment

    Goods ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms.

Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing

An Australian swimwear label moved its range to chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life and Carvico ECONYL fabrics, made in our Guangzhou factory. Fabric-related returns fell from 9.2% to 3.1% and its average rating rose from 4.1 to 4.6.

Embroidery Services

A six-store Spanish beachwear retailer moved logo embroidery on its own-label swimwear to our in-house team, with Pantone-matched threads and every unit checked before shipment. Embroidery defects at goods receipt fell from 6.0% to 0.8%, and first-sample approvals rose from 40% to 90%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you show examples of swimwear you've made for brands selling in Europe or North America?

Yes. Every year we make samples and bulk orders for more than 350 brands, and some of those labels sell in Europe and North America. Here are three recent projects, with client names kept private. A start-up bikini label in the United States came to us with sketches and a mood board for its first collection. Our in-house design team turned them into patterns graded from XS to XL, and the fit samples were ready in 10 to 15 days. The label launched six styles at our 50-piece minimum per style in each colour, shipped them DDP to its warehouse and reordered its two best sellers the next season. A swim school in Canada needed chlorine-resistant training suits in its club colours, with its logo printed and sizes from children's to adult. We matched the club colours to its Pantone references, sublimated the logo on chlorine-resistant polyester and packed mixed sizes in each carton at no extra cost. Bulk took 35 to 40 days after sample approval, and the approved style now repeats without a new sample. A plus-size swimwear label in Germany moved a range to us after cups gaped and straps dug in on its previous factory's run. We redrafted the styles on a 2XL base, wet-tested the samples on a 2XL fit model and graded them up to 5XL. An SGS inspector checked the bulk before shipment, and the label relaunched the range the following season. For those markets, we hold OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS, RCS, bluesign, ISO 9001 and WRAP, with amfori BSCI audits. Our forwarding partners ship FOB, CIF or DDP. If your market requires test reports for the styles you order, we have those styles tested at an accredited lab, and the lab fees go on your balance invoice at cost.

Have you ever taken over a production that went wrong at another factory, and what did you do?

Yes. When we take over a run that went wrong at another factory, we find the fault and get a corrected sample approved before bulk production starts again. Most failed runs come from mistakes in the pattern, grading or fit, not from the sewing. If we produce again from the same faulty pattern, the defect comes back at our factory too. Switching factories doesn't fix it on its own. Send us your approved sample or tech pack along with a few of the faulty pieces, and we'll reply within one business day. After we agree on the price, our in-house design team fixes the pattern, grading and fit and makes a new sample in 10 to 15 days. Bulk production then takes 35 to 40 days from when you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, depending on order size and season. The extra sample round means delivery takes longer than just re-running the order. If the original fault was clearly in the fabric or trims and not the fit, that extra round adds little. We also accept fabric and trims you've already bought for the order. We inspect them when they arrive and tell you about any shortages or defects within 3 business days, before cutting.

Have you worked with small start-up brands placing their first order, like us?

Yes. We work with new labels on their first order, and we offer a starter pack and separate startup packages for your first collection. The starter pack contains three finished samples, twenty fabric swatches, a catalogue of the ready-made styles we keep in stock and a short guide to launching your own swimwear brand. The startup packages cover design, development and manufacturing. Many new labels combine these packages with our consulting service. The starter pack and the startup packages are offered separately.

Can I speak to one of the brands you've worked with as a reference?

Yes, but only once that client has given written consent. When they do, we put you in touch within 5 business days of your request. We keep our private-label clients anonymous, and that includes brands whose failed production runs we've taken over. So we never share a reference contact without permission. In the meantime, we can send you our catalogue and up to three garments from earlier production runs within 1 to 2 business days, depending on availability.

How we take over a run that went wrong at another factory

  1. Compare faulty pieces

    Faulty pieces are compared against the approved sample to find the cause.

  2. Correct pattern and grading

    Our in-house design team corrects the pattern, grading and fit.

  3. Approve new sample

    A new sample is made in 10 to 15 days and approved before any bulk is cut.

  4. Bulk production

    Usually 35 to 40 days after approval and deposit.
