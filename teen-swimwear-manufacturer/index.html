Custom Teen Swimsuits Sized by Age or Height

You can order your teen swimsuits by age, in sizes 8-10 to 12-14 for tweens and young teens, or by height up to 164 cm, which is the chart we recommend for European markets.

Before bulk production starts, you approve fit samples in two sizes: one from the younger end of the range and one from the older end.

OEM Teen Swimwear Pattern Drafting, Grading and Design

We draft every teen style on its own block instead of enlarging a children's pattern.

That way it allows for a developing bust, a defined waist, fuller hips and a longer torso. We grade each of those areas separately to keep proportions, coverage, support and tension right. We also check fit in movement, where riding up and gaping show.

Our in-house team handles OEM and ODM development from your design, picture or idea. We take care of patterns, grading and fit, so there's less back-and-forth on the design.

Our Own Teen Swimwear Factory in Guangzhou, China

We make your teen swimwear in our own factory, a single site in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, and we check quality throughout production. We've been running since 2011, and through our factory and export business we keep quality, cost and delivery under our direct control.

You agree every B2B order directly with our team, not through online shops or marketplaces.

Teen Swimsuit Styles We Make

Training and sun styles are the steady core of a teen range, for lessons, clubs and holidays, while fashion trims follow trends and prints.

Classic one-pieces

We make scoop-back suits with adjustable or fixed straps, so you get a choice of straps on a classic silhouette.

Training cuts

Our chlorine-resistant cross-back and racerback one-pieces stay secure through active play, dives and turns, for school programmes and club training.

Sun coverage

For your outdoor range, we make zip-front long-sleeve UV suits and short-sleeve sun suits with leg coverage.

Fashion styles

You can choose frill shoulders, ruffle trims or dress-like skirted one-pieces. Thin straps, frills and skirts suit recreational swimming and learn-to-swim, but not squad training.

Coordinated sets

We cut boys' trunks, board shorts, rash guards and matching sets on the same age chart as the girls' styles and grade every piece from one shared base size.

Fabric Performance and Azo-Free Dye Checks

We match the fabric to how the suit will be used.

Pool performance

Chlorine-resistant fabric lasts longest with frequent swimming and keeps its colour and stretch. It feels firmer and costs more than standard stretch fabric, though.

Skin and sun

Our stretchy fabrics feel gentle on the skin. Brushed-back fabric is the softest and warmest, but it holds more water and dries slowest. UPF 50+ adds sun protection and dries as fast as a standard knit.

Azo-free dyes

We check the dyes in every fabric to confirm they're azo-free, to meet the children's compliance requirements for your goods.

Fabric sourcing service

Through our Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing service, we supply nylon or polyester blends, including 80% nylon 20% spandex with matching linings. On request, we also supply LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL, REPREVE and Italian Carvico fabrics.

Production certifications

Our production certifications cover OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Seam Construction and Skin Comfort

Soft bindings, backed zips and padding add sewing steps and raise your unit cost.

  • Seams and openings

    We overlock seams and topstitch them flat. We bind openings with soft elastic instead of bare elastic, and we loosen the leg elastic slightly so it doesn't rub or leave marks.

  • Straps and zips

    Wide straps with bar-tacked ends spread the pressure. On zipped styles, we add a chin guard and back the full length of the zip with soft fabric.

  • Padding options

    You can have sewn-in or removable foam padding, removable cup pockets and power mesh in one-pieces, cross-backs, racerbacks, zip-front UV suits, tankinis and bikini tops.

  • Structured support

    Underwire channels and moulded cups suit only the older sizes. We use them in structured bikini tops and one-piece bodices.

  • Tagless labels

    For kids' swimwear, we recommend heat-transfer tagless labels over sewn-in ones.

  • Hardware service

    Our Swimwear Hardware service has standard stock ready in 3 to 7 days and offers samples of most items. It also covers OEKO-TEX and international swimwear safety compliance, plus checks before shipment.

  • Care label service

    With our Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels service, your care labels say to hand-wash or machine-wash cold on a gentle cycle, with no bleach, tumble-drying or ironing. They also say to rinse in fresh water after swimming and dry flat in the shade.

  • Wear recovery

    For teen suits, we use chlorine-resistant elastic and fabrics chosen for strong stretch recovery and colourfastness.

Prints and Artwork Scaled Across Sizes

Pattern-based checks

Before strike-off, we check scale, placement and stretch on the garment patterns. If artwork is only judged flat, distortion, seam splits and exposed base fabric don't get resolved.

Print methods

We sublimation-print on polyester for full colourfastness. We also offer placement and engineered panel prints, with motifs scaled for each age group.

Dark print grounds

For dark sublimation prints, we print on matching pre-dyed grounds instead of white. If you need white, we back the high-stretch zones with dark lining.

Stretch approval

Before you approve a strike-off, we stretch-test it at 30% across the bust, seat and leg openings.

Print themes

For the print direction of your teen collection, you can choose from tropical, floral, geometric, rainbow and animal designs.

Price and preparation

Engineered prints, placement prints, pre-dyed grounds and rescaling add preparation, cost and lead time. Long-sleeve UV construction and frills raise the unit cost too.

Style quotations

We confirm bulk prices once we've reviewed the design and fabric. The price takes in the UPF rating, sleeves, construction, print method, artwork complexity, quantity, labels and packaging.

Custom Printing service

Our Custom Printing service sources almost any colour or print and offers digital printing. Exclusive artwork adds cost and lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you print licensed character artwork on my teen suits, and what proof of licence do you need?

Yes, as long as you own the artwork or hold the rights to sell it commercially. Before sampling, send us a written licence from the rights holder that covers the product type and the sales territory. Without it, we can't sample or produce licensed characters, logos or trademarks.

What restrictions do you apply to functional ties and decorative cords in teen swimwear for different ages and destination markets?

Our teen and kids' styles follow EN 14682 for Europe and ASTM F1816 for the US. That means no drawstrings at the hood or neck, and waist drawcords are bar-tacked at the centre with no toggles or knots. For ages 7 to 14, the free ends of functional ties and decorative cords are no longer than 14 cm.

How do you select lining and coverage to keep light-coloured teen suits opaque when wet and stretched?

For light-coloured and white teen suits, we use double shell fabric, a 140 GSM stretch tricot lining or a fully lined front panel. That keeps them fully opaque after complete immersion.

Besides one-pieces and sun suits, can you make teen bikinis and tankinis with modest coverage, and what cuts do you offer?

Yes, we make modest tankinis, including cross-strap tops with swim shorts, and age-appropriate bikinis too. You can choose high-neck or sporty crop tops, with full-coverage or mid-rise bottoms, swim shorts or skirted bottoms.
