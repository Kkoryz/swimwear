Custom Embroidered Swimwear: Flat, 3D Puff, Appliqué and Patches

Flat embroidery keeps your logo edges crisp and your fine text readable on swimwear. For raised detail, we use 3D puff embroidery, which sits slightly above the fabric. Appliqué gives you layered texture: we lay another fabric over the base and stitch it in place.

We also make embroidered patches in the size, layout and colours you specify.

Branding from Small Tags to Full Garments, Made in Our Own Factory

We handle your branding at any scale, from small embroidered tags to coverage across the whole garment.

We make your swimwear in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. We start with a small logo, and we move to larger coverage once you approve a sample on your actual swim fabric.

Which Swim Fabrics Take Embroidery Well

Embroidery works reliably on our standard nylon and polyester spandex blends from 180 gsm up, on recycled and chlorine-resistant swim fabrics, and on lined or double-layer areas.

We pick the fabric and the placement together and lean towards heavier, stable blends. We use a stretch stabiliser so the thread moves with the fabric, and we add a soft facing on the side against your skin.

Designs go in low-stretch areas such as leg hems or waistbands, because placing them in high-stretch areas causes puckering. On fabrics under 150 gsm and on mesh, ribbed or crinkle textures, we limit designs to outline or light-fill logos no wider than 5 cm.

A patch or a lined panel gives you another option. We test-stitch your fabric before sampling.

How We Keep Stitching Flat on Stretch Fabric

On stretch nylon or polyester, we prevent puckering by adapting the embroidery setup to knit fabric. We don't just copy a setup made for woven fabric.

We back the fabric with a stretch stabiliser, add pull compensation and a light underlay to the stitch file, and test-stitch on your fabric before sampling.

Our anti-pucker setup for stretch swim fabric

  1. Hoop fabric relaxed

    Hooped in its natural resting state, not stretched

  2. Back with cut-away stabiliser

    Supports the knit while it is stitched

  3. Fit ballpoint needle

    75/11 ballpoint needle for stretch nylon or polyester

  4. Lower stitch density

    About 15% lower than for woven fabric

Beading, Sequins and Hand-Crochet Embellishments

  • Hand-sewn decoration

    We decorate your bikinis and cover-ups with beaded details, beaded chains, sequins and sequin appliqués. We sew on every bead and sequin one at a time and never glue or heat-press them.

  • Stones and rhinestones

    If you ask for stone or rhinestone details, we sew them on by hand. We use heavy beading and stone work only on cover-ups and pieces that get light wear.

  • Handmade crochet trims

    We do all our crochet work by hand, and then we hand-sew the finished trims onto your garments.

  • Fabric sourcing

    Our fabric sourcing covers nylon/polyester-spandex blends, including 80% nylon 20% spandex with matching linings. On request, we also source chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL recycled nylon, REPREVE recycled polyester and Italian Carvico fabrics.

Embroidery Threads, Pantone Matching and Colourfastness

Thread and colour selection

We only use high-quality threads, and we adjust the stitching to suit your fabric. We match your Pantone references from our wide colour range, and before sampling we check the match on your actual fabric in daylight.

Swim-rated colourfastness

Our polyester embroidery threads are rated grade 4 or above for colourfastness to chlorinated pool water, sea water and sunlight, so your logos stay bright however often the suit is worn.

Existing thread reports

The threads among our tested trims are covered by our OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification, and we'll send you the reports if you ask.

Style-specific laboratory testing

If your market needs a report for the threads on the style you've ordered, we arrange testing at an accredited laboratory. We pass the lab fees on to you at cost on the balance invoice.

Production certifications

Our production certifications cover OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Working from Your Existing Embroidery Files

Already have a DST or other embroidery-ready file? We'll use it once a test sew-out on your swim fabric shows it works.

If the file was set up for woven or non-stretch fabric without underlay and pull compensation, or it has lettering under 5 mm high, we redigitise it. Redigitising adds a one-off setup charge per design.

From Artwork Submission to Inspected Production

  1. Send your requirements

    Send us your vector artwork, colour references and placement marked on the actual garment size, using our online form or email. Tell us your design, thread and embroidery type.

  2. Talk it through

    Use any of our contact options to go through your requirements with us.

  3. Agree the quotation

    In our quotation process, sampling starts once we've agreed the price. Embroidery, screen-printing and label quotes reach you within one business day. Cut-and-sew or sublimation quotes take 2 to 3 working days.

  4. Confirm colour and placement

    We confirm the thread colour match and the exact embroidery placement with you before we make any sample.

  5. Approve your sample

    We prepare a physical or digital sample and make any changes you ask for before production. Fine lettering and designs in stretch zones need a physical stitch-out, because a digital approval alone isn't enough for them.

  6. Inspect during production

    During production, we check every unit's thread, colour and alignment against the embroidery requirements you approved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you attach embroidered patches to stretch swim fabric, and can I order the patches by themselves?

We sew patches on around the edge with a zigzag stitch or coverstitch, so the seam stretches with your suit. We don't use heat-seal glue backings because they lift in chlorine and salt water. Yes, you can order patches on their own, starting at 50 pieces per design.

What is the smallest lettering, the largest embroidery size and the most thread colours you can stitch in one design on swimwear?

We can stitch lettering as small as 5 mm high with lines at least 1 mm thick. For 3D puff, the minimum is 10 mm high. One hooping fits a design up to 30 x 30 cm, and each design can use up to 12 thread colours.

Can you embroider on top of a printed swimsuit fabric or combine embroidery with sequins and appliqué in one design?

Yes. We combine embroidery with sequins, appliqué, printing, laser cutting or embossing. Once your sublimation or digital print is set, we stitch over it and match the thread to your print colours. Beads and sequins go on after the embroidery, away from seams and straps, so they don't rub or snag.

What drives the embroidery cost on my order: stitch count, colours, size, technique or number of logo positions?

Stitch count affects cost the most, and fill density drives stitch count more than size does. A solid fill costs more than an outline of the same size. Each logo position needs its own hooping and machine run. Extra colours, 3D puff, appliqué and patches add setup and handling time, and digitising adds a one-off setup charge per design. If you choose outline or open-fill designs with fewer positions and colours, you keep your cost down and the suit stays comfortable.

Can you scale the same embroidered logo across children's and adult sizes while keeping its lettering and detail readable?

Yes. We make 2 to 3 size versions, for example about 4 cm wide for children's sizes and 7 cm wide for adult sizes. We redigitise each version instead of shrinking the file, so the lettering stays at least 5 mm high and fine detail stays readable.

Can you embroider fabric or pre-cut swimwear panels supplied by us, rather than only your own production or finished garments?

Yes. We embroider fabric or pre-cut swimwear panels that you supply, from 50 pieces per design. 3% spare panels are added to cover setup and machine loss.

Which appliqué fabrics do you offer that remain compatible with the base fabric's stretch and wet-use requirements?

We use a stretch swim fabric similar to your base fabric, so both layers stretch and dry together. The options are 80% nylon 20% spandex and polyester spandex, and on request chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, recycled ECONYL nylon and REPREVE polyester. We also offer metallic foil spandex, swim-grade sequin fabric and stretch mesh. We edge them with a stretch satin stitch and back them with a soft facing for wet use.
