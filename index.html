    Our Own Swimwear Factory in China

    We make your swimwear in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

    Production and export sit within one company, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control. Our factory has been running since 2011. We check quality throughout each order and sell B2B only, through this website and our team.

    You agree every order directly with us, never through online shops or third-party marketplaces. Our production certifications include OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

    Custom Swimwear in Every Style and Size

    • Women's collections

      We make women's swimwear in contemporary and classic shapes, seamless styles included, in plain or printed fabrics chosen for your collection.

    • Men's swimwear

      We make men's swim briefs, board shorts and swim trunks in durable, quick-drying fabrics, with modern cuts, functional waistbands and secure stitching. You choose the specifications and can ask for sample adjustments before production starts.

    • Children's collections

      Our dedicated children's lines run from babies to older children and include shorts, sets and boys' pieces with rubber patch labels. We take care of everything from printing to finishing, using soft fabrics, smooth stitching and strong seams, and we test samples for stretch and durability.

    • Matching family sets

      We cut matching adult and children's pieces from a single print run, and we agree the fabric quantities for every size group beforehand. Separate runs cause shade and alignment differences that show when the pieces are worn side by side.

    • Coordinated couples' swimwear

      We also make paired swimwear for couples, building coordinated looks around the fabric, print and style specifications you choose.

    • Beach cover-ups

      We make cover-ups, sarongs, pareos and kaftans to go with your swimwear, in the silhouettes, fabrics, prints and trims you pick.

    • Resort clothing and accessories

      You can add headbands, dresses, blouses, tops, skirts and pants to your range. We make them alongside the swimwear, so you get a complete resort collection.

    • Crochet and knits

      For beachwear collections, we also make hand-made crochet pieces and space-dyed knits in 100% cotton.

    Custom Swimwear in Every Cut, Fabric and Finish

    You can choose from triangle, bandeau, halter and underwire bikinis, tankinis and monokinis, through to burkinis and high-cut one-pieces.

    • Custom fit and construction

      We build made-to-order styles on your patterns and customise the silhouettes, panels, coverage, colours, prints, embroidery, hardware, cups and performance features. During sampling we refine bikini straps, linings and closures, along with the stretch, support and shape retention of one-pieces.

    • Plus-size and larger cups

      We fit these samples on a body in the target size. Cup depth, strap angle and coverage all change between sizes, so scaling alone leads to gaping, digging or too little support. The extra sampling round adds time before bulk production.

    • Tankini and bandeau fit

      We develop tankini tops that pair with several bottoms and choose the coverage, breathability and stretch. For bandeaus, extra-stretch fabrics, the right linings and sample adjustments give a secure, comfortable grip.

    • Long-sleeve and performance styles

      We test long-sleeve samples for UV resistance and stretch and keep plus-size proportions balanced. Performance styles use compression, resistance and speed-focused fabrics, hydrodynamic patterns and reinforced seams, and we test the samples for performance accuracy.

    • Fabric sourcing options

      Our fabric sourcing includes nylon/polyester-spandex blends, such as 80% nylon 20% spandex, with matching linings. Chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL recycled nylon, REPREVE recycled polyester and Italian Carvico are available on request.

    • Custom Printing service

      Our Custom Printing service prints polyester and sun-protection fabrics on our factory's thermal sublimation printer before cutting, so printing and manufacturing happen in the same place.

    • Embroidery service limits

      Our Embroidery service offers standard machine or hand embroidery plus 3D, reflective and stone finishes. Embroidery and screen printing need 50 pieces per design, thread colours come from the available libraries, and custom woven labels need 1,000 pieces.

    • Swimwear Hardware service

      Our Swimwear Hardware service supplies colour-matched straps, rings, sliders, hooks and buckles in metal, plastic, resin or eco-friendly materials, along with drawstrings, eyelets and zippers.

    Private Label Swimwear, Branded and Packed as Yours

    • Private label collections

      Our private label service covers both existing and custom designs. Your branding goes on the finished garments, and ours never does.

    • Collection customisation

      You decide the fit, construction, materials, features, finishes and size ranges. We help you select fabrics, match colours and develop silhouettes for your label.

    • Logo positioning

      You choose where your logo goes. We confirm the label type and position during sampling, before we make your branded collection.

    • Labels against wet skin

      A woven neck label rubs against wet skin and pulls at thin necklines. A tagless heat-transfer label sits flat, and you can't feel it.

    • Individual branded packing

      We mark and pack each piece individually in custom packaging built around your logo. We work directly with you all the way through worldwide delivery.

    • Packaging and labels service

      Our Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels service includes a heat-transfer label, a hygiene liner and a hang tag, carrying your inside-back logo, washing instructions and size information. You can also add branded clips, silicone logos and fabric tags.

    • Launch service packages

      The starter pack in our Launch service contains three finished samples, twenty fabric swatches, a stock-style catalogue and a launch guide. We also offer separate packages for designing, developing and manufacturing your first collection, as well as consulting.

    From Brief to Sample to Shipment

    1. 01

      Free quotation

      For a free quote, email or WhatsApp us your tech pack or design, or coded front and back drawings or screenshots. Include your fabrics, colours, prints, quantities, sizing and packing needs.

      We assess feasibility and quote you an estimated price and timing. Then we agree the specifications, minimums and availability with you before you order.

    2. 02

      Brief and specifications

      We go through your brief with you in a consultation and confirm the technical requirements, including fabric and stretch. Then we send you digital mockups to approve.

    3. 03

      Sampling before commitment

      We source and prepare the materials, then revise and fit-test development samples. That way you can judge the product before you commit to bulk manufacturing.

    1. 04

      Written fit approval

      We don't cut any bulk fabric until you approve the final fit sample in writing. Changes made after cutting waste fabric and delay the schedule.

    2. 05

      Production and finishing

      We manage cutting, sewing and assembly, followed by washing and functional finishing. We also handle sourcing and inspections for you for the whole order.

    3. 06

      Shipment and support

      Once your goods pass inspection, we pack them and store them until they ship. After that, our team is still available for after-sales support.

    Start at 50 Pieces: MOQ, Fees and Terms

    • Minimum order quantity

      Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, so small and growing labels can place production orders.

    • First trial runs

      We agree first-trial quantities case by case. How flexible we can be on quantity depends on what production allows for your order.

    • Sample and design fees

      Each sample costs USD 50. Design costs USD 50 per design, meaning per pattern or print, with all its colourways included. Neither fee is refunded or credited against bulk orders.

    • Payment schedule

      You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order. The remaining 50% is due before your goods ship.

    • Shipping terms

      We ship FOB, CIF or DDP. With worldwide DDP you get one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle freight, customs and duties.

    • Quality problem resolution

      Under our quality terms, report any defects within 7 days of receiving your goods and send photos or videos. Once we've assessed them, we repair, remake, replace or credit the affected pieces. Shipping and handling are not refunded.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which kinds of swimwear do you make, such as bikinis, one-pieces, swim shorts and cover-ups, and is it for women, men and children?

    Yes, we make swimwear for women, men and children. That covers bikinis, tankinis, one-pieces, monokinis, rash guards, swim shorts, board shorts and swim briefs, as well as sarongs, kaftans and beach dresses. We work from your designs or from our existing styles, in sizes XS to 3XL, and up to 5XL for plus sizes.

    How do you check the fit and finish of each order before it ships?

    Before bulk production, we refine and fit-test your samples. After that we check the incoming fabric, the cutting and the sewing, run random checks during production and finish with a full final inspection. Before packing, we check the components against the sample you approved.

    Can you handle the whole job, including fabric sourcing, trims, labels and packaging, or do we have to supply some of it?

    We handle the whole job under one roof, from fabric sourcing, hardware and accessories to labels, packaging and export shipping. You don't have to supply any of these components. Because we check them all together against your approved sample, there are fewer handoffs between suppliers and less risk of mismatched trims, wrong labels or packing errors.
