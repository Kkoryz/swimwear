Your Designs or Ours, Made and Checked in Our Own Factory

For your swimwear brand launch, you can pick styles from our existing range or bring us your own designs. Either way, we make it in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Because we run a factory-owned production and export operation, we keep quality, cost and delivery under our direct control, and we check your order at every stage.

Quality checks at every stage of an order

  1. Fabric on arrival

    Incoming fabric is checked when it arrives

  2. Cutting

    Cut panels are checked

  3. Sewing

    Garments are checked during sewing

  4. Random checks

    Random checks are made on finished goods

  5. Full final inspection

    Every order gets a full final inspection

Before the Launch: Design, Samples and Certified Production

  • Design development

    Our in-house OEM and ODM development team turns your design, picture or idea into patterns, grading and fit, so there's less back-and-forth on the design.

  • Sample approval

    You judge the quality on samples first. Your bulk order goes ahead once you've approved them.

  • Production certifications

    Our certifications are OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for fabrics and trims, GRS/RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

From Pattern to Bulk: Steps, Timing and Terms

  • Starting quantities

    Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. That gives small and growing labels a place to start production.

  • Trial runs

    We agree your first trial quantity with you case by case. How far we can adjust it depends on production requirements.

  • Bulk timing

    Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit. Your order size and the season set where you fall in that range.

  • Production payments

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm your order and the other 50% before we ship the goods.

  • Shipping terms

    You can choose FOB, CIF or DDP. With worldwide DDP you get one all-in price delivered to your door, and we handle duties, customs and freight through our forwarding partners.

  • Fabric sourcing

    Our Fabric sourcing covers 80% nylon/20% spandex as well as your own textiles. If you supply fabric, give us the mill name or code. We report any shortages or defects within 3 business days, before cutting. If our error causes damage beyond the 5% overage, we pay you back at the documented purchase cost.

  • Custom printing

    Our in-factory Custom Printing service prints stocked fabrics the same day. Standard jobs take 2–3 days after you approve the artwork, and a full print run is finished within 2.5 weeks, inside the 35 to 40 days of bulk production. You approve the print by video or with a physical strike-off on your fabric.

  • Swimwear hardware

    Our Swimwear Hardware service supplies branded metal fittings that resist salt water and chlorine. Standard hardware starts at 100 to 500 pieces per colour or size. Logo or embossed stoppers, clasps, hooks, sliders and rings, and special finishes, start at 1,000 pieces per item.

Samples First: Lead Time and Fees

  • Sample timing and cost

    We make a standard sample in 10 to 15 days. Each sample you order costs USD 50.

  • Starter pack for new labels

    Our starter pack gives you three finished samples, twenty fabric swatches, a catalogue of the ready-made styles we keep in stock and a short guide to launching your swimwear brand.

  • Design fees

    The design fee is USD 50 per design, whether it's a pattern or a print, and it covers all colourways. We don't refund sample or design fees or credit them against bulk orders.

  • Courier and inspections

    Under our sample delivery and inspection terms, you pay USD 50 for courier delivery on top of the sample fees. Independent pre-shipment inspectors such as SGS or BV are welcome.

  • Quotation first

    Send us complete designs, meaning front and back images, tech packs or clear line drawings. We'll give you a sampling-to-shipping quotation within 2 to 3 working days, and sampling starts once we've agreed the price.

Launch Photography Shot From Your Own Samples

Samples to shoot

Your samples go straight from our factory to the shoot, so your images are finished before your bulk stock arrives.

Campaign and product images

On set, our partner photography team styles and shoots your collection for editorial campaign images, clean product photographs and close-up details for your online store, and we coordinate the shoot.

Consistent product pages

You can choose on-model, flat-lay or ghost-mannequin formats, or combine them. Pair your on-model campaigns with one white-background product format per category, and your store imagery stays consistent.

Locations and casting

Our partner team shoots in a Guangzhou studio or at a beach or pool chosen for your collection, and casts professional models, including plus-size models for 2XL samples and child models for kids’ swimwear.

Live shoot approval

You can attend the shoot in person or follow it over a live video link, and you approve the images as they are taken.

Sample sizes and quantities

Shoots use size M, or 2XL for plus-size styles, using 1 piece per style and colour per model. If a style is shown on two models together, we need a second piece.

Your samples returned

The shoot samples stay yours, and we ship them back to you with your completed bulk order.

True Colour, Finished Images and Shoot Pricing

  • Approved colour reference

    You approve each physical fabric colour under daylight. We then match every image to that swatch using a colour reference card and daylight-balanced lighting.

  • Faithful retouching

    We only remove dust, lint and temporary marks. Retouching never changes the fabric texture, coverage, cut or fit on the body.

  • Image files and crops

    For each style, you get 3 retouched images per look as high-resolution JPEGs at 4000 px on the long edge, along with web-optimised versions and 1:1, 4:5 and 2:3 crops.

  • Proofs and final delivery

    We send you proofs within 3 business days of the shoot and deliver your final retouched images within 10 business days.

  • Shoot pricing

    Your photo and film price depends on the number of styles, looks, models, locations, shoot days and retouched images, plus the length and number of video edits.

  • Agreement before booking

    Before we book any shoot or film work, we agree your project’s dates, deliverables and price with you.

Launch Film Cut for Social and Your Store

We plan your film and stills together and set aside time to capture movement, stretch and fit both in the water and on land.

Shared shoot production

The same partner crew films alongside your stills at the same locations, and edits the footage for your store and social channels.

Launch film formats

You can choose a hero campaign cut for launch and paid media, vertical reels and stories, and product motion clips that show movement and fit.

Branding and captions

We add logo intros, end cards and lower thirds in your brand fonts and colours, with captions either burned in or supplied as separate SRT files.

English voiceover

You get a professional English voiceover, recorded by our partner voice artists from the script you approve, and you can pick from 2 voices for your film.

Translated video versions

Each edit can have up to 5 language versions, including Spanish, French, German, Japanese or Arabic. You approve the translations in a text file before we add subtitles and on-screen text.

Paid media use

The music is commercially licensed for paid social and search advertising, and you can use your final edits with no time or territory limit.

Raw footage handover

If you ask for it, we hand over your raw footage as a file download within 5 business days of final delivery.

Brand Identity: Logo Marks, Colours and Typography

  • Logo marks for garments

    We start by designing your logo to work very small and in one colour on woven labels, hang tags and hygiene liners, and then we settle its full-colour screen version.

  • Identity beyond the logo

    Alongside your logo marks, we develop your colour palette and typography, with guidelines that show you how to use the identity across labels, tags and screens.

  • Packaging and supplied labels

    Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels uses marked, retail-ready 5-ply export cartons. If you supply your own labels, tags or bags, send 3% extra at least 7 days before cutting. We check them when they arrive and report any shortages or defects within 3 business days, before cutting.

An Online Store Ready on Launch Day

We build and set up your online store, lay out its product pages and place your images. Then we hand it over to you to own and run.

Every swimwear product page is built around fit, with a clear size guide, the size the model is wearing, the fabric composition, coverage and care notes.

Your Launch Step by Step, From Brief to Go-Live

We invoice launch services separately from production: half when you book and half on delivery of the finished work, by T/T in USD or EUR.

  1. Share your brief

    Send us your brand name, market, mood board, style images or tech packs, fabrics, collection size, quantities, branding needs, budget and shipping destination. If you don't have any assets yet, your brand name and a few reference pictures are enough to get started.

  2. Agree your plan

    We reply to your enquiry within one business day. Then we work out which launch services your project needs and quote them separately from production.

  3. Make and brand

    Branding runs alongside sampling and takes 2 to 3 weeks. We finalise it during sampling, so your labels, tags and packaging are ready for bulk production.

  4. Shoot approved samples

    Your photo and video shoot takes 1 to 2 weeks. It happens during bulk production and uses the samples you've approved.

  5. Build during production

    Your store build takes 2 to 3 weeks. It also runs during bulk production, alongside the work of getting your collection ready for launch.

  6. Complete your launch

    Allow about 10 to 12 weeks from brief to launch across all six stages. We fit sampling, branding, the shoot and the store work around production.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you shoot or build a store for swimwear I already have from another supplier, or only for styles you manufacture?

We work with swimwear from other suppliers as well as the styles we make. You can book photography, film, branding or a store build on its own, with no package or garment order needed. Send us the service you want and the materials you already have, and we'll explain what the project needs. If we manufacture your collection, we build the store around your styles, your identity and your campaign images.

What revision rounds are included for the photos and films, and which changes would require a new shoot rather than an edit?

Photo retouching comes with 2 revision rounds, and so does every film or video edit. These rounds cover colour, image selection, crops, cut order, timing, captions and music. Please put your team's feedback together in one list for each round. New models, new locations, new styling, styles that weren't in the shoot and fresh angles all need a new shoot. Branding comes with 2 logo concepts and 1 revision round on the concept you pick. Store builds come with 2 rounds of changes before handover.

What usage rights do I receive for the photos and videos, including model releases and any music licences, across my store, advertising and retailer channels?

Once you've paid, you get unlimited commercial rights to the final photos and videos. You can use them in your store, on your social channels, in paid advertising and with retail or wholesale partners, with no time or territory limits. Before every shoot, our partner photography team collects signed model releases that cover all of these uses. The only music we use is royalty-free and licensed for commercial and paid social use.

Is the launch service meant for new founders only, or can an established label use it for a new swim line?

It's for both new founders and established labels. Founders without assets can book the full package, from brief to launch. Established labels adding swimwear can pick individual services. Either way, one team runs your development, sampling and production and coordinates your launch.

Does the launch step include advertising setup, campaign management or social publishing, or does your service end with delivering the assets and store?

Our service ends when we hand over your finished assets and your store is ready to take orders. We don't do advertising setup, campaign management or social publishing. You or your marketing team handle those with the files we deliver.

What decides how much a full launch costs, and which choices make it more or less expensive?

Each shoot, film, branding and store package is priced per project, and the total moves with your number of styles, shoot locations and models, photos and film edits, video length, branding scope, packaging items and store complexity. Production costs follow design complexity, fabric choice, quantity and whether you choose custom designs or catalogue styles. Send us your styles and the services you want, and we quote production within 2 to 3 working days, with launch services quoted separately.

Will one person manage my whole launch across production, the shoot and the store?

When you enquire, we assign you a dedicated swimwear specialist. That person is your one contact for production, photography, branding and the store. A merchandiser tracks sampling and bulk production and sends you photo updates at every stage. You also deal directly with our design and production teams, with no agents in between.
