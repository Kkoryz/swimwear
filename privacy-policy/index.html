Strandmill Privacy Policy

Contact us

How We Handle Your Information

Strandmill is a business-to-business swimwear manufacturer.

When you contact us through our enquiry or quote forms, by email, or by phone or WhatsApp, we collect your name, company, contact details and any files you send, such as tech packs or artwork.

We use them only to answer you, quote, sample and handle your orders, and we keep your designs confidential. We keep these details while we work together and for as long as order records require. To see, correct or delete your details, write to sales@strandmill.com.

What this site collects

WhatWhat is recordedWhy
The enquiry formYour name, email and message; your company and phone if you give them, and that you agreed to this useTo answer your enquiry
With each enquiryThe page you sent it from, the page that linked you here (referrer) and any campaign tags in the link you followed (UTM)To know which pages and campaigns bring enquiries
Visit statistics (Umami)Pages viewed, the site you came from, browser, device type and country, and when a form is sent; counted without cookies and without identifying youTo see which pages help visitors
