The Right Way to Reach Us for Each Request

We send you a quote before we make your swimwear sample.

Once you agree the price, standard sample development takes 10 to 15 days. To request a sample, email sales@strandmill.com or use the contact form on our website. Attach your tech pack, design or reference pictures, along with your chosen fabric, colours and sample size.

You can also send these files over WhatsApp. Our quotes are free. Your enquiry goes through a feasibility check, a quotation and a product review. After that, we agree the details with you so you can place your order.

How a free quote moves from your files to a confirmed order

  1. Send your files

    Email or WhatsApp your tech pack or design with materials, prints, colours, order quantity, size chart and packing needs; screenshots or line drawings with style codes also work.

  2. We check feasibility

    We check that the style can be made.

  3. Quotation sheet sent

    We reply with an estimated price and timing on a quotation sheet.

  4. Phone call review

    We usually set up a call to go over products, artwork, sizing, quantities, pricing and minimums.

  5. Confirm and place order

    Once you confirm quantities, colours and customisation, we confirm availability and lead times and agree every detail before the order is placed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will someone reply to my message, and is it slower on weekends or holidays?

We reply within one business day, and our business days run Monday to Saturday. Sunday falls outside our working week. If you send a message during a Chinese public holiday, we answer it within one business day of our return. Our factory closes for about 3 weeks over Chinese New Year and for 7 days during National Day week (1 to 7 October), and production timelines move back by the same amount of time. If you need bulk shipment before Chinese New Year, place your order at least 8 weeks before the holiday. If you approve just before a closure, your delivery is delayed by more than the approval time itself.

Which time zone are you in, and what are your working hours?

We work on China Standard Time (UTC+8), Monday to Saturday, from 9:00 to 18:00. If you write outside those hours, we reply within one business day. To get the fastest answers, send key decisions during our working hours in China.

Which languages can I write to you in?

You can write to us in English or Chinese. If you write in any other language, we reply in English, and we keep quotations, tech packs and order documents in English. When you confirm key decisions in writing in English, everything you've agreed stays documented.

Can I visit your office or showroom in person, and where is it?

You can visit our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, during development or mass production. Our sample room displays in-stock styles and fabric swatches. Book at least 5 business days ahead by email or WhatsApp. If you can't travel, we show you the production floor on a live video call instead.
