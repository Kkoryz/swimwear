As a founder or executive, you work with us on positioning, unit economics, sourcing, sales channels and where to put your capital, the decisions that shape your brand.

Swimwear Brand Consulting Built on Execution, Not Theory

We work with founders and executives on the business decisions that shape a swimwear brand. Our swimwear consulting covers how you position the business, what each unit earns, how and where you source, which sales channels you sell through and where you put your capital.

What Our Consulting Covers and How It Differs From Design Support

Consulting ends with a written business strategy.

Design and development support ends with patterns, fits and samples. Our consulting team handles the business decisions. Our in-house design team handles pattern design, grading, fit development and sampling alongside manufacturing. Tell us whether you need a business problem solved or a product made.

We scope and invoice the two services separately, so you can book either one on its own. Finish the strategy before product work commits you to fabrics and price points.

For new labels, our Launch Services starter pack and separate startup packages give you three finished samples, twenty fabric swatches, a stocked-style catalogue and a short launch guide. You also get separate design, development and manufacturing support for your first collection.

Senior Consultants Backed by Our Own Factory

A senior consultant leads your engagement.

They have at least 10 years of experience in swimwear and apparel brands, sourcing and finance, and a research and modelling analyst supports them. You work with them directly, with no agents in between.

Before any cost, fabric or lead-time assumption goes into a deliverable, we check it with our design team and production floor. Those checks use our own factory data. An assumption about another vendor needs that vendor's quote. We make swimwear in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Because of our company and factory ownership, production and export sit within one company, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control.

Market Sizing, Customer Research and Brand Positioning

Market size and segments

We estimate your addressable market and split it into segments. You get customer profiles and an overview of the competitive landscape.

Target market coverage

One project covers up to 3 target markets, including the US, EU, UK, Australia or Canada. We size and segment each market separately.

Local selling windows

We map swim seasons, roughly March to August in the Northern Hemisphere and September to February in the Southern Hemisphere, along with year-round resort demand. That way your demand estimates and launch timing line up.

Desk research boundaries

Our standard research draws on market data, competitor ranges, retail prices and customer reviews. It tells you what competitors sell and what they charge, but not why customers choose them.

Direct customer research

When fit or price is uncertain, interviews or surveys test what customers prefer. On request, we add 8 to 12 interviews with target customers or an online survey of around 200 respondents.

Positioning grounded in gaps

Once the market and competitor analysis is done, you get a positioning statement, brand architecture and price-value map. The positioning goes after gaps competitors leave open instead of repeating the founder's preferences.

Line Plans, Target Cost Sheets and Vendor Comparisons

You get a line plan, target cost sheets and a sourcing strategy, all worked backward from your target landed cost and our minimums.

  • Collection depth first

    When something has to give, we cut styles or colourways before we touch fabric or construction quality. A narrower launch gives your customers fewer choices, but it improves sell-through and protects quality.

  • Range and launch dates

    We time your drops to each market's season and plan your bikini sets, separates and one-pieces. A first collection uses 8 to 12 styles in 2 to 3 colourways.

  • Size range planning

    We set your size range, for example XS to XL, and add cup-sized tops from B to DD where you need them.

  • Minimum order quantities

    Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. A matched bikini set counts as one piece, and separates count individually. We check your choices against your budget.

  • Cost option comparisons

    Your target cost sheets compare sublimation with screen printing, and standard with custom-branded hardware and trims. You see the difference in landed cost and unit margin before you commit.

  • Vendor assessment criteria

    We compare cost, minimums, lead times, quality and certifications across our factory, other manufacturers, mills and trim suppliers.

  • Freedom to source elsewhere

    We judge our own offer on the same criteria. Where another source suits a style better, the comparison shows it, and you're under no obligation to manufacture with us.

  • First trial runs

    We agree trial quantities with you case by case, within what production allows.

Ethical Sourcing Standards and Claim Compliance Reviews

We set ethical sourcing standards and check your claims before anything is printed. Wording the evidence doesn't support gets dropped, softened or tested, because reprinting after a challenge costs more.

  • Market-specific compliance

    We review your claims against up to 3 target markets, such as the US, EU and UK, and build your sustainability story around documented evidence.

  • Recycled-content evidence

    We check sustainability and recycled-content claims against our OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS, RCS and bluesign certificates, plus the GRS or RCS transaction certificates linked to your order.

  • Fabric certificate limits

    A fabric certificate doesn't back every finished-garment claim in every market, so your wording has to match what the documents prove for the goods you ordered.

  • Sun protection claims

    We check UPF 50+ claims against AS/NZS 4399 documentation. That standard rates dry, relaxed fabric, not how the fabric performs wet or stretched.

  • Garment labelling checks

    Before your labels and hangtags are printed, we review fibre-content and care labels, plus the drawstring and small-part rules for children's swimwear.

  • Packaging and extra tests

    We check “recyclable” and “compostable” wording on your packaging. Any additional evidence you need, including wet/stretched UPF results or reports on the styles you ordered, comes from accredited labs, and you pay their fees at cost.

  • Fabric sourcing options

    Through our fabric sourcing you get matching linings, blends such as 80% nylon 20% spandex and, on request, LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL, REPREVE and Italian Carvico.

Sales Channels, Online Returns and Wholesale Timing

Wholesale cash trade-off

Wholesale ties up cash in production earlier and earns a lower margin per unit than direct sales, so we let its calendar set production dates before we build your online pricing.

Channel strategy outputs

We plan your mix of direct-to-consumer and wholesale sales. You get a channel strategy, a pricing and promotions framework, and an e-commerce roadmap.

Online fit and returns

We build size guides, fit notes and model measurements into your product pages, allow 25 to 35% for returns in your online margins and review return reasons every season.

If you leave returns out, your margin looks higher than it really is.

Wholesale calendar first

Buyers place orders 6 to 9 months before the selling season. We schedule line sheets, samples and production around those windows so bulk orders arrive before stores need them. If you miss the window, you lose that season.

Our sales and marketing strategy defines your sales funnel, your target wholesale accounts and the metrics you track.

You get a go-to-market plan, a marketing mix, funnel KPIs, target account lists and outreach materials for your team to put to work. Before handover, name someone on your team to own wholesale outreach.

Your team makes the contacts, so buyer relationships stay with your brand and not with an outside adviser. When it's time to execute, Launch Services produces campaign photography, video, branding and e-commerce content and builds your online store.

We then hand it all over for you to own and run. Wholesale sales calls aren't part of Launch Services.

Financial Models, Margins and Inventory Planning

Unit economics and capital

We analyse what each unit earns, build gross margin bridges and assess how quickly you recover your cash. You get a financial model, a margin analysis and a capital-needs plan.

Pre-launch model inputs

Before you make your first sale, we work from your target prices, landed-cost estimates based on real fabric and production quotes, and swimwear benchmarks for conversion, returns and acquisition costs.

Three financial scenarios

We model conservative, base and upside scenarios that are ready for investor scrutiny. Once you start selling, you swap the benchmark assumptions for your actual results.

Seasonal size planning

We size your first buy to the selling window and build its size curve from fit data and category benchmarks, for example S:M:L:XL at 1:2:2:1.

Restocking proven sellers

Your first buy stays small and focuses on core sizes. Repeat orders take 35 to 40 days and restock your best sellers, so you don't tie up cash in excess stock upfront.

End-of-season cash protection

We build markdowns into your cash forecast. Unsold sizes and colours are the most common reason a young swimwear brand runs short of cash before its second season.

Process Improvement and Team Structure

Before you hire anyone, we map your processes and set out who makes which decisions.

Our operations optimisation work gives you a process map, a KPI dashboard and a cost-reduction roadmap, backed by performance management. Our organisational structure and talent work clarifies roles, assigns decision rights and sets hiring priorities together with you.

You get an organisation design, a hiring plan and an analysis of what to build in-house and what to hand to partners. Clear responsibilities and a short KPI list show you where the gaps are, and your first hires fill the roles partners can't cover well.

Fixed-Scope Projects and Choosing an Engagement Format

Fixed-scope launch projects

Your pre-launch decisions make up a finite project with agreed objectives, deliverables and timeline. We fix the schedule at the start: 6 to 8 weeks from initial consultation to implementation roadmap.

Single workstream timing

A focused workstream, such as sourcing strategy, takes 3 to 4 weeks and settles the decision you need without you commissioning a full brand project.

Weekly working sessions

Fixed-scope engagements include one 60-minute working session a week over video, and you can attend any session in person at our Guangzhou factory.

Changes agreed before work

On every consulting engagement, we agree any scope change with you in writing and quote it before the extra work starts.

Choosing your format

If you have a quick question, start with a one-off session. Fixed-scope work settles your launch decisions, and a retainer supports ongoing review once you have live sales and production results.

Advisory Retainers: Sessions, Billing and Notice

  • Scheduled advisory time

    If you're scaling your brand, the advisory retainer gives you two 90-minute working sessions a month with senior advice.

  • Between-session access

    Between sessions, you can email or message your senior advisor. We reply within one business day to quick checks that unblock your team.

  • Focused decision sessions

    Bring a short list of decisions to each session. Detailed pricing, sourcing and range planning belong in focused working time, not in messages.

  • Monthly retainer payment

    We invoice consulting separately from production. You pay your retainer at the start of each month by T/T bank transfer in USD or EUR.

  • One-off session payment

    You pay for a one-off consulting session in full before it starts, also by T/T bank transfer in USD or EUR.

  • Minimum term and notice

    A retainer runs for at least 3 months. After that, either of us can end it with 30 days' written notice.

How Engagements Start: Inputs, Steps and a Roadmap

Before we start, send us your target markets, delivery countries, expected quantities, budget and launch date, plus any sales, margin and cost data you have.

  1. Agree the engagement

    In the initial consultation, we agree the problem, scope and outcomes with you before the work begins.

  2. Diagnose the business

    We work from your real quantities, costs and margins. If you don't have sales data yet, we work from your brand brief, target prices, competitor benchmarks and our production cost estimates.

  3. Design the recommendations

    We base every recommendation on costs and order sizes, so it still works once your styles reach sampling and bulk production.

  4. Decide the implementation

    We tie the outcomes we agreed to implementation decisions and lay out the work in a roadmap your team can carry out.

  5. Deliver the roadmap

    You get an implementation roadmap with owners, milestones and KPIs, so it's clear who is responsible for what and how progress is measured.

  6. Continue into execution

    An advisory retainer reviews your progress against the roadmap. For product recommendations, we also offer design, fabric sourcing, production in our factory and delivery.

Samples, Bulk Production, Payment and Shipping Terms

  • Standard sample timing

    Working from your brief or tech pack, we have a standard sample ready in 10 to 15 days.

  • Sample and design fees

    Each sample costs USD 50. Design costs USD 50 per design, pattern or print, and that covers all colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

  • Bulk production timing

    Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Order size and season set the exact timing.

  • Production payment schedule

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before it ships.

  • Final inspection and shipment

    We carry out a full final inspection, then ship your order on FOB, CIF or DDP terms.

  • Worldwide DDP delivery

    DDP is available worldwide. You pay one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle duties, customs and freight.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I hire you for consulting without placing a production order, including if I keep my existing manufacturer?

Yes. Consulting doesn't require a garment order or any production commitment. You can stay with your existing manufacturer and use us for strategy only.

Is this consulting meant for a brand at my stage, from pre-launch to established label?

Yes. We tailor the scope to your stage, whether you're a founder before launch, a label on its first collection, a direct-to-consumer brand moving into wholesale or an established label repositioning.

Can I commission only one workstream, such as sourcing strategy or financial planning, rather than a full brand consulting project?

Yes. A single workstream has its own fixed scope, deliverables and timeline, and you can add others later. One workstream takes 3 to 4 weeks.

What decides how much a project or retainer will cost me?

Project fees depend on the scope, the number of workstreams, the duration and the deliverables you need, including financial modelling or direct customer research. Retainer fees depend on the number of monthly sessions and the advisory time between them. We confirm your fee in writing before any work starts.

Do we own the models and strategy documents you deliver, and can we use them with other suppliers or investors?

Yes. Once you've paid the related fees, the models, reports, strategies, line plans and other files we deliver belong to you, and you can use them with suppliers, partners or investors. We keep copies only to handle your reorders and never use them for other clients.

How far before our swimwear collection launch should consulting begin for us to receive and use the recommendations in time?

Start 6 to 9 months before launch. A fixed-scope project delivers its recommendations within 6 to 8 weeks. That leaves time for 10 to 15 days of sampling, 35 to 40 days of bulk production and shipping before the selling season. Settle your line plans and costing before sampling, because late decisions squeeze production time.

Will we receive editable financial models, cost sheets and plans that our team can update after the engagement?

Yes. You get models and cost sheets in editable Excel or Google Sheets, with the formulas and assumptions open. Plans and strategy documents come in editable Word, Google Docs or PowerPoint, so your team can keep updating them.
