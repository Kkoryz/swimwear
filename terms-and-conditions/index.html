Terms and Conditions for Working With Strandmill

Contact us

Who We Are and Variances on Stock Styles

We make all of our swimwear ourselves, in our one factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Stock styles can vary slightly in size, construction, colour or shade from item to item. As a factory-owned B2B business, we keep production and export within one company, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control. We sell only through our website and our team.

There's no online shop or third-party marketplace store, and you agree every order directly with us.

Quality Claims, Returns and Outside Vendor Liability

When you inspect your goods and send us evidence for specific cartons, we can trace a quality problem and work out a remedy without the goods being shipped back.

  • Inspect on arrival

    As soon as your goods arrive, inspect a sample from each carton before any decoration or relabelling. This inspection is your responsibility.

  • Report within seven days

    Report any quality problem within 7 days of receipt. Include photos or videos that show the defect, the carton markings and the labels.

  • Assessment and remedy

    We use your evidence to trace the production lot and assess the cause. Then we repair, remake or replace the pieces, or credit you their cost.

  • Shipping costs remain payable

    We don't refund shipping and handling charges, and you pay the courier and shipping charges for any replacement pieces.

  • Written return authorization

    Get our written return authorization number before you return anything. We refuse any package or carton that doesn't show it.

  • Authorized return charges

    An authorized return carries a 20% restocking fee, and we bill return freight separately on top of that fee.

  • Excluded goods and samples

    Samples can't be returned. Neither can goods altered by screen printing, embroidery, relabelling, washing, dyeing, embellishment or any other means.

  • Outside vendor liability

    We aren't liable for work done by third parties or outside vendors, and our quality remedies don't cover defects caused by that work.

Samples, Order Confirmations, Shipping and Refunds

We quote before we make any samples, and sample-related fees stay separate from your bulk order.

  • Price agreement first

    We agree the price with you through our quotation process before we move on to making your sample.

  • Sample development

    Our development process turns your brief or tech pack into a standard sample in 10 to 15 days. You approve its quality before bulk production and full final inspection.

  • Sample and design fees

    Each sample costs USD 50. The design fee is USD 50 per design and covers its pattern or print and all its colourways.

  • No fee credits

    We don't refund sample and design fees, and we don't credit them against your bulk order.

  • Sample courier charge

    Courier delivery of samples costs you USD 50, on top of the sample fee.

  • Enquiry response time

    When you contact our team, we reply to every enquiry within one business day.

Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour.

You pay a 50% deposit at confirmation and the other 50% before shipment. Settle sizes, colours, quantities and artwork before you confirm. We book materials and production time soon after, so later changes cost money. Cancel within 24 hours of confirmation for a full refund.

After that, or once goods are ready or shipped, there's no refund.

Once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days.

Your order size and the season set where it falls in that range. We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. DDP is available worldwide: you pay one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle the duties, customs and freight.
