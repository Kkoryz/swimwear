    Target Dates, Rush Orders and Realistic Timing

    We check your required delivery date against our timelines, which are 10 to 15 days for sampling and 35 to 40 days for bulk production.

    Then we put realistic timing on your swimwear quotation sheet. You don't need a target date to ask for pricing. If your deadline is tighter, we offer rush production when our schedule allows it.

    We quote the rush surcharge as a percentage of the order value, and you pay the full surcharge with your 50% deposit.

    Minimum Order Quantities, Trial Runs and Lead Times

    • Minimum order quantity

      You can start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, which works well for small and growing labels.

    • First trial run

      We agree your first trial run with you case by case. How flexible we can be on quantity depends on what production allows.

    • Bulk production start

      Bulk production starts once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit. Your order size and the season set the production timing.

    What to Send for a Free, Accurate Quotation

    Please send a complete brief: missing details mean follow-up questions or assumptions in the quotation, which delays our reply.

    1. Send your style details

      Send us your tech pack or design, along with your preferred fabrics, colours, prints, size chart, quantity per colourway and packing needs. This lets us check whether your swimwear can be made.

    2. Choose your format

      If you don't have a tech pack, send front and back screenshots or clear line drawings marked with style codes, fabrics and colours. You can send your brief by email or WhatsApp.

    3. Receive your free quotation

      Once we have complete design information, we prepare your free quotation sheet within 2 to 3 working days. It estimates price and timing for sampling, fabrics, accessories, branding, labelling, production and shipping.

    4. Check service response times

      For screen printing, embroidery and labels, we answer within one business day. Cut-and-sew or sublimation quotations take up to 3 business days. Multi-colourway team programmes get a separate quotation for each configuration.

    5. Review the details together

      We set up a phone call with you to go through products, artwork, sizing, quantities, pricing and minimums, and to settle anything your first brief left open.

    6. Confirm before you order

      You confirm quantities, colours and customisation. We confirm availability, lead times and every other detail before you place your order. Once you agree to the quoted price, we start making your sample.

    Sample Fees, Payment Terms and Shipping Options

    We confirm components and consumption before we commit to a bulk cost. Before sourcing, any price is an estimate, and specialty trims can make sourcing take longer.

    • Component selection

      Choosing the fabrics, ribbons, hardware and tags for your style takes about a week. With some suppliers it takes longer.

    • Material consumption and costing

      We work out the components each piece needs and the total across your bulk order. We then use those figures and component availability to estimate your bulk production and total product costs.

    • Sample and design fees

      Each sample costs USD 50. The design fee is USD 50 per design and covers the pattern or print and all its colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

    • Standard sample development

      With our standard sample development, your sample is ready in 10 to 15 days.

    • Deposit and balance

      You pay a 50% deposit when your order is confirmed and the remaining 50% before shipment.

    • Payment methods

      Under our payment terms, you pay the deposit and balance by T/T bank transfer in USD or EUR. For samples and courier charges, you can also pay by PayPal.

    • Shipping terms and coverage

      We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. With DDP, available worldwide, you pay one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle duties, customs and freight.

    • Final inspection before shipment

      When bulk production is finished, we do a full final inspection before we ship your order.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which currency do you quote in, and does the price include shipping and import duties?

    We quote in US dollars or euros, whichever you prefer, on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. The terms we agree on decide what the price covers for shipping and duties. With DDP, you pay one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we take care of duties, customs and freight. DDP is available worldwide.

    How long is the quoted price valid, and can it change before bulk production?

    Your quotation is valid for 30 days from the date on it. During that time, the price changes only if you change the fabric, print, trims, quantity or packing. If you approve the sample and pay the deposit while the quote is still valid, the price is fixed for that bulk order's quoted specification. If you change that specification later, we reopen the price. After 30 days, we recheck current fabric and trim costs and send you an updated quotation.

    Will you sign an NDA and keep the designs we send confidential?

    Yes. We sign an NDA or cooperation agreement, and we keep your designs, prints, patterns, brand name and ideas confidential. We use the patterns you give us only for your company. We never reveal your brand without your approval, and private label clients stay anonymous.

    Which payment methods do you accept for the deposit and the balance?

    You pay both the 50% deposit and the remaining 50% balance by T/T bank transfer in USD or EUR. The deposit is due when your order is confirmed, and the balance is due before shipment. For sample fees and courier charges, you can also pay by PayPal.
