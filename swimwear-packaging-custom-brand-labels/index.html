Branded and Ready to Sell, or Packed in Your Own Materials

We finish your swimwear order with hang tags, garment labels, hygiene liners and bags, so every piece leaves our factory branded and ready to sell. If you'd rather supply your own labels or packaging, we attach them and pack your finished pieces in your own materials.

Branding Add-On Costs and Supplying Your Own Materials

Each branding extra, from embroidered labels and DTF logos to printed bags and branded liners, adds to your per-piece cost, and we list each one separately on our quotation.

Start with one or two customer-facing extras to keep your first selection focused, then let sales guide what you add on reorders. We check any materials you supply and report issues before cutting.

How we handle the labels, tags and bags you supply.

  1. Send 3% extra

    Add 3% extra of each label, hang tag and bag to cover wastage.

  2. Arrive before cutting

    Everything reaches our factory at least 7 days before the scheduled cutting date.

  3. Count and check

    We count and check your materials on arrival.

  4. Report any issues

    We report any shortages or defects to you within 3 business days, before cutting.

Custom Swimwear Labels, Branded Bags and Lead Times

We develop and approve your swimwear labels and packaging alongside your samples. When your branding is settled before bulk production, your schedule is several weeks shorter.

Main label choices

We build your branding into production with woven main and neck labels, plus satin, cotton, transparent, stamped, printed, heat-transfer and silicone options.

Care and size labels

Your care, wash and size labels come woven, printed or heat-transferred. Heat-transfer care labels keep the suit tagless, and woven or printed labels come with recycled-material options.

Finished garment logos

If you want branding on the finished piece, we print your logo after sewing. Labelling is an add-on and doesn't count towards your garment minimum quantities.

Branded bag charges

Logo-printed zip-lock bags start at 300 pcs with a USD 35 screen fee. A printing screen for cotton drawstring bags is also USD 35.

Sampling and mockups

We develop your heat-transfer labels, hang tags, hygiene liners and gift bags with your samples, prepare mockups for you to review, and lock in prints and packaging before bulk.

Accessory preparation

Labels and hangtag accessories take 7 to 10 days to prepare, so we build their development into sampling.

Quotation timing

Our quotation service answers screen-printing, embroidery and label requests within one business day. Cut-and-sew or sublimation quotations take 2 to 3 working days.

Tagless Heat Transfer Labels Printed Inside Each Piece

  • Comfort against skin

    We heat-press soft labels directly onto the inside of each piece in our factory. This option needs simple, bold artwork, and that applies to both samples and bulk.

  • Templates and colours

    You choose our template or your own artwork, a standard or custom shape, and black or white printing.

  • Size and legibility

    Finished labels run from 2 x 1 cm to 8 x 6 cm, and every text element must be larger than 5pt.

  • Artwork file formats

    Send vector artwork with outlined fonts as AI, EPS or PDF, or a PNG with a transparent background at 300dpi or higher.

  • Width and sizing

    Give us your exact print width in centimetres, within the finished-size range, and send your size guide as a separate file.

  • Fabric suitability

    Our tests support heat transfers on swimwear and activewear fabrics, except textured swim fabrics such as textured recycled nylon. Those fabrics need a different label option.

  • Custom printing artwork

    For Custom Printing, send 300 dpi artwork, with scaled, seamless repeats for all-over prints. We adjust repeat, scale and placement to each style.

DTF Logo Transfers for the Outside of the Suit

Exterior colour branding

We match DTF logos to your Pantone references and place them on the outside of the suit.

Flat fabrics only

Our testing supports DTF on all flat swimwear and activewear fabrics. Textured fabrics need a different branding option.

Dimensions and sampling

We produce DTF at any size up to 35 x 10.7 cm (13.8 x 4.2 in), and a DTF sample takes 2-3 days.

Solid logo limits

DTF stretches far less than swim fabric, so large solid logos stiffen the area and lift or crack when stretched. For that reason, solid areas on stretch zones are capped at 8 x 8 cm.

Placement and open designs

We turn larger artwork into outlines or open designs with gaps of at least 1 mm. We place logos on lower-stretch areas: hips, centre backs or leg hems.

Thickness and durability

DTF is about 0.2 to 0.3 mm thick, so it's firmer, less stretchy and longer-lasting. Inside heat transfers are about 0.1 mm thick and softer, and they move with the fabric.

Embroidered Logos for Stretch Swim Fabrics

Our Embroidery service adds embroidered logos at low-stretch hems or waistbands on swim fabrics from 180 gsm; on lighter or textured fabrics, we use small outline logos or patches.

Hang Tag Shapes, Paper Stocks and Finishes

We match the shape and board weight of your hang tag to your brand's look and to the size of the garment.

  • Six shape options

    You can choose from 6 hang-tag shapes: rectangles, squares, a circle and an oval. Each one is sized for a different way of presenting the garment.

  • Rectangle dimensions

    For a rectangular branded hang tag, choose 5 x 9 cm, or 4 x 10 cm for a slim version.

  • Square dimensions

    Our standard square and our rounded-corner square are both 6 x 6 cm, so you get two corner styles at the same size.

  • Circle and oval

    The round hang tag is 6 cm across. For a narrower format, the oval is 5 x 8 cm.

  • Paper and finishes

    You can have 350gsm, 400gsm or 600gsm paper board, or 350gsm or 400gsm recycled stock. Each comes in black or white, with a gloss or matte finish.

  • Printed brand details

    Our packaging suppliers print your logo and brand details on the tag, leaving room for your brand story and QR codes.

  • Branded hardware alternative

    With Swimwear Hardware, each logo mould costs USD 200 and unit prices depend on the design. Resin O-rings start at 100 pieces per colour, and branded metal tags or embossed labels start at 100 pieces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which packaging is recyclable or industrially compostable, and do those claims cover printed packs?

Our corn-starch bags break down under industrial composting conditions, but not in home compost or landfill. Our eco zip-lock bags are reusable, and for any recycling claim we send you the material supplier's data. The supplier data covers only the bag material, not your printed finished pack. You check the disposal and warning wording against your market's and your retailer's rules, and only wording you've approved in writing goes to print.

What shelf life and storage conditions should we plan for before corn-starch bags become brittle, sticky or otherwise unsuitable for sale?

Our corn-starch bags stay strong, clear and ready to sell for 12 months from production when they're sealed in cartons below 30°C and out of direct sunlight. Plan to sell through within that window. Long exposure to temperatures above 40°C or to direct sun shortens their life and turns the film brittle or tacky. That's why your bags are made close to shipment.

Can your bags be printed with suffocation warnings and meet FBA or retailer polybag rules?

Yes. Our bag suppliers print suffocation warnings in the languages you choose, in type of at least 10pt. For Amazon FBA, we use 50 µm (about 2 mil) film that's clear enough for barcode scanning, and we put warnings on every bag with an opening of 12.7 cm (5 in) or wider. For other retailers, we match the thickness, warning text and label placement to the packaging manual you send us.

How do you prevent dark or sublimated swimwear fabrics from discolouring a white heat-transfer label?

On dark, bright or sublimated fabrics, our label supplier prints white labels over an anti-migration blocker, and we press them at about 130°C so the dye doesn't bleed into the print. Before bulk, we check a pressed strike-off on each fabric. If the dye still shows through, we switch that label to black.

My collection uses textured fabric. Which label should I choose instead of a heat transfer or DTF label?

On textured, ribbed or crinkle swim fabrics, we use an embroidered label on a low-stretch waistband or leg hem. The other option is a heat transfer on the smooth inner lining instead of the textured outside. Men's and boys' swim trunks can also take a sewn-on rubber patch.

How are hang tags attached without snagging or putting holes in the swimsuit?

We loop cotton or recycled-polyester string, or ribbon, around a strap or through the care label, so the swim fabric is never pierced. If you'd rather have a plastic fastener or safety pin, we attach it only through the sewn-in care label or the seam allowance, never through the body fabric. We use a fine 1 mm needle so it doesn't snag.
