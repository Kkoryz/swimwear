OEM, ODM and Private Label Baby Swimwear

You can start your baby swimwear order with a tech pack, a reference sample or a clearly defined product concept, and we develop the goods from there. We make custom baby and kids’ swimwear for you under OEM, ODM or private label arrangements.

Launch serviceOur Launch service confirms testing, documents, sampling and production schedules for the design and materials you choose, checked against your destination market and launch date.

Wholesale MOQ, Lead Times, Payment and Shipping Terms

  • Minimum order

    Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. That keeps production within reach of small and growing labels.

  • First trial run

    We agree your first trial quantity case by case, and our flexibility goes as far as production allows.

  • Production timing

    Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days from sample approval and receipt of your deposit. Your order size and the season set where your order falls in that range.

  • Deposit payment

    We take a 50% deposit when you confirm your order, before bulk production starts.

  • Balance payment

    You pay the remaining 50% before shipment, which completes payment for the confirmed order.

  • Final inspection

    Your completed bulk order goes through a full final inspection before we ship it.

  • Shipping terms

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. Worldwide DDP includes delivery to your door, and we handle duties, customs and freight through our forwarding partners.

Girls' and Boys' Baby Swim Styles and Size Ranges

  • Girls’ and boys’ styles

    For girls, we make crisscross-strap and tank one-pieces, swim dresses and tankinis. For boys, we make swim trunks with soft elastic enclosed in the waist, cut to sit over a swim nappy, in solids or prints.

  • Your size run

    We produce any baby, toddler or kids’ size run to your grading rules and size labels, and we set chest and waist measurements from your measurement sheet.

  • Baby base chart

    Our baby chart goes by height and weight: 0–3M is 55–62 cm and 4–6 kg; 3–6M is 62–68 cm and 6–8 kg; 6–12M is 68–76 cm and 8–10 kg; 12–18M is 76–82 cm and 10–11.5 kg; 18–24M is 82–88 cm and 11.5–13 kg.

  • Toddler sizes

    After 24 months, sizes move to our 2T to 4T toddler size chart, set out by height and weight.

  • Matching gift sets

    You can pair a swimsuit with one or two matching hats, headbands or swim nappies. The print and size stay consistent, so the pieces make one coordinated gift.

  • Gift set packing

    We label every component for packing and restocking and pack it in your gift box, printed pouch or hanger card. Then we ship in 5-ply export cartons marked with the style, colour and size breakdown.

  • Matching accessories

    Our custom bucket hats, scrunchies and headbands start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. We make them in your swimwear fabric or print so they match the range.

  • Infant openings and trims

    Infant suits open with crotch or shoulder snaps or a full-length front or back zip with a soft guard, and carry no removable decorations. The pull tests and metal limits every trim meets are on our children’s swimwear page.

Sun-Protective Fabrics Made for Sensitive Skin

Fabric choiceWhat it means for your range
Polyester/spandexFor a range aimed at the pool, polyester keeps its colour and shape through repeated swims in chlorinated water.
Nylon/spandexFor softness and beach use, nylon/spandex holds up to saltwater and sunscreen without losing quality. With frequent chlorine exposure, though, it loses colour and shape sooner.
Design requirementsWe offer fabrics suited to sensitive skin, recycled and custom stretch options, UPF 50+ fabrics and quick-dry choices. We set print scale, lining and stretch recovery to match your design.
Specialist sourcingThrough Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing, we supply blends such as 80% nylon 20% spandex with matching linings, and we source LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL, REPREVE and Italian Carvico when you ask for them. For weekly toddler lessons, we specify the firmer chlorine-resistant swim-school fabrics used in our children's range. Before sampling, we confirm that the baby fabrics, linings and trims you choose are certified to OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Product Class I for harmful substances, and the certificates go in your order documents. For the US, CPSIA calls for testing at a CPSC-accepted third-party lab, whose reports back the Children's Product Certificate (CPC) the importer issues; REACH is tested substance by substance. We arrange both at cost.

Customisation, Decoration and Private Label Branding Options

Style customisation

You choose the fabric, colour, print, size, trims, labels and packaging. We make solid or printed styles using cut-and-sew, digital printing, sublimation, embroidery and lining work.

Comfortable decoration

On all-over polyester prints, the sublimation dye bonds into the fibre, so the print stretches without cracking. We keep embroidery and heavy logos away from areas that touch the skin, and we use flat printed labels so nothing rubs.

Brand labels

Our Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels service adds your branding during manufacture. That covers heat-transfer care labels for tagless suits, recycled woven or printed labels, and logos printed after sewing.

Printing inks

Our Custom Printing service prints your fabrics, digital prints included, with OEKO-TEX certified, environmentally responsible inks.

Fabrics and Construction That Keep Baby Swimsuits in Shape

To stop the seat sagging when it's wet, we use 180 to 200 gsm nylon or polyester spandex fabric with at least 18% spandex.

We add a full lining of 110 to 130 gsm at the seat. A suit gets heavier when it's wet, and stretch recovery and seat support are what hold its shape. We use chlorine-resistant elastic at the waist and legs.

Leg openings are finished with soft, flat elastic at gentle tension. Flatlocked or covered seams sit away from the inner thigh and crotch, so they don't rub while babies crawl and kick. For shape that lasts longer, LYCRA Xtra Life is available.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you adjust infant patterns to accommodate a swim nappy without making the waist and leg openings loose?

We add 2–3 cm of rise and seat room through the back and crotch. Soft elastic bindings, cut 10–15% shorter than the openings, keep the waist and legs close-fitting. Before grading, we check every infant fit sample over a swim nappy.

Can you build a swim nappy layer into baby swimsuits or trunks, or make reusable swim nappies to sell alongside them?

Yes, we make both. Built-in layers use a soft inner brief with snug elasticated waist and leg cuffs. Separate reusable swim nappies come in sizes 0–3M to 24M and start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. We settle built-in nappy features before the first fit sample.

For infant swim trunks, what waistband options can replace exposed drawstrings to avoid entanglement risks?

You can choose soft elastic fully enclosed in a 3 cm fabric casing, a flat-front waistband with elastic at the back only, or a drawcord kept entirely inside the waist channel. Our kids’ trunks follow EN 14682, and under-7 sizes have no drawcords hanging outside the garment. We settle the enclosed-elastic construction before the first fit sample.

Do you make thermal-lined swim warmers as well as standard stretch swimwear?

Yes. We make swim warmers with a soft brushed thermal lining for babies who get cold quickly, starting at 50 pieces per style in each colour. We confirm the thermal lining before the first fit sample.

Which baby-specific features, such as a nappy layer, zips, snaps, UPF fabric or matching accessories, add to the unit cost?

Built-in nappy layers, guarded zips, snap closures, UPF 50+ fabric, all-over custom prints and matching hats or accessories all raise your unit cost above a plain lined suit, because each one adds materials and sewing time. Branded or recycled fabrics also cost more than our standard blends.
