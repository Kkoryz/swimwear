About Us: Strandmill's Own Guangzhou Factory

Since 2011 we have run bulk lines of about 100,000 pieces a month alongside a 65-person sampling studio, so your collection gets its own development capacity at any order size.

What We Make In-House and How Much Each Month

We make about 100,000 swimwear pieces a month in bulk, so we take on everything from small runs to large-volume orders. Our 65-person sampling studio works alongside the bulk lines and turns out about 55 custom samples a day. That gives your collection its own development capacity.

Our Story: One Guangzhou Factory Since 2011

We've made swimwear in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, since 2011. We're one company that owns its factory and runs both production and export, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control.

The Brands We Work With Directly

Every year we make samples and bulk orders for more than 350 brands, with design, production and private labelling for clients from small businesses to large enterprises.

First-time and emerging brands

If you're a founder, we help you turn your ideas into specifications. We work with private-label start-ups and with emerging bikini labels that are planning seasonal online launches.

Established and apparel labels

We produce to your tech packs. We work with swimwear, resort, fashion and activewear labels, and with boutique and apparel brands.

Retail and trade buyers

E-commerce swim and sports labels, online retailers, wholesalers and importers work with us on their sampling and bulk orders.

Specialist organisations

We also manufacture for swim schools, surf shops, lifeguard programmes, resorts and event organisers who buy directly from our factory.

Direct business orders

We sell B2B only, through this website and our team, and we agree every order directly. We don't have an online shop, a third-party marketplace or a platform store.

Everything Under One Roof, From Pattern to Carton

The pattern maker and team who make your sample also make its bulk adjustments.

That way one team handles fit corrections from the first pattern to the sealed carton. We do design development, digital patterning, fabric sourcing, sampling, printing, embroidery, cutting, sewing, hardware, accessories, labels, finishing, packing and export shipping ourselves. No stage goes to an outside workshop.

When you buy from us, you buy from the factory that sews your swimwear.

Order Minimums for Small and Growing Labels

Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour.

For your first trial order, we agree the quantity with you case by case, within our production limits. Small or complex runs go on flexible setups, and high-volume repeat styles go on dedicated bulk lines.

Our experienced technicians run modern, automated cutting and sewing machinery around the clock, including flatlock machines and sublimation printers. Production follows ethical labour standards. If you're an established label, you get dedicated project management.

How an Order Moves From Sample to Shipment

OEM and ODM development starts from your design or idea; samples take 10 to 15 days and cost USD 50 plus USD 50 a design.

  1. 01

    Final sample approval

    You approve the final sample in the actual bulk fabric, colour and print. That signed-off piece becomes the sealed production reference.

  2. 02

    Order confirmation

    We take a 50% deposit when you confirm your order. Bulk production starts once you've approved the sample and we've received that deposit.

  3. 03

    Bulk production

    Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Your order size and the season set the exact timing.

  4. 04

    Reference-led production

    Cutting, stitching, assembly and finishing follow the approved sample under controlled production standards. We check fit, construction and finish the whole way through, and sizing stays consistent across repeat orders.

  5. 05

    Balance payment

    You pay the remaining 50% before shipment, so your confirmed order is fully paid before the goods leave.

  6. 06

    Shipment terms

    We ship FOB, CIF or DDP. With worldwide DDP you get one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle duties, customs and freight.

How We Check Quality at Every Stage

  • Checks throughout production

    Our structured QC system covers incoming fabric, cutting and sewing. We also run random checks and finish with a full final inspection.

  • Every piece inspected

    Before packing, we check every bulk piece for measurements, size tolerance, symmetry, seam strength and comfort, stretch recovery, colour consistency, print clarity, and wires and straps.

  • Agreed inspection criteria

    We inspect bulk against your approved sample and the tolerances we've agreed with you in writing. Before shipment, we send you the final inspection report so you can decide whether to release the goods.

  • Consistent bulk finish

    Every piece gets export-standard stitching and finishing, whatever the size of your order. We check that colour stays uniform across the whole run and matches across fabric, lining and trims.

  • Materials and results

    We use quality yarn and fabric, aim for zero defects on every order and keep our qualification rate above 99%.

  • Experienced inspection team

    Our dedicated QC team has more than 10 years of inspection experience and oversees every run. If you ask, they'll provide remote quality checks, video inspections or goods presentations.

Certifications and Compliance Paperwork

We ask for your target market when we quote. That way we pick fabrics, trims, labels and tests that meet its rules from the start.

  • Certifications held

    Our production holds OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance. Our factory is also BSCI certified, and we meet GB 18401 and REACH requirements.

  • Production audit requirements

    Our production is audited against CPSIA, CCPSA and SASO requirements. Every stage, from fabric to finished garment, follows international quality-system certification standards.

  • Market compliance paperwork

    We give you complete paperwork and test reports to support your market's regulations. Our certifications back shipments to Australia, the EU, the US, the Middle East and other regions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who runs the company, and which managers will we be dealing with directly?

We run the company ourselves, and production and export sit under one management. You work with a dedicated swimwear specialist and a merchandiser. We own our single swimwear factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, and it has been running since 2011. We assign your specialist when you enquire. Your merchandiser tracks sampling and bulk production and sends you photo updates at each stage. You deal directly with our design and production teams, and there are no agents in between. Every quotation and pro-forma invoice shows our registered company name and full factory address. If the company name on the pro-forma invoice matches the bank beneficiary, you know you're dealing with us directly.

Do you have your own designers and pattern makers in-house to develop our styles?

Yes. Our in-house designers and pattern makers handle pattern design, grading and fit development for your styles. Because this work stays within our team, you avoid rounds of back-and-forth on the design.

Can we visit your factory, or get a video tour, before we place an order?

Yes. You're welcome to visit our production floor or book a live video tour before you place an order. You can also visit or arrange a live video audit during sampling or bulk production. If you can't come in person, we show you around the factory on a live video call as an online audit.
