Bali Swimwear Manufacturer

We Build Swimwear Brands

Swimwear Bali is a specialist custom swimwear manufacturer in Bali, making private label bikinis and one-piece swimsuits from your designs, our Ready Designs or both. We started in 1993 with just a few labels and now produce for 300+ independent labels a year. Bydee and Leni Swims both launched with us and reached international retailers in their first year. Your product development coordinator is one call, email or WhatsApp away.

Swimwear Bali focuses exclusively on swimwear.

Fit comes first

Digital Patterns for a Perfect Fit

From seamless bikinis to reversible one-piece swimsuits to underwire tops, every custom pattern is made in-house in 2D AutoCAD, perfecting the body-sculpting fit and grading every size accurately. Starting from proven shapes in our pattern library, we aim to match your handover in the first sample round, so we never rush a sample.
Your own designs usually cost close to our Ready Design prices, and we see custom collections gain traction quickly and come back for larger orders.

If something goes wrong

Your Collection, Kept On Track

If anything goes wrong, your product development coordinator tells you straight away and agrees the fix with you. We simply adjust your pattern in our 2D AutoCAD software, saving you days of waiting and the cost of a remake. If a sample differs noticeably from your approved handover, we correct the pattern and remake it at no cost, and your designs are never made for another label. You’re never left on your own, because we care about your brand as much as you do.

Founder questions

Swimwear Manufacturing FAQ

How many styles should my first swimwear collection have?

Most swimwear labels launch with 8 to 12 styles across tops, bottoms and one-piece swimsuits, plus one or two extra to sample.

Start your swimwear brand →

How much does it cost to sample swimwear?

Custom swimwear samples cost double the wholesale price plus a USD 30 development fee per style, credited against your bulk deposit. Ready Design samples have no fee.

Swimwear sampling →

What is the difference between private label and white label swimwear?

Private label swimwear is made from your own designs, so nobody else sells your shapes. White label, like our Ready Designs, starts from existing styles and adds your logo.

How much can I sell my swimwear for?

Swimwear labels typically sell at 4.6 to 7 times what they pay us: around US$70 to US$109 for a bikini top or bottom in Carvico Vita, and US$89 to US$150 for a one-piece swimsuit.

How much does it cost to ship swimwear from Bali?

Swimwear Bali ships worldwide by DHL Express in 3 to 5 days. Shipping costs vary by destination: bulk orders cost approx. USD 1.30 to 2 a piece, and up to 10 samples cost up to approx. USD 100. Contact us for a price to your location.
