Watersports Apparel Made in Our Own Guangzhou Factory

We let you order from 50 pieces per style in each colour.

We set that starting quantity for small and growing labels. We make the swimwear ourselves in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. We agree the quantities for your first trial run case by case, within our production limits.

A standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days. Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days after you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, depending on order size and season. We also make rash guards for surfing, swimming, watersports and swim camps.

Custom Watersports Apparel: Sample Fees, Payment and Shipping

Each sample costs USD 50, and the design fee is USD 50 per design, covering its pattern or print in all colourways; neither fee is refunded or credited against bulk.

You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the other 50% before shipment. We ship FOB, CIF or DDP, where one all-in price covers delivery to your door.

Our factory-owning production and export company has run since 2011. Development takes your design, picture or idea through in-house patterns, grading and fit.

Our swimwear fabric sourcing covers 80/20 nylon-spandex, with LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL and REPREVE on request, and our production certifications include OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS and bluesign. Custom Printing sublimates polyester and pigment-prints nylon, and Swimwear Hardware tests clasps and rings for chlorine, salt spray and UV.

We arrange market lab tests at cost, welcome independent inspection and reply to every enquiry within one business day.

We settle the quotation and price agreement before we make any samples. With our Launch service, we work from your destination market and launch date. We then confirm the testing, documents, sampling and production schedule for the design and materials you've chosen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which fabrics do you offer that have UPF 50+ sun protection and resist chlorine and salt water?

We offer UPF 50+ fabrics. On request, we also offer chlorine-resistant fabrics such as LYCRA Xtra Life. We test fabrics for chlorine resistance and for colour fastness after repeated chlorine exposure. Our stretch and recovery testing checks that the suit returns to shape after wear and time in the water. We also test strength, softness and durability.

Can you grade our styles across an extended size range, including plus sizes and kids' sizes?

Yes. We make styles for women, men and children, and we grade from XS to 3XL against US, EU or UK size charts. We also produce fully customisable plus-size collections with curvy fits and larger cup sizes. We don't charge for grading any size, and we refine the fit as we build each size. You can send us your own size chart, measurement sheet or reference garment, and we cut to it. Once the chart is based on your fit model, we check every production piece against it and set grading rules separately for each style. If you don't have a chart yet, our pattern makers prepare one for your target market. We cover the EU, UK, US, Australia and the Middle East. We also prepare team size charts for club and school programmes. For a new fit, approve the base-size sample on your own fit model in the exact production fabric. That gives us the correct starting point before we fix the grading rules. Swim fabrics differ in stretch and recovery. If you change the fabric, the same pattern fits tighter or looser, and grading from a wrong base carries that error into every size. This approval adds a sampling round and lead time. For repeat styles in a fabric we've already fitted, it matters less.

Which ways can you put our logo and artwork on the garments: sublimation, screen print, heat transfer or embroidery?

We apply logos and artwork with sublimation, screen printing, heat transfer or embroidery. We also offer silicone printing and silicone patches. Your branding also goes on sewn-in labels or woven waistbands, and we provide product-specific branding. For club programmes, we recommend sublimation. We screen print labels and care instructions using a chlorine-resistant method. We also handle labelling and packaging. That includes branded hang tags, poly bags, retail packaging and printed cartons. For kids' swimwear, we provide branded size labels and packaging.
