Watersports Apparel Made in Our Own Guangzhou Factory

We let you order from 50 pieces per style in each colour.

We set that starting quantity for small and growing labels. We make your watersports apparel ourselves in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. We agree the quantities for your first trial run case by case, within our production limits.

A standard sample is ready in 10 to 15 days. Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days after you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, depending on order size and season. We also make rash guards for surfing, swimming, watersports and swim camps.

Watersports Apparel We Make for Surf, Swim and Team Labels

Our watersports range covers the garments a surf, swim or team label needs, and each main garment has its own page with the construction details.

Rash guards

Long- and short-sleeve rash guards for surfing, swimming, watersports and swim camps, in compression or relaxed cuts, plus custom team styles for swim schools and clubs.

Surf and resort boardshorts

Boardshorts in lightweight 4-way stretch with seams built to reduce chafing for surf and performance labels, and tailored quick-dry looks for resort and lifestyle ranges.

Swim trunks

Swim trunks in crisp, tightly woven quick-dry polyester or in 4-way stretch fabric with added spandex, for swimmers who paddle or swim hard.

Full-coverage water styles

Long-sleeve rash guards and swim leggings for surfing and other water sports, made alongside our modest swimwear sets, swim hijabs and caps.

Training suits

Women's performance athletic suits for training, swim teams and club use. They don't carry World Aquatics approval as standard, and we supply samples and fabric data for your own approval application.

Thermal layers

Fleece-lined thermal rash guards in brushed polyester-spandex for cool-water swimming and surfing, alongside our lighter shirts for warm water.

Watersports Fabrics Matched to Pool, Sea and Surf

We choose each fabric by where the garment will be worn, rather than one blend for every sport.

Pool and training

For pool use we put chlorine resistance and colourfastness first. Chlorine-resistant fabrics such as LYCRA Xtra Life are available on request for styles worn in chlorinated water.

Open water and sea

For open water we prioritise UPF and how the fabric behaves when soaked. Our surf and watersports styles use UPF 50+ fabric, and our prints hold up in salt water.

Surf and board contact

Nylon blends resist rubbing from the board best, while polyester blends hold their colour better in sun and salt water. Choose by whether wear or colour matters more for your range.

Shorts shells and liners

Boardshort shells come in lightweight 4-way stretch with DWR coatings. Compression liners or unlined shells suit surf ranges, and mesh liners suit resort ranges worn as everyday swim shorts.

Prints for the water

Sublimation dyes bond into polyester fibres, so prints resist chlorine and salt water and don't crack or peel. Nylon pieces take our own pigment prints.

How Each Watersport Shapes the Garment

Each sport puts different demands on fit, coverage and construction, so we set them for the activity your customers buy for.

  • Surf sessions

    A relaxed cut, grip hems or board-short loops that keep the shirt down, and boardshorts with compression liners or none to cut chafing on long sessions.

  • Paddling and hard swimming

    Trunks in 4-way stretch fabric with added spandex, so they move with you when you paddle or swim hard.

  • Pool training and swim teams

    Compression cuts for training and performance suits for swim teams and clubs, in fabric chosen for repeated time in chlorinated water.

  • Open-water and sea swimming

    UPF fabric that keeps its shape when soaked, with hooded or high-neck styles for extra sun cover on long days outside.

  • Swim lessons and coaching

    High necks and thumb loops give swim-school coaches full neck and arm coverage, and back-zip styles let kids get in and out easily.

  • Resort and beach days

    Tailored casual looks in quick-dry technical fabrics, and mesh-lined shorts that wear as everyday swim shorts.

Developing a Watersports Style, From Swatch to Wet Fit

Share a reference garment, a fabric swatch, a sketch or a tech pack, and we recommend the knit, the construction and the production timeline your style calls for.

Before sampling begins, we review the design for construction, fabric needs, finishing details and manufacturability, and confirm each detail taken from your tech pack or reference piece. Garments for the water are judged wet, not only flat and dry.

We fit each style on a base-size fit model against your measurement sheet and wear-test it wet to check stretch, coverage and opacity before grading the full size range.

The DWR finish we offer on boardshorts and trunks is PFAS-free, and we supply the finish supplier's declaration on request.

Custom Watersports Apparel: Sample Fees, Payment and Shipping

Each sample costs USD 50, and the design fee is USD 50 per design, covering its pattern or print in all colourways; neither fee is refunded or credited against bulk.

You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the other 50% before shipment. We ship FOB, CIF or all-in DDP, delivered to your door by our forwarding partners.

Our factory-owning production and export company has run since 2011. Development takes your design, picture or idea through in-house patterns, grading and fit.

Our swimwear fabric sourcing covers 80/20 nylon-spandex, with LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL and REPREVE on request, and our production certifications include OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS and bluesign. Custom Printing sublimates polyester and pigment-prints nylon, and Swimwear Hardware lab-tests clasps and rings for chlorine, salt spray and UV.

We arrange market lab tests at cost, welcome independent inspection and reply to every enquiry within one business day.

We settle the quotation and price agreement before we make any samples. With our Launch service, we work from your destination market and launch date. We then confirm the testing, documents, sampling and production schedule for the design and materials you've chosen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which fabrics do you offer that have UPF 50+ sun protection and resist chlorine and salt water?

We offer UPF 50+ fabrics. On request, we also offer chlorine-resistant fabrics such as LYCRA Xtra Life. Our partner lab tests fabrics for chlorine resistance and colour fastness after repeated chlorine exposure. Our stretch and recovery testing checks that the suit returns to shape after wear and time in the water. We also test strength, softness and durability.

Can you grade our styles across an extended size range, including plus sizes and kids' sizes?

Yes. We make styles for women, men and children, grading women's XS–3XL and men's S–3XL against US, EU or UK charts. We also produce fully customisable plus-size collections with curvy fits and larger cup sizes. Grading within these ranges is free, and we refine the fit as we build each size. You can send us your own size chart, measurement sheet or reference garment, and we cut to it. Once the chart is based on your fit model, we check every production piece against it and set grading rules separately for each style. If you don't have a chart yet, our pattern makers prepare one for your target market. We cover the EU, UK, US, Australia and the Middle East. We also prepare team size charts for club and school programmes. For a new fit, approve the base-size sample on your own fit model in the exact production fabric. That gives us the correct starting point before we fix the grading rules. Swim fabrics differ in stretch and recovery. If you change the fabric, the same pattern fits tighter or looser, and grading from a wrong base carries that error into every size. This approval adds a sampling round and lead time. For repeat styles in a fabric we've already fitted, it matters less.

Which ways can you put our logo and artwork on the garments: sublimation, screen print, heat transfer or embroidery?

We apply logos and artwork with sublimation, screen printing, heat transfer or embroidery. We also offer silicone printing and silicone patches. Your branding also goes on sewn-in labels or woven waistbands, and we provide product-specific branding. For club programmes, we recommend sublimation. Labels and care notes get a partner's chlorine-resistant screen print. We also handle labelling and packaging. That includes branded hang tags, poly bags, retail packaging and printed cartons. For kids' swimwear, we provide branded size labels and packaging.
