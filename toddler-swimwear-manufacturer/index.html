    Chlorine- and Salt-Resistant Fabrics for Little Swimmers

    Fabric considerationWhat it means for your collection
    Water and washingOur chlorine- and salt-resistant fabrics hold up to active play and repeated washing without fading, and they're gentle on sensitive skin.
    Sun protectionYou can choose UPF 50+ fabric to block UV rays while toddlers play outdoors.
    Fabric selection and complianceThrough our Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing service, we offer matching linings and blends such as 80% nylon 20% spandex. Softer nylon-spandex suits the beach and occasional pool wear, because frequent chlorine exposure reduces how well it keeps its colour and shape. Firmer polyester-spandex, such as our 84/16 blend, keeps its pool fit longer. PBT has the highest chlorine resistance, so it suits weekly lessons. Before sampling, we confirm OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Product Class I coverage for toddler fabrics and trims that meet EU REACH and US CPSIA requirements. The certificates come with your order documents.

    Soft, Secure Construction for Every Splash

    Baby fit construction

    Our baby swimwear construction pairs gentle elastic and covered seams with 180 to 200 gsm fabric, at least 18% spandex and a full lining. A 110 to 130 gsm seat lining keeps the seat from sagging when it gets wet.

    Skin-contact finishes

    We line the front and seat, finish skin-contact seams flat or covered, and enclose the waist and leg elastic. These finishes deal with friction as toddlers crawl, sit and move around in wet suits.

    Lining trade-off

    Full linings and covered seams add fabric, sewing time and cost, and they make suits a little slower to dry.

    Children’s construction options

    Our children’s swimwear comes with guarded zips, 3-snap crotches and a choice of liners. We carry out 90 N attachment testing and EN 1811 nickel testing, and we put no removable decorations on styles for under-3s.

    Lead stays within CPSIA limits of 100 ppm for metal and 90 ppm for coatings.

    Styles for Girls and Boys

    Girls’ core shapes

    You can pick from crisscross-strap one-pieces, tank one-pieces that stay in place without tugging, swim dresses with attached shorts, and tankini tops and bottoms you can mix and match.

    Boys’ swim trunks

    We make boys’ trunks with an elastic waist and a drawstring, in solid colours or in prints for your collection.

    Repeat approved shapes

    Reordering or recolouring a core shape that has already passed its fit and safety checks is quicker. Every new shape needs its own pattern, sample and approval.

    Seasonal variety

    A narrow range of shapes looks repetitive. If your brand sells on newness, add one or two fashion shapes each season so your customers get familiar fits alongside fresh silhouettes.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do the drawstrings on your toddler trunks and the ties on girls’ styles meet children’s drawstring safety rules for the US and EU?

    Yes. Our toddler trunks and girls’ tie styles follow EN 14682 and ASTM F1816 for the US and EU. There are no neck drawstrings, and waist drawcords are bar-tacked at the centre with no toggles or knots. Every toddler size falls under the under-7s rules, so drawcords and ties don't hang outside the garment.

    Can you manufacture toddler swimsuits with an integrated reusable swim-diaper layer designed for solid-waste containment, rather than ordinary swimsuit lining?

    Yes. We build in a reusable swim-diaper layer that holds solids and lets water drain out. It's a snug inner brief with elasticated waist and leg cuffs, not ordinary lining. You can also choose a unisex design with adjustable snaps that fits securely across several sizes.

    Can you develop separate fits for toddlers wearing swim diapers and toilet-trained toddlers, rather than simply increasing the overall garment size?

    Yes, we make separate patterns. The swim-diaper fit adds 2 to 3 cm to the standard seat and rise and widens the leg openings. The toilet-trained fit keeps the standard seat, rise and leg openings. We sample both for your approval before bulk production. If you just add a diaper layer to a standard pattern, the suit leaks or pulls, because the extra room and cuffs change the fit.
