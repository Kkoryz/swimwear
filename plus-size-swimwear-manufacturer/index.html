Bust, Tummy and Waistband Support That Stays in Place

We match each support feature to its job, solving digging straps, rolling waistbands and riding leg openings at the pattern stage, without stacking every feature onto one suit.

  • Straps and bands

    For plus-size and extended-cup ranges, we reposition and widen adjustable straps, widen back bands, reinforce the underbust elastic and build in bust support.

  • Cup options

    Structured underwire tops run from D through DD+ up to a G cup. Moulded cups come in A to DD.

  • Cup-sized construction

    If you ask, we add underwire channels and grade to your size chart. Cup-sized underwire gives the best support, but every band-and-cup combination counts as its own size.

  • Removable padding

    Our bikinis and one-piece sets come with removable padding, so the wearer can adjust coverage and shaping herself.

  • Firm tummy control

    Where your design needs firm control, a separate power mesh front panel anchored into the side seams provides it.

  • Light front smoothing

    A double front lining gives light smoothing and opacity without the compression of a shaping construction.

  • Strongest shaping

    We cut the front body or the whole suit in compression swim fabric, and add an extra power mesh panel if you specify one.

  • Secure waistbands

    Our waistbands are 3 to 4 cm wide, with fully enclosed rubber elastic stitched through every layer. Where it's needed, we add a high-waisted cut or a side-seam bone.

Heavier Fabrics, Full Front Linings and Power Mesh

Heavier base fabric

For larger sizes we specify about 200 to 220 gsm, in 80% nylon 20% spandex or a higher-spandex blend. It holds its shape better and stays more opaque under larger-size stretch than our standard 180–200 GSM fabric.

Lining and trade-offs

Full front linings are 140 gsm nylon-spandex, and we add power mesh only where you need extra hold. Heavier fabric and full linings cost more and dry more slowly, so they work against very lightweight or resort briefs.

Specialist fabric sourcing

Our fabric sourcing covers nylon and polyester blended with spandex. On request, you can also have chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL recycled nylon, REPREVE recycled polyester and Italian Carvico.

Hardware performance

Our Swimwear Hardware service supplies elastics and straps from 3 mm to 25 mm wide. They recover 95% or more of their stretch after 100 cycles and withstand more than 200 hours of chlorine exposure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you take our current straight-size styles and extend them into plus sizes with adjusted fit and support?

Yes. We extend your styles up to 5XL by redrafting each one on a 2XL base, regrading it and adjusting the support. That means we reposition straps, widen back bands, reinforce the underbust elastic and add built-in bust support. We sample each extended style in 10 to 15 days, and you approve the fit before bulk production starts.

What makes a plus-size style cost more than a standard-size style, such as extra fabric, underwires, shaping linings or extra sizes?

Larger sizes use more fabric. Underwires, moulded or cup-sized construction, power mesh and shaping linings add materials and sewing time. Dedicated base patterns, fit sessions and extra graded sizes add development time and cost. With band-and-cup sizing, every combination counts as its own size.

How do you verify that bust support and coverage remain secure when a plus-size suit is wet and the wearer swims or bends?

We check the sample against your measurement sheet on a real 2XL fit model, then wet-test it before grading. She swims, bends and lifts her arms for at least 30 minutes while we check support, stretch, coverage and opacity. Finished-measurement tolerances are -0.5 to 0.5 cm for straps and bindings and -1 to 1 cm for body measurements.

Do you build plus-size patterns from a dedicated plus-size base size, or simply grade up a regular-size pattern?

We start from a dedicated 2XL base pattern fitted on a 2XL model and grade up to 5XL from there. We don't just enlarge a straight-size pattern. Bust, waist and hip grading steps are 5 cm per size, against 4 cm in straight sizes. Extra length in the rise, straps and back band keeps the coverage as body proportions change.

From plus base pattern to the full size range

  1. Draft 2XL base pattern

    The style is drafted on its own 2XL plus base pattern, not graded up from straight sizes.

  2. Fit on 2XL model

    The sample is fitted on a real 2XL fit model and checked against your measurement sheet.

  3. Wet wear test

    The model wears it in the water for at least 30 minutes, swimming, bending and lifting her arms, to confirm support, stretch, coverage and opacity.

  4. Grade up to 5XL

    The approved base is graded up through the range to 5XL.
