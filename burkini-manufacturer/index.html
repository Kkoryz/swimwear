Burkini Sets and Matching Pieces Made in China

We make burkinis as two-piece or three-piece sets: a tunic and full-length leggings, paired with either a hood or a separate hijab.

We make all the swimwear ourselves in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. To go with your sets, our matching cover-ups include quick-dry swim skirts, swim dresses, long beach kaftans and robes in the same prints and colours. Full-length, long-sleeved options give the same coverage underneath.

Fabrics That Stay Light, Opaque and Sun-Protective in Water

  • Nylon-spandex drape

    Our nylon-spandex drapes softly and sits closer to the body when wet, but it holds more water and dries more slowly. On premium lines, you can also choose a matte option with a softer hand.

  • Polyester-spandex weight

    Our polyester-spandex holds less water, so it feels lighter after a swim and dries faster. That makes it the right choice for full-length pieces worn all day.

  • Full-length fabric comparison

    You compare the two fabrics in full-length samples worn wet. Soaked tunics and leggings show differences in weight, cling and drying time that small dry swatches hide.

  • Fabric sourcing service

    Through Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing, we offer 80% nylon 20% spandex blends, matching linings and UPF 50+ options, and on request LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL, REPREVE and Carvico fabrics. We test them for chlorine resistance, colourfastness, stretch, recovery, strength, softness and durability.

  • Sampling trade-off

    Comparing both fabrics adds a development round and extra sample cost. For short sport cuts, the performance gap is smaller, so you get less out of that extra work.

  • Wet opacity checks

    We keep the garment opaque with a lining or a double layer, then check the finished piece both wet and under stretch.

  • Recycled polyamide option

    If you choose recycled polyamide, we offer fabric spun from reclaimed fishing nets and supply its certification with the fabric.

Burkini Construction Tested in Motion

  • Fastenings in motion

    We keep only the fastenings your design needs. We confirm where each zip, tie and drawcord sits while the wearer moves the way she would in the water.

  • Movement over standing

    A standing fitting doesn't show whether fastenings stay comfortable during strokes or bending. Movement shows up rubbing, snagging and fastenings that shift out of reach.

  • Fitting time trade-off

    Fitting in motion takes longer. When it shows a pattern problem, another revision comes before approval.

  • Seam placement

    We run seams along muscle lines and keep them away from friction points, which cuts down contact in those areas.

  • Bust support

    We build in bust support with a compression shelf, and you can specify removable cups as an extra option.

  • Protected, reachable closures

    Every zip is chlorine-rated and has a full internal guard behind it. We place drawcords and internal ties where the swimmer can reach them.

  • Coverage and matching pieces

    Our modest swimwear options include three coverage levels, three hijab constructions (one is a two-piece cap and drape) and ready-made two-piece or three-piece designs with your branding.

Lead Times and Seasonal Planning

  1. 01

    Start the autumn before

    For a summer season, development starts the autumn before, because chlorine-resistant knits in specific colours get booked up early.

  2. 02

    Book approved colours

    We confirm fabric and colours, then book the knit as soon as the colours are approved. Fabric procurement sets the schedule for chlorine-resistant swimwear, and committing to colours early leaves less room for late trend changes.

  3. 03

    Finish fit details

    We finish the fit details while the fabric is on order. Through our Launch service, we check testing, documents, sampling and production schedules against your destination market and launch date.

  4. 04

    Confirm order arrangements

    Under our order terms, courier delivery of a sample costs you USD 50 on top of the sample fee. We confirm your exact lead time with the order, and independent SGS or BV inspectors are welcome.

  5. 05

    Run bulk production

    Once you approve the sample and we receive the deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Order size and season decide the exact time.

  6. 06

    Inspect before shipment

    Our quality checks cover incoming fabric, cutting and sewing. After that come random checks and a full final inspection before shipment.

OEM Burkini Design, Sampling and Water Testing

  1. Develop your starting point

    Our Development service turns your design, picture or idea into OEM or ODM patterns and fit. We grade XS to 3XL free of charge against US, EU or UK size charts.

  2. Set the sample timing

    A standard sample takes us 10 to 15 days, so you have a physical garment to assess before bulk production.

  3. Confirm development fees

    Each sample costs USD 50. The design fee is USD 50 per design, which covers its pattern or print in all colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

  4. Swim-test the prototype

    We swim in every prototype to check drag, shifting, cling and wet opacity. Once it's dry, we inspect it again to find any construction or fabric problems.

  5. Test with your customers

    After our swim tests, you wear-test the revised prototype on your customers' body types and swimming movements, because fit and comfort depend on shape and use.

  6. Approve after feedback

    We recommend approving the pre-production sample after your customers have wear-tested it.

Price Factors, Minimums and Order Terms

Your price comes down to set configuration, bust support and fabric. Once those are settled, we confirm your price.

  • Set configuration

    A three-piece set with a separate hijab costs more than a hooded two-piece. It uses more fabric and adds cutting, sewing and packing.

  • Support and pricing

    Cups or a compression shelf raise the price, and a simpler two-piece without cups brings it down. We confirm bulk pricing after we've reviewed your design and fabric.

  • Order minimum

    Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. We set the minimum there so small and growing labels can order.

  • First trial run

    We agree the quantity for your first trial run case by case. How flexible we can be is limited by what production allows.

  • Payment terms

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the other 50% before we ship the goods.

  • Shipping terms

    We ship FOB, CIF or DDP. With worldwide DDP you get one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle freight, customs and duties.

  • Quote before sampling

    Our quote process prices your order first. Once you agree the price, we start making your sample.

  • Contact and response

    Contact our team by email, WhatsApp or the website form. We reply to every enquiry within one business day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you manufacture burkinis using fabric we supply, and what stretch, recovery and wet-opacity requirements must it meet?

Yes. We manufacture burkinis from your fabric if it has at least 50% stretch in both directions, at least 90% recovery and full opacity when wet and stretched. If it isn't opaque enough, it needs a lining, and lining changes the weight, feel and drying time. We accept fabric by testing a sample first. That adds testing time, and it settles whether you need lining before you supply the full minimum in each colour plus a cutting allowance.

Does a complete burkini set count as one piece towards the 50-piece MOQ, and can I order the hijab or leggings separately?

Yes. A complete set of tunic, leggings and hood or hijab counts as one piece towards our minimum of 50 pieces per style in each colour. You can also order hijabs or leggings separately, and each separate piece has its own minimum of 50 pieces per colour.

Apart from tunic-and-leggings sets, can you also make one-piece full-body burkinis, sporty athletic burkinis, and swim dresses or skirts?

Yes, we make all of these styles. Our one-piece full-body burkinis are close, streamlined suits that cover both the top and the legs. Sport burkinis pair a shorter fitted tunic with athletic leggings, and swim dresses and swim skirts are worn over leggings.

How we handle fabric you supply for burkinis.

  1. Send a fabric sample

    We ask for a sample of your fabric first.

  2. Test stretch and opacity

    We test stretch, recovery and wet opacity before accepting the fabric; this testing adds time.

  3. Add lining if needed

    If the fabric isn't opaque enough, we add a lining, which changes weight, feel and drying time.

  4. Send the full quantity

    Enough fabric for the full minimum in each colour plus a cutting allowance, so no second dye lot is needed.
