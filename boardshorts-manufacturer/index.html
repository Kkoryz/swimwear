Custom Boardshorts for Surf, Resort and Merchandise Labels

You decide the fabric, waistband, drawcord, pockets, logo and fit of your solid boardshorts.

We make boardshorts for men and boys, knitted swim shorts for men, knitted swim trunks for boys, and swim trunks for women. Surf and performance labels get lightweight stretch builds with chafe-reducing seams. Resort and lifestyle labels get tailored casual looks in quick-dry technical fabrics.

We also make branded boardshorts for corporate and event merchandise.

Our Boardshorts Factory in China and the Styles We Make

We make our swimwear in our own factory, a single site in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Woven boardshorts

These use woven shells such as polyester microfibre twill or a polyester/spandex stretch weave, with a fixed waistband, faux fly and drawcord. Mesh lining is optional.

Knitted swim styles

Our knitted swim shorts and trunks are made from nylon or polyester spandex fabric, with a full elastic waist, an internal drawcord and standard mesh lining.

Adult lengths

Men's outseams come in 17, 19 and 21 inches, with inseams of about 5, 7 and 9 inches. Women's come in 12 and 15 inches, with inseams of about 3 and 5 inches.

Kids and custom lengths

Kids' outseams are 13 and 15 inches. We also cut custom outseams from 10 to 22 inches to your brand's measurement sheet.

Waist sizing

Men's waists run from 28 to 44 inches in 2-inch steps. That covers S to 3XL, plus the extension up to 5XL.

Sample before grading

We choose woven or knit construction based on end use. Outseam and waist sizing are then approved on one sample before we grade the range.

Your size chart

Send us your own waist-size chart or a reference garment and we grade to it free of charge from S to 3XL.

Factory ownership

Our factory-owning production and export company has been running since 2011. We control quality, cost and delivery directly, with inspections at incoming fabric, cutting and sewing, plus random and full final inspections.

Wholesale Boardshorts: MOQ, Samples, Lead Times and Payment

  • Order minimum

    Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. First-trial quantities are agreed case by case within production limits.

  • Sample timing

    A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days and costs USD 50. Design costs USD 50 per pattern or print, covering all colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited.

  • Bulk and payment

    Once the sample is approved and you've paid a 50% deposit, production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on size and season. The remaining 50% is due before shipment.

  • Shipping terms

    We ship FOB, CIF or DDP worldwide. With DDP you get an all-in door-delivery price, and we handle duties, customs and freight through our forwarding partners.

  • Courier and inspection

    Under our sample delivery and inspection terms, you pay USD 50 for courier delivery on top of the sample fee. Independent pre-shipment inspectors such as SGS or BV are welcome.

  • Free quote inputs

    For a free quotation, email or WhatsApp us your tech pack or coded front and back drawings, along with your materials, designs, prints, colours, quantities, sizing and packing needs.

  • Quote confirmation

    We check feasibility, send you estimated pricing and timing, and go over the specifications with you by phone. Then we confirm availability, lead times and every detail before you place the order.

  • Enquiry response

    We reply to every enquiry within one business day, so you know exactly when you'll hear back.

OEM and ODM Boardshorts Customisation

If you're working to a target price, we spend your budget on one or two distinguishing features and keep the rest of the build standard.

OEM and ODM choices

We take on your own swim-short designs and customise fabrics, prints, colours, logos, fit, embroidery, functional features and finishing details.

Higher-cost features

Branded or recycled fabrics push the unit price up the most. Next come exclusive all-over prints, compression liners, extra zip or cargo pockets, and embroidered or silicone logos.

Lowest-cost build

Unit prices stay lowest with standard polyester or polyester-spandex shells, existing prints, unlined or mesh-lined construction, simple drawcord waists, woven labels and larger quantities per style and colour.

In-house development

Our design and development team handles patterns, grading and fit in-house, so your collection goes through fewer rounds of design back-and-forth.

New-label support

Our Launch service offers a starter pack with three finished samples, twenty fabric swatches, a stocked-style catalogue and a launch guide. We also offer separate design-to-manufacturing startup packages and consulting.

Boardshort Shell Fabrics and Liner Options

We pick the shell and liner together, so the fabric's stretch and weight suit how the liner will be used.

  • Standard shell specifications

    For your specification sheet, our 100% polyester microfibre peach twill weighs about 120 GSM, and our 95% polyester / 5% spandex 4-way stretch weighs about 150 GSM.

  • Other performance shells

    Quick-dry nylon weighs about 110 GSM and is lightweight, durable and water-resistant. You can also choose 4-way stretch polyester, quick-dry and UV-resistant fabrics, with DWR specified to AATCC 22 spray rating 80.

  • Recycled polyester options

    If your collection is positioned as eco-friendly, we offer recycled polyester shells for your boardshorts, including recycled microfibre as a performance fabric.

  • UPF test conditions

    Our UPF 50+ fabrics are lab-tested dry and relaxed to AS/NZS 4399, and we supply the documentation. If you want wet and stretched testing, an accredited lab does it at your cost.

  • Liner by use

    For long surf and performance sessions, compression liners and unlined shells reduce chafing and bulk. For resort and lifestyle styles worn as everyday swim shorts, mesh liners suit best.

  • Fabric sourcing

    Our Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing service supplies blends such as 80% nylon 20% spandex with matching linings. On request, it sources chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL, REPREVE and Italian Carvico.

  • Production certifications

    Our production credentials cover OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Waist, Seam, Hem and Pocket Construction

For surf use, we pair secure fixed or hybrid waists with closed pockets. For lifestyle wear, we use easier elastic waists.

  • Waist constructions

    You can choose a flat-front lace-up fly secured with hook-and-loop or snaps, a hybrid flat-front waist with an elastic back, or a full elastic waist with an internal drawcord.

  • Stitching alignment

    We reinforce the stitching and keep stitch lines precisely aligned across the whole pair of shorts.

  • Main pocket choices

    Pick a back flap pocket, a concealed back pocket with an elastic closure, side-seam pockets, or a back pocket closed with a zip or hook-and-loop.

  • Additional pocket details

    We add drain eyelets, inner key pockets, leg cargo pockets and key loops inside the waistband to your specification.

  • Matching hardware

    Our Swimwear Hardware service supplies drawstrings, eyelets and zippers, along with colour-matched straps, rings, sliders, hooks and buckles in metal, plastic, resin or eco-friendly materials.

Sublimation Prints and Colour Matching

Every colour and print is customised to your design, and we take your artwork through colour approval, printing and cutting. With our Custom Printing service, all of this stays inside the factory, right alongside the rest of your boardshort manufacturing.

How your print goes from artwork to the cutting room

  1. Print-ready artwork files

    Artwork is supplied as print-ready files scaled to the actual pattern pieces.

  2. Colour strike-off

    Colours are struck off on the production base fabric before bulk printing.

  3. In-house sublimation printing

    Designs, including full-coverage all-over prints, are printed on our own thermal sublimation printer on polyester and sun-protection fabrics.

  4. To the cutting room

    The printed fabric goes on to cutting, so printing and the rest of manufacturing stay under one roof.

Private Label Boardshorts: Logos and Labels

Logo method and placement

Woven labels go inside the back. Embroidery or raised, waterproof 3D silicone badges go on the waistband or hem, where your branding shows.

Embroidery service minimums

Our Embroidery service offers durable machine or hand embroidery, including standard, 3D, reflective and stone options. Embroidery and screen printing need 50 pieces per design, colours come from our thread library, and custom woven labels need 1,000 pieces.

Labels and packaging

Our Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels service takes care of product branding, chlorine-resistant screen-printed care labels, hang tags, poly bags, retail packaging and printed cartons, including branded size labels and packaging for kids.

Preventing Common Boardshort Defects

We build defect prevention into sampling, so you test drawcords, eyelets, seams, print fastness and closures before bulk production starts.

Every drawcord is bar-tacked at the centre so it can't pull out. Eyelets are rust-proof stainless steel or plastic, so they don't corrode. Inner seams are triple-stitched and flat-finished to reduce chafing.

To stop sublimated colours bleeding or fading, we fix the inks at around 200°C and check every print colour for wash fastness on the sample. Closures are soft, low-profile hook-and-loop, with a fabric-flap option, so they don't snag other fabrics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is your DWR finish fluorine-free (PFC/PFAS-free), so the shorts can be sold in markets that restrict PFAS?

Yes. The optional DWR finish we offer for trunk and boardshort shells is PFC-free and PFAS-free. It uses fluorine-free chemistry with no fluorinated repellents, so it works for markets that restrict PFAS. Applied over quick-drying fabric, it makes water bead up and run off rather than soak in. We provide the finish supplier's declaration on request.

How do you adapt waistband drawcords on boys' boardshorts for the age group and destination market's cord-safety requirements?

Our boys' boardshorts follow EN 14682, the European standard for cords on children's clothing. We bar-tack waist drawcords at the centre and leave the ends with no toggles or knots. For under-7s, drawcords don't hang outside the garment. For ages 7 to 14, free ends are 14 cm long at most.

On stretch boardshorts with all-over sublimation, how do you stop white base fabric showing through at the seams and when the fabric stretches?

We extend the artwork at least 1 cm past the seam allowance on every cut panel, so no white shows at the seams after sewing or when they open. For dark or strong backgrounds, we use a base fabric dyed close to the main print colour and stretch-test the printed panels to 20% before sewing, so white doesn't grin through in wear.

Which shell and lining combinations prevent lightweight or pale-coloured boardshorts from becoming see-through when wet?

We specify the shell weight together with a full lining, a double layer or a lined front panel, so lightweight or pale boardshorts stay opaque in and out of the water. We check the finished shorts wet and stretched, not just flat and dry.
