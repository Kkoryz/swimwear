OEM One-Piece Swimsuits and ODM Programs

We build custom one-piece swimsuits for you in two ways. OEM programs are made from your own designs, and ODM programs are developed from our styles. Whether you start from your design or one of ours, your range can include monokinis and cut-out designs.

One-Piece Styles From Our Block Library

For ODM styles, we start from the library block closest to your target market and adapt it.

Classic maillot

You can start from our classic one-piece block, which has a clean square or scoop neckline.

Sculpting monokini

This sculpting silhouette has cut-outs shaped at the side or front and joined with connecting hardware.

One-shoulder silhouette

We develop asymmetric one-shoulder styles for your range, finished with either a single strap or a ruffle detail.

Deep-V plunge

An internal power-mesh layer holds the deep-V plunge silhouette and supports the shape of the suit.

Surf and active styles

For surf and modest-leaning markets, we offer long-sleeve swim tops and zip-front styles. We also offer sport-cut racerbacks with wide straps and more back coverage.

Specialist fit ranges

Our women’s swimwear range includes wholesale tummy-control styles and maternity fronts cut 4 to 6 cm longer. It also includes long-torso fits that add roughly 5 cm when torso girth exceeds our chart by more than 4 cm.

Private Label One-Piece Orders: MOQ, Samples, Lead Times and Payment

Order quantities

Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. For a first trial, we agree the quantity with you case by case, within production limits.

Samples and design fees

A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days and costs USD 50. Design costs USD 50 per pattern or print and covers all colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited against bulk.

Bulk production timing

Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

Payment schedule

You pay a 50% deposit when we confirm your order and the remaining 50% before shipment.

Shipping terms

We ship FOB, CIF or DDP. Worldwide DDP gives you one all-in price delivered to your door, and we handle duties, customs and freight.

Development and fit approval

Our Development service handles patterns and grading in-house. We revise samples based on your feedback, sign off fit against the measurements and check every graded size before production.

Quotation before sampling

Through our quotation process, you get detailed costs from sampling to shipping within 2 to 3 working days of sending us complete front-and-back images, tech packs or clear drawings. Sampling starts once we agree on the price.

Enquiry response time

We reply to every enquiry within one business day, whether you're discussing a trial run or your next production order.

Custom One-Piece Design Options: Neckline, Back, Leg Line and Cut-Outs

You can customise every design, print, colour, cut and detail of your one-piece. We choose the neckline, back and leg line together so the support stays balanced.

  • Neckline and back

    Pick a scoop, square, plunge, halter, one-shoulder or high-neck neckline and pair it with an open, cross-back, racerback, zip-back or low-back design.

  • Leg line choices

    Your one-piece range can use classic, high-cut, French-cut or sport leg lines.

  • Reinforced cut-outs

    Cut-outs go at the side, front or underbust, and we reinforce every stress point with bar tacks.

  • Mixed fabric panels

    When the fabrics have similar stretch, we combine power mesh, rib or crinkle knits and compression fabric up to 220 gsm. This includes mesh insets, or textured fronts with compression backs.

  • Different stretch levels

    When two fabrics differ in stretch by more than 15%, we back the softer panel with body lining, sew the joins with stretch coverstitching and fit-sample the seams before bulk.

  • Custom Printing service

    Our Custom Printing service maps your motifs across the pattern pieces. Tight curves and high-stretch areas shift the alignment, so we check fitted samples and keep key motifs away from tight curves.

Shaping Fabrics, Linings and Performance Options

Base fabric and sourcing

Our default is 80% nylon 20% spandex with a matching lining. Through our fabric sourcing, you can also ask for polyester blends, chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, recycled ECONYL nylon, REPREVE polyester and Italian Carvico fabrics.

Sculpting shell weight

Sculpting and shaping styles get a heavier 200–220 gsm shell plus a second layer of fabric.

Lining weight and coverage

Your lining is a matching nylon-spandex or polyester-spandex of about 120–140 gsm, in the main colour, nude, white or black. Most styles are fully lined. For lighter-weight or lower-price dark styles, you can choose front-only lining.

Pool and training performance

For pool and training ranges, we pick chlorine-resistant spandex. You can also add quick-dry performance and four-way stretch to your suits.

Beach and surf performance

For beach and surf ranges, we pick UPF 50+ UV-blocking yarns and salt-fade proofing to handle sun exposure and saltwater wear.

Construction, Bust Support and Trim Details

We match bust support to your cup sizes and neckline.

Small-bust fashion styles get shelf linings or light padding, and fuller-bust and cup-sized ranges get underwire or foam cups. Your options include removable cups in sewn-in pockets, fixed padding, power-mesh shelf linings, built-in shelf bras and underwire channels. Underwire styles can also take foam cups or power mesh.

We flatlock the side seams and finish the edges with fold-over elastic or turned, coverstitched picot elastic, depending on your price point. High-cut styles get silicone-backed leg elastic so they don't ride up. Our swimwear hardware options include zinc-alloy rings and sliders with gold or silver plating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you manufacture one-pieces using fabric we supply, and what stretch and recovery requirements must that fabric meet?

Yes. We make one-pieces from your fabric if it has at least 15% spandex, at least 60% four-way stretch in each direction and at least 90% recovery.

Which one-piece construction choices push the unit price up, such as underwire cups, rings and sliders, cut-outs, double-layer fabric, zips or long sleeves?

Each of these adds construction steps, trims or fabric, so each one raises your unit price. The more of them you combine, the higher the price goes. Your quote also depends on your fabric and whether it's available, the printing method, where the print goes and how many panels are printed, custom trims, branding, and how many pieces you order per style and colour.

How do you make sure a white or pastel one-piece is not see-through when it gets wet?

We keep white and pastel one-pieces opaque when wet by adding lining, a double layer or a lined front panel, or by using a heavier fabric.

How do you size a one-piece offered in cup sizes, and up to which cup size can you go?

We make cup-sized one-pieces on request. We grade each band-and-cup combination to your chart and count each combination as one size. Our Plus Size Swimwear programme supports up to G cups, and our standard one-piece range runs from XS to 3XL.

How do you prevent laser-cut hollow-out edges from curling or tearing during repeated stretching and wear?

We only laser-cut fabrics with at least 18% spandex, so the heat seals the edges and they don't fray. Inside each opening we bond 1 cm clear elastic or heat-seal tape to stop the edges curling. To stop tears when the suit stretches, we leave at least 1.5 cm between openings and round every corner to a radius of at least 3 mm.
