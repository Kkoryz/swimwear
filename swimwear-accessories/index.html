    Custom Metal Hardware, Charms and Finishes

    • Exposure and finish

      We pick the base metal and plating to stand up to chlorine and salt water. Our electroplated charms keep their bright finish after saltwater and sun.

    • Custom mould fee

      Custom logo hardware has a one-off USD 200 mould development fee, which you pay with your order deposit. Your design sets the unit price.

    • Metal and weight

      Brass and stainless steel cost more per piece than zinc alloy, and heavier hardware uses more metal.

    • Plating and detail

      Thicker or multi-layer plating and special finishes add to the cost. Fine logos need complex moulds and longer finishing, while simpler detail plates more evenly.

    • Hardware range

      Our Swimwear Hardware range covers metal rings, sliders, buckles and hooks in gold, silver, rose gold or gunmetal, and moulded plastic parts in black, white, clear or Pantone-dyed colours.

    • Buyer-supplied trims

      If you supply your own trims, send 2 pieces per item for sample checks. Then send the bulk quantity plus 3% extra at least 7 days before scheduled cutting.

    • Hardware safety

      If you need hypoallergenic trims, our Swimwear Hardware metals are lead-free and tested against the EN 1811 nickel-release limit.

    • Hardware ordering

      You can have our Swimwear Hardware supplied as part of garment production, or you can buy it separately at its applicable minimum quantities.

    Rhinestones, Crystals and Beads for Swimwear

    Decoration choices

    We offer heat-press and sew-on rhinestones and crystals in a range of sizes, colours and patterns. We also offer acrylic, resin, metal and wood beads, including custom-moulded designs matched to your colours.

    Swim-ready materials

    Stainless steel or plated brass beads, resin and acrylic beads, and glass crystals sewn into metal claw settings all hold up to repeated pool and sea use.

    Shore-only materials

    Wood beads, shell pieces and heat-press rhinestones go only on shore-only accessories such as hats and bags; on garments, we sew every stone on by hand. On your trim sheet, we mark every option as swim-ready or shore-only.

    Stretch-safe placement

    We keep dense decoration away from side seams, cup edges and leg openings. We sew small clusters at least 3 mm apart, so the fabric around them still stretches by about 30%.

    Protected skin contact

    We stitch each decoration through soft backing and cover knots and thread ends with the lining. That way nothing rubs the skin or snags nearby fabric.

    Material cost drivers

    Your price is set by complexity, materials, decoration count and order quantity. Glass crystals and metal beads cost more than acrylic or resin, and every extra stone adds material and handwork.

    Labour cost drivers

    Hand sewing takes longer than heat-pressing. Decoration over seams, cups or curves also takes more labour than decoration on flat panels.

    Straps and Strings Made to Your Specs

    You choose the width, stretch, colour and texture of our straps and strings. We then match the construction to the load each one carries: firmer flat or braided straps handle support, and softer ruched or tube straps are mainly decorative.

    Eco Packaging Printed With Your Logo

    Your packaging design

    We customise every eco packaging option with your logo and layout. We keep the artwork simple and use only a few print colours, so the packaging stays recyclable and looks the same from order to order.

    Branded bag minimums

    Through our Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels service, we print zip-lock bags from 300 pcs with a USD 35 screen fee. Screens for cotton drawstring bags also cost USD 35.

    Wet and dry uses

    For damp suits, we offer ventilated cotton mesh bags and wet/dry bags that are water-resistant but not leakproof. Cotton drawstring, corn-starch and eco zip-lock bags are for dry presentation and shipping.

    Composting and recycling

    Our corn-starch bags break down within about 180 days in industrial composting, but not in home compost or landfill. Eco zip-lock bags have to go to a collection that accepts soft plastic (LDPE 4).

    Disposal claim approval

    Supplier data covers the bag material, not the printed packs. We print only the disposal wording you approve in writing under your market's rules.

    Bucket Hats, Hair and Beach Accessories

    • Finished accessory range

      We make waterproof bucket hats and swimming scrunchies in durable fabrics with reinforced stitching. We also make hair elastics and adjustable headbands.

    • Hat design choices

      Our straw, camo and floral bucket hats come with adjustable chin straps. You can also choose wide-brim, crochet, sports, summer and XXXL designs.

    • Waterproof hat construction

      Your waterproof bucket hats have polyester or nylon shells with PU-coated backs and taped seams, so water stays out.

    • Sun protection rating

      Hats made with our UPF 50+ fabrics come with AS/NZS 4399 test documentation. The rating applies only to dry, relaxed fabric.

    • Head size coverage

      Children's sizes run from 48 to 54 cm, standard adult sizes from 56 to 60 cm and XXXL from 62 to 64 cm. Chin straps or inner drawcords fine-tune the fit.

    • Repeated swimming use

      Hats in nylon, polyester and spandex blends, with taped or overlocked seams and soft, unstiffened brims, stand up to repeated pool and sea use when they're rinsed in fresh water and dried afterwards.

    • Shore-only hat care

      Natural straw and crochet hats are for the shore only, because soaking weakens their fibres and pulls the crowns out of shape. Each hat's care label matches how it's meant to be used.

    Quality Control and Material Safety Testing

    • Colour approval

      We check your accessories against swatches from the actual production fabric lot under the same light. We pay particular attention to custom-dyed parts.

    • Finish and fit

      We check that matte, glossy and brushed surfaces are even. Then we test each accessory on its own and fitted to the garment, and check colour and finish again.

    • Children's safety figures

      For children's swimwear, trims pass 90 N pull tests, pieces for under-3s carry no removable decorations, and metals meet EN 1811 and CPSIA limits: 100 ppm for base metal and 90 ppm for coatings.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can you provide a silicone-free grip solution for customers who cannot wear silicone tape?

    Yes. We use a textured polyurethane grip print, ribbed or plush-back elastic, or an edge band cut 1 to 2 cm shorter so its tension holds the garment in place. Before bulk production, we sample the option you choose on your style so you can approve the hold.

    Can you supply latex-free elastic components for accessories intended for latex-sensitive customers?

    Yes. For straps, scrunchies, hair elastics and headbands, we use spandex-covered or clear TPU elastic in place of rubber thread. We mark these components as latex-free on your trim sheet and keep them stored separately during production.

    For children's bucket hats, can you provide breakaway chin straps or strap-free constructions to reduce entanglement risk?

    Yes. We fit plastic safety clips that release under about 25 to 45 N. Or we leave the strap off and use an elasticated inner band with an adjustable toggle behind the crown.

    What usually goes wrong with swimwear accessories, how do you prevent it, and what care instructions should I give my customers?

    The main failures are tarnished plating, rust marks, loose stones or beads, and elastic or grip tape that goes slack. Before you sign off bulk, we lacquer plated hardware and soak-test it in salt water for 24 hours, pull-test sewn and heat-pressed stones, and stretch-test elastics and grip tape. Your hangtag or care label tells customers to rinse in cool fresh water after every swim, hand wash with mild soap, dry flat in the shade, and keep away from hot tubs, sunscreen oils and tumble dryers.

    What bead sizes and bore diameters can you supply to fit my swimwear cords?

    We supply beads from 6 mm to 20 mm across, with bore diameters from 2 mm to 5 mm in 2, 3, 4 or 5 mm options. They fit cords from 1.5 mm to 4.5 mm thick. Send us a 20 cm sample of your cord and we match the bore so the beads slide with light friction and stay where you put them.

    Which accessory choices raise or lower the cost per piece, so I can balance looks against my target price?

    Resin or plastic costs less than metal. Stock hardware and beads cost less than custom-moulded parts, and those add a one-off mould fee. Standard gold or silver plating costs less than rose gold, gunmetal or matte black. Heat-press decoration is quicker and cheaper than hand-sewn stones, but it goes on shore-only pieces. Plain liners and packaging cost less than printed ones.
