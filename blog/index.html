  • What Is a Tech Pack?

    18 November 2026

    A tech pack, short for technical package, is the instruction document a clothing manufacturer uses to develop and make a garment. It brings together drawings, materials, measurements, construction details and revisions so the brand and factory can work from the same specifications. See UpHance's guide to creating a fashion tech pack.

  • How much money do you need to start a clothing line?

    17 November 2026

    To start a clothing line, you need enough cash to develop your products, get inventory delivered, launch sales, and pay the bills until customer receipts can fund operations and replenishment. For a sense of scale, Erply's 2025 guide to clothing line startup costs budgets **$5,000–$15,000** for print-on-demand or dropshipping with basic branding and limited marketing, and **$15,000–$50,000** for small-batch production with professional branding and a comprehensive marketing launch.

  • Which is better, OEM or ODM?

    17 November 2026

    For a US swimwear brand, OEM is usually better when distinctive fit or construction is essential and you can support custom development. ODM is usually better when an existing design meets your brief and you want to reduce development work. Compare the actual samples, launch cash, delivery plan, and file handover before choosing; the manufacturing label alone does not settle those questions. The general tradeoff is explained in SEACOMP's OEM vs ODM manufacturing guide.
