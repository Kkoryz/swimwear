Custom Swimwear Prints Made in Our Own Facility

Every swimwear design we print goes through our own facility, so we control each stage and keep your turnaround short. We run industrial digital machines with a sublimation process tuned specifically for printing swimwear fabric.

If you have several designs, you can combine them in one production run, and we'll prepare them together.

Factory-Direct Sublimation Printing from Guangzhou

Your swimwear is made in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Because we run a factory-owned production and export operation, quality, cost and delivery stay under our control. We check your order throughout production and give it a full final inspection. Our Launch service covers your project from brief to after-sales support.

For quote requests covering screen printing, embroidery and labels, you'll hear back within one business day. Cut-and-sew or sublimation quotes take 2 to 3 working days. Every enquiry gets a reply within one business day.

How a full-package swimwear project runs, from brief to delivery.

  1. Consultation and design

    Your first brief, consultation and design development.

  2. Tech pack and patterns

    Tech pack and pattern creation, pattern adjustments, print development and placement.

  3. Fabric and trim sourcing

    Fabrics and trims sourced from suppliers worldwide, with our technical advice.

  4. Samples and fit review

    Prototypes and full samples, a fit review, grading and pre-production samples.

  5. Bulk production

    Precise cutting, sewing, printing or embroidery, washing, finishing and trimming.

  6. Branding and quality control

    Label printing, private-label branding, quality control and retail-ready packaging.

  7. Shipping

    Shipping worldwide, booked by our team through our forwarding partners.

Printable Swim Fabrics, Including Recycled Options

We begin with the colour result and handfeel you need, then match the printing method to the fibre.

Polyester print bases

We print full-colour sublimation on durable 100% polyester, performance blends and poly-blends.

Recycled sublimation microfibre

We print with eco-friendly ink on a 150 g/m² microfibre made of 86% recycled polyester and 14% elastane. Recycled microfibre is also available for shorts.

Other recycled options

You can choose recycled polyamide/elastane swim fabric, performance fabric for swim fit or super-soft recycled nylon. Nylon is printed with digital pigment printing.

Your supplied fabric

We accept nylon or polyester blends with spandex. Sublimation requires polyester, and nylon requires digital pigment printing.

Base fabric requirements

The base you send must be white or light, with no water-repellent, silicone or other coated finish.

Testing before cutting

Before we cut your supplied fabric, we test it for shrinkage, colour fastness and stretch.

Fabric sourcing

Our fabric sourcing covers nylon/spandex and polyester/spandex with matching linings, including 80% nylon 20% spandex. It also covers chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL recycled nylon, REPREVE recycled polyester and Italian Carvico on request.

Certified Inks That Resist Chlorine, Salt and Fading

If your swimwear will be used often in the pool or the sea, we sublimate onto polyester-rich fabric so the print lasts.

  • Certified printing inks

    Every print uses ecological inks that carry their maker's OEKO-TEX® ECO PASSPORT certification, along with OEKO-TEX® certified dyes.

  • Water-based dyes

    Our dyes are water-based. They bond into the fibres instead of leaving a thick layer on the surface.

  • Lasting print quality

    Our high-resolution sublimation prints resist chlorine, salt water and repeated washing without cracking or peeling.

  • Production certifications

    Our sustainability certifications are OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics/trims, GRS/RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Artwork Files, Repeats and Design Support

Send your artwork at 300 dpi or higher, as vector files such as AI or PDF or as image files such as PNG or TIFF.

We accept other formats too. Your artwork needs a seamless repeat or a layout that's ready to repeat. A motif repeat is typically 15–20 cm, but your design doesn't have to match that size.

If you have a non-repeating image that works for it, we help you set it up as a four-way mirror. We check the repeat seams and scale at actual garment size.

If there's a tiling problem, our design team fixes it instead of sending the file back to you. You can send print-ready artwork or develop it with us, and our file-setup guidance is free.

Placement Prints and Layout on Every Style

  • Placement instead of repeat

    If your artwork doesn't repeat, or a motif has to sit in one exact spot, we offer placement printing.

  • Artwork layout options

    You can choose front and back prints, sleeve branding, full-wrap graphics and gradient fades.

  • Consistency across sizes

    We engineer the placement on each size's cut pattern pieces, so your motifs sit in the same visual position across your whole size range.

Pantone Colour Matching for Your Prints

We match your colours to a picture you send, to our colour chart or to Pantone references. For sublimation calibration, we prefer a physical Pantone TCX reference over TPG.

You confirm the colour on a sample of the actual production fabric in daylight, so you aren't judging the result on a screen.

Mock-ups, Strike-offs and Sample Approval

  1. Scale and trial print

    We send you digital mock-ups so you can approve the scale, and we turn strike-offs around in one day. We then send a 30x30cm trial-print swatch so you can approve colour and quality, and we revise it as needed.

  2. A4 approval options

    The fastest option is an A4 strike-off that we photograph or film and email to you, at USD 35 per motif. If you want the same A4 posted to you, it costs USD 35 plus courier.

  3. Printed fabric checks

    If you need to check drape or stretch, two metres cost USD 35 per metre (USD 70) plus courier. This adds a few extra weeks.

  4. Sample and design fees

    Standard samples take 10 to 15 days and cost USD 50 each. Design costs USD 50 per design, and that covers all colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited towards your bulk order.

  5. Sampling and reorder terms

    Under our sampling terms, you pay the courier, which costs USD 50. New moulds or prints add 2 to 3 weeks. Approved reorders skip sampling, and you're welcome to arrange independent SGS or BV inspections.

  6. Development service timing

    With Development, confirming fabric and trims takes 1 to 3 days, cards and swatches reach you within 10 days, and fit approval takes 3 to 7 days. Digital samples add about one week, and revisions add time.

Print Lead Times, Pricing and Order Terms

  • Lower-cost repeat printing

    On larger runs, an all-over seamless repeat on standard print fabric keeps your costs down.

  • Higher-cost print treatments

    Exclusive development, a placement setup for each size, and specialty metallic or fluorescent inks all cost more. Keep these treatments for your hero styles to control costs.

  • Premium fabric costs

    Branded and recycled print fabrics cost more than our standard print fabric choices.

  • Bulk production timing

    Once you've approved the sample and we've received your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Order size and season set where it falls in that range, and approved repeat orders follow the same timing.

  • Payment schedule

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the remaining 50% before it ships.

  • Shipping terms

    We ship your finished order on FOB, CIF or DDP terms, and DDP is available worldwide.

  • Door delivery pricing

    With DDP, you pay one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle the duties, customs and freight through our forwarding partners.

  • Returns

    Claims and returns on printed orders follow our terms and conditions.

Print Themes and Exclusive Print Development

Print theme choices

We source nearly any colour or print for your digital-printed range, including floral, paisley, retro, zigzag, animal, strawberry, lemon and watercolour themes.

Exclusive print development

We develop exclusive prints just for you, and this adds lead time. The per-design fee covers developing the print in all colourways, plus its tech pack and CAD pattern.

Colourways for later seasons

If you develop one exclusive print and add colourways later, you don't pay a new design fee. Physical strike-offs and printed approval fabric are still charged separately for each motif.

Minimum Order Quantities and Trial Runs

Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, so small and growing labels have a quantity they can start with. We agree your first trial run case by case, and we can flex on quantity only as far as production allows.

Frequently Asked Questions

For your digital printing, do the number of artwork colours or the amount of printed coverage change the production cost?

The number of colours doesn't change your digital printing cost, because every design prints in one pass at the same rate. Coverage does matter when you compare print types. We cost all-over prints by the metre of fabric, and a small placement print on a plain piece costs less than printing the whole garment.

Can you print on a black or coloured base fabric, or does my print have to go on white fabric?

We print on black or coloured bases, but sublimation only produces tones darker than the base. All-over sublimation needs a polyester-rich fabric. Its dyes are transparent, so they give the truest colours on white or light bases and can't print lighter than the fabric. For white or pastel details, we use a white base, or digital pigment printing with a white underlayer on dark fabric.

Can I order custom-printed swimwear fabric from you without ordering garment manufacturing?

No. We only offer custom printing on swimwear we manufacture for you, and we don't take fabric-only orders. Any printed fabric we produce ships with your garment order.
