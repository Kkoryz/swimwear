Kids' Swimwear for Boys, Girls and Tweens

We're a children's swimwear manufacturer that cuts every style to suit its age group, from toddlers up to tweens.

For girls, we make bikinis, two-piece sets and one-pieces, with open backs, front zips or sun-protective long sleeves. For boys, we make solid or printed trunks and shorts with elastic waists and drawcords bar-tacked at the centre.

You can add shaped front panels, curved hemlines, side pockets or bound hems.

Rash Guard Sets, Sun Suits and Printed Beach Sets

Coordinated swim assortments

We make rash guard sets, sun-protective long-sleeve suits and printed beach sets. If you use the same fabric base and print across your assortment, the pieces match when they're sold together.

Baby swimwear

Our baby swimwear covers reusable swim nappies and nappy-friendly suits. We use separate infant blocks for 0–3, 3–6, 6–12, 12–18 and 18–24 months, and toddler sizes run from 2T to 4T.

Our Own Children's Swimwear Factory in Guangzhou, China

We make your swimwear ourselves, in a single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. Our checks run from production through to dispatch. Our factory and export company has been running since 2011.

That keeps quality, cost and delivery under our direct control, and you agree every order directly with our team.

How Zips and Size Bands Affect Unit Price

A front zip costs more per unit than a pull-on style, because it adds a zip, a placket and extra sewing time.

Larger sizes use more fabric, so they cost more too. Your quotation shows these differences. Size-band pricing suits runs weighted toward larger sizes, and one flat price suits runs spread evenly across sizes. You choose which quotation format we use.

UPF 50+ Swim Fabrics and Chlorine Resistance

  • Fabric choices

    We offer quick-dry polyester/spandex, nylon/spandex, soft stretch knits and recycled-fibre fabrics. They hold their colour through frequent washing, and we supply chlorine- and salt-water-resistant options on request.

  • Fabric by use

    For pool and swim-school ranges, we use chlorine-resistant PBT or elastane blends. For holiday and beach styles, we use softer standard stretch knits.

  • Pool durability trade-off

    PBT polyester and chlorine-resistant elastane such as LYCRA Xtra Life hold their shape longer than standard spandex, but they give up some softness and stretch. PBT is firmer and resists chlorine better. Elastane stays softer and stretchier, and it keeps a close fit through weekly lessons.

  • Skin and sun coverage

    We choose skin-friendly fabrics, including UPF 50+ materials that block UV rays. Long sleeves give more coverage, and that includes our made-to-order girls' one-pieces.

  • Standard UPF testing

    Our UPF 50+ fabrics are lab-tested to AS/NZS 4399, and we supply the documentation. The rating applies to the fabric dry and relaxed, as it was tested.

  • Additional performance tests

    If you want to make claims about performance after washing, we arrange accredited-lab testing after 20 or 40 wash cycles, plus wet and stretched testing. You pay the lab fees at cost.

  • Sourcing and toddler certification

    Through Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing, we offer 80% nylon 20% spandex with matching linings. For toddler styles, the fabric, lining and trims are certified to OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Product Class I for harmful substances; CPSIA and REACH need lab tests.

  • Certified sustainable options

    Our sustainability work includes certified recycled sourcing and production certifications: OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS, RCS, bluesign, ISO 9001 and WRAP. Together they cover tested materials, recycled content, chemistry, quality management and social compliance.

Soft Linings, Seams and Secure Trims for Comfort

Comfort against skin

We use smooth linings, secure elastic and waistbands, and soft seams that won't chafe children's skin.

Soft boxer-brief liners

For younger children and long pool sessions, our stretch nylon-spandex boxer-brief liner has flatlock seams and covered leg elastic, so it lies flat and doesn't rub.

Mesh or unlined shorts

Mesh briefs suit older boys and fast-drying beachwear. Unlined shorts fit over a swim nappy or separate undershorts.

Secure attached components

We sew or rivet on every snap, ring, slider, buckle, bead and trim, and each one has to pass a 90 N pull test.

Decoration restrictions

Under-3 sizes have no removable decorations. None of our children's designs use hanging charms, glued or heat-fixed stones, or beads small enough to swallow.

Patches and appliqués

We stitch rubber patches and appliqués all the way around the edge instead of relying on glue, so they don't lift with repeated chlorine exposure and washing.

Metal hardware testing

Metal hardware is lab-tested to EN 1811 for nickel release and kept within CPSIA lead limits of 100 ppm in base metal and 90 ppm in coatings. For US orders, a CPSC-accepted third-party lab tests your styles at cost.

Hardware sourcing service

Through Swimwear Hardware, standard stocked components are ready in 3 to 7 days. Sample sets are available for most items, and we check quality before dispatch.

CPSIA Tracking Labels and Traceability for Kids' Swimwear

  • US tracking requirement

    For children's swimwear going to the US, we sew in permanent tracking labels under CPSIA Section 103.

  • Brand and date

    Your tracking label shows your brand and the month and year of manufacture.

  • Location and batch

    We add Guangzhou, China, and the batch or PO number. We also keep production records so every order can be traced.

  • Wash-durable identification

    We use wash-durable ink on sewn-in care labels or separate labels, so the details stay readable through repeated washing and chlorine exposure.

  • Label placement

    Where the construction allows, we put the tracking and care details together at a side seam, away from the neck.

  • Launch planning service

    Our Launch service checks your testing, documents, sampling and production schedules against your destination market, launch date, design and materials.

Tween and Teen Swimwear Sizes and Shaped Fits

For tween and young teen styles that need room at the bust, waist and hips, choose a shaped teen fit instead of our proportional children's grading.

  • Sizes by age or height

    Order tween and young teen sizes by age, from 8-10 to 12-14, or by height up to 164 cm, the chart we recommend for European markets.

  • A separate teen block

    Each shaped style is drafted on its own teen block, allowing for a developing bust, a defined waist, fuller hips and a longer torso.

  • Graded area by area

    We grade the bust, waist and hips separately, so proportions, coverage, support and tension stay right from the smallest teen size to the largest.

  • Fit checked in movement

    As well as checking fit standing, we check every teen sample in movement, where riding up and gaping show.

  • Support for older sizes

    Sewn-in or removable foam padding and cup pockets suit teen tops and one-pieces, while underwire channels and moulded cups go only into the older sizes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do the drawstrings on your kids' swim trunks meet children's cord and drawstring safety rules for the EU and US, and can you make the waist without a free cord?

Yes. Our trunks and beach sets follow EN 14682, and we offer a covered flat elastic waistband, 2 to 3 cm wide, with no drawcord. Our tops follow ASTM F1816, so there are no drawstrings at the hood or neck. Waist drawcords are bar-tacked at the centre, with no toggles or knots. On under-7 sizes the drawcord stays inside the garment, and for ages 7 to 14 the free ends are 14 cm long at most.

How do you make sure white and pastel kids' swimsuits are not see-through when wet?

To keep white and pastel suits opaque when wet, we use heavier fabric, a lining, a double layer or a lined front panel. During sampling, we check the suit wet and stretched, and that tells us which construction to use.

Can you print licensed cartoon or character artwork on kids' swimwear, and what licence authorisation do I need to provide?

We print licensed characters, logos and trademarks only when you give us a written licence from the rights holder that covers the product type and sales territory. Send it to us before sampling. Without it, we can't sample or produce the artwork.

On front-zip kids' swimsuits, how do you stop the zip from pinching or rubbing the child's chin and skin?

On children's one-pieces and rash guards, a fabric zip garage covers the puller under the chin, and a soft, full-length inner placket sits behind the zip. For toddlers, you can choose a 3-snap crotch or a front or back entry zip. Snaps have soft backing behind them, zips are guarded, and snap and puller attachments are tested to 90 N. For infants, the options are crotch or shoulder snaps and full-length front or back zips. Every zip has a soft guard running the full length of the track, so the teeth and metal stay off the skin.

Can you make separate fits for toddlers in swim nappies and for toilet-trained toddlers?

Yes, we cut separate patterns. The swim-nappy fit adds 2 to 3 cm to the standard seat and rise and widens the leg openings, while the toilet-trained fit keeps the standard seat, rise and leg openings. We sample both for your approval before bulk, because a nappy layer added to a standard pattern changes the fit and the suit leaks or pulls.

Can you make tween and teen bikinis and tankinis with more coverage?

Yes. We make tankinis with more coverage, including cross-strap tops with swim shorts, as well as age-appropriate bikinis. Tops come high-neck or as sporty crops, and bottoms come full-coverage or mid-rise, or as swim shorts or skirted bottoms.
