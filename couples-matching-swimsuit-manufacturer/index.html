Private Label Matching Sets for Couples

You can order different quantities of the women's and men's pieces in our couples matching swimsuits, as long as each style reaches 50 pieces per colour.

We make these coordinated sets under your brand. Each style comes in 3 to 4 sizes. The standard S:M:L:XL ratio is 1:2:2:1. You can also set different size ratios for the men's and women's styles.

Our Own Swimsuit Factory in China

We make your swimwear in our own factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

It's a single factory, and our company owns it. Our factory and export operation are part of the same company, so we control quality, cost and delivery directly. We check your goods throughout production and again before shipment.

Our production certifications include OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Custom Prints Matched Across Different Fabrics

If your set uses different fabrics, your print goes through four steps: a digital preview, shared artwork, separate colour matching for each fabric, and a physical review of the two pieces together.

A screen can't show how each fabric base takes the print. Approving the preview settles scale and placement, but it doesn't give you reliable colour agreement. Waiting for physical samples takes longer than approving on screen alone, but it avoids colour surprises in production.

Our Custom Printing service uses water-based digital printing with calibrated colour. That gives you sharp, high-resolution artwork, less deviation on stretch fabric and consistent colour from one run to the next.

How one print is matched across two fabrics.

  1. Digital preview

    You receive a preview of the print on each fabric, showing colour matching, motif scale and placement on both garments, and approve scale and placement from it.

  2. Print from one file

    Both pieces are printed from the same artwork file at the same scale.

  3. Colour-match each fabric

    Colour is matched on each fabric separately.

  4. Compare physical samples

    The two pieces are compared side by side on the samples for every set, where colour match is judged.

Keeping Colour Consistent Between His and Hers Pieces

Different dye lots are the most common reason his and hers pieces look mismatched under shop lighting.

We plan a single dye lot to cover both pieces for your whole order, which removes that cause, and we check the pair together at final inspection. If you reorder the matching pieces together, the restock stays coordinated.

If a later reorder needs a new dye lot, it has to be colour-matched against your earlier stock. To keep the pair matched after wear, we use the same fabric base and print method for both pieces, and you can choose the chlorine-resistant fabric option for both garments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which women's and men's styles can be paired into one couples matching set?

Any women's style we make can go with men's swim trunks, board shorts or swim briefs in the same print or colour story. That includes triangle and bandeau bikinis, one-pieces and tankinis. One print also works for more than one pairing, so you could match a bikini and a one-piece to the same trunks.

Do you have ready-made couples matching designs we can brand or buy wholesale, or does every set have to be custom developed?

You don't have to develop every set from scratch. We hold some styles for wholesale, so ask us which couples designs are available right now. Our ready-made designs stay open to every buyer, but your prints, artwork and branding are used only for you. Any custom or ODM couples set we develop for your brand is exclusive to you.

How does positioning coordinated motifs on both garments affect fabric use and cutting cost compared with sharing an all-over repeat print?

Placed coordinated motifs use about 10 to 15% more fabric than an all-over repeat, because every panel has to line up with the print. Each piece takes longer to cut and costs more. The artwork has to be made for each size, and we charge physical approval strike-offs separately for each motif. An all-over repeat covers both pieces, nests tightly with the least waste and makes size grading simpler, so it's the easier choice for a first collection. If you keep placed motifs for a few hero sets, you limit the extra work and still get the deliberate, mirrored placement an all-over repeat can't give you.
