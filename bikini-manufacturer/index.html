Bikini Factory in Guangzhou, China

We make bikini sets and separates in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, from 50 pieces per style in each colour, so small and growing labels can order with us.

Send us sketches, a reference sample or a full tech pack. We'll develop your pattern, balance its proportions and grade it so the fit stays consistent across the sizes you order.

OEM Bikini Sampling, Lead Times, Payment and Shipping

Since 2011 we've kept production and export under our control. We ship FOB, CIF or all-in DDP, delivered to your door by our forwarding partners.

  1. 01

    Enquiry and quotation

    For a free quote, send us your design, materials, colours, quantities, size chart and packing needs. We agree pricing with you before sampling starts. When you contact our team, your dedicated specialist replies within one business day and arranges a callback.

  2. 02

    Design and development

    Through our OEM/ODM development, our in-house team turns your design, picture or idea into patterns, grading and fit. Our Launch service takes custom bikinis and one-pieces from brief through customisation, production, shipping and after-sales support.

  3. 03

    Sampling and fees

    A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days and costs USD 50. Design costs USD 50 per design and covers its pattern or print and all its colourways. Neither fee is refunded or credited.

  4. 04

    Confirmation and production

    Trial quantities depend on our production capacity and what we agree with you individually. Confirmed orders need a 50% deposit, and the other 50% is paid before shipment. Bulk production takes 35 to 40 days after sample approval and deposit, depending on quantity and season.

  5. 05

    Standards and inspection

    Our certified production holds OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS, RCS, bluesign, ISO 9001 and WRAP, and you can ask for recycled fabrics. We check fabric, cutting and sewing, then run random and full final inspections.

  6. 06

    Branding and printing

    Our Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels service supplies care tags, size labels and private-label logos. If we don't have the print you want, it needs a custom design. Through our Custom Printing service, sublimation prints start at 50 pieces per design.

Custom Bikini Fabrics, Linings and Support

  • Fabric feel

    Nylon-spandex feels softer and smoother on wet skin, with a close, sculpting stretch and quick recovery. Polyester-spandex dries faster, stays more stable and is easier to look after.

  • Chlorine and sun

    Polyester-spandex holds its shape better through repeated chlorine and sun exposure. For nylon styles, we offer chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life to extend stretch recovery.

  • Support construction

    We match the construction to cup size and use. Foam cups or power mesh give lighter support. Larger cups and active styles need underwire, reinforced elastics and adjustable straps and hardware to keep their shape when wet.

  • Structure trade-offs

    Lighter builds stretch out quickly under heavier busts. Underwire and extra hardware add cost and development time, and they make lounging tops less soft and packable.

  • Lining and coverage

    You can choose full or front-only swim lining in nude, white, black or a shade matched to the shell, and you can ask for recycled lining. White and pale colours need full lining, double layers, a lined front panel or a heavier shell fabric.

  • Your supplied components

    We work with your own fabric, cups and underwires. Send 2 cups and 2 underwires per cup size for sample fit, chlorine and salt-water checks. Then supply the bulk components with 3% extra cups and underwires.

  • Sourcing choices

    Through Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing, we offer 80% nylon 20% spandex, matching linings, and ECONYL, REPREVE and Italian Carvico on request. We also offer textured, ribbed, satin, glitter, metallic, sequin, towel and leather fabrics.

  • Branded hardware

    Through Swimwear Hardware, custom moulds cost USD 200 each. Resin O-rings need 100 pieces per colour, logo metal tags 100 pieces. For trims you supply, send 2 test pieces of each, then 3% extra that arrives at least 7 days before cutting.

Bikini Reinforcement, Edge Finishes and Lacing Details

Reinforced stress points

We reinforce the straps, side seams and crotch on every style. Putting a bikini on and wearing it wet puts strain on these points, and once one of them fails, the garment is finished.

Stitched elastic edges

We fold rubber or swim-grade elastic into a binding, or turn it and coverstitch it. Stitched elastic gives your ties and leg openings the firmest grip and lasts the longest.

Bonded minimal finishes

Bonded and laser-cut edges lie flatter and can't be seen under clothing. They hold less tension, though, so they suit lighter-support tops and cheeky bottoms. We keep the reinforcement flat and narrow so it adds as little bulk as possible.

Eyelets and lacing

You can add eyelets and lacing as embellishments or special finishes. We choose each edge finish to match the tension that part of the garment carries.

Hand-applied decoration

Our Embroidery service adds beads, sequins, stones, rhinestones, beaded chains, appliqués, embroidered patches and crochet trims to your bikinis and cover-ups. We work the crochet by hand and sew on each bead and sequin one at a time.

None are glued or heat-pressed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the sample fee cover a full bikini set, or are the top and bottom charged as separate samples?

If we develop a matching top and bottom together, they count as one sample at USD 50, plus USD 50 for courier delivery. A top or bottom developed on its own is charged as its own sample. Each physical fit revision costs another USD 50 and adds courier time. We don't refund these fees or credit them against your bulk order. Minor digital adjustments are free. You'll avoid extra rounds if you finalise your size chart, measurement sheet and construction details before sampling starts. Underwire, reversible and laced builds need at least one physical revision for a wear check.

Which construction and trim choices on a bikini add the most to the price, compared with a basic lined triangle set?

Cup construction adds the most: underwire, moulded or removable cups, and power mesh. Next come extra hardware, reversible or double-layer builds, hand-applied embellishment, sublimation printing and the packaging format you choose. Branded or recycled fabrics also raise the price.

Which of your metallic, glitter, velvet and other fashion-finish bikini fabrics suit repeated pool or sea swimming rather than mainly dry resort wear?

Our swim velvet suits regular sea swimming. It's a 240 g/m² blend of 90% polyamide and 10% spandex that resists salt water and gives UV protection. Metallic foil, glitter and other surface coatings are for mainly dry resort wear, because repeated chlorine exposure dulls their sparkle. For styles that will see a lot of pool swimming, we use solid-dyed or printed nylon and polyester, including chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life.

How do you construct reversible bikinis so seams, padding and closures work with either side worn outward?

We sew layers with matching stretch as two finished outer faces and enclose the seam allowances, so no overlocking shows on either side. The inner cup pocket has a 6 cm opening, so the padding comes out whichever side is facing outward. We use double-sided rings, slides or tie strings in place of one-sided hooks, and we wear-check both sides before bulk production.

How are the ends of your bikini strings and lacing finished so they don't fray or slip out of the eyelets?

We turn in and bar-tack the ends of self-fabric tubes, or heat-seal the cord tips. You can also choose metal or silicone tips, or beads. Lacing ends get knots or stoppers at least 3 mm wider than the eyelet opening. Before packing, we pull-test the strings and eyelets at 70 N.
