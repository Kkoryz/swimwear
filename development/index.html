    Swimwear Development from Your Idea to an Approved Sample

    Your own sketch becomes a new style, with a fit sample for you to approve first.

    Talk to us about development
    • 3 to 5 days Pattern drafting time
    • 2-3 weeks Extra for custom development
    • 1.5 metres Sampling fabric per style
    • 10 to 15 days Standard sample time

    Ready Design, Custom Design or a Mix?

    You can choose our ready designs, develop custom swimwear, or combine both in one collection.

    Our ready designs already have finished patterns and grading. If you only change colours or prints, or add your own hardware, you skip sampling and order bulk straight away. If you change the fit or construction, we make a new fit sample first.

    We manufacture your swimwear ourselves, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

    Quality Checked at Every Stage of Your Order

    Our factory ownership and quality controls keep production and export within one company. That puts quality, cost and delivery under our direct control, and we run checks throughout your order.

    When you enquire about development, we assign you a dedicated swimwear specialist, who arranges a call back to talk through your project.

    Quality checks at every stage of an order

    1. Fabric on arrival

      Incoming fabric is checked when it reaches the factory.

    2. Cutting

      Cut pieces are checked at the cutting stage.

    3. Sewing

      Garments are checked during sewing.

    4. Random checks

      Random checks are made on finished pieces.

    5. Full final inspection

      Every order gets a full final inspection.

    What You Can Send Us to Start

    Your starting reference

    Send us annotated screenshots, a tech pack, a hand-drawn or digital sketch, or an existing swimsuit or lingerie piece, and we build your custom prototype from it.

    Details beyond drawings

    Development is anchored on your most concrete reference. If you start from a sketch, marked-up photos give us the details a drawing leaves out, including sewing, cleavage, waist and back coverage.

    Accepted file formats

    We take CAD files in DXF (Gerber/AutoCAD) and DGS formats, and tech packs in whatever format you already use.

    Included development work

    The per-design fee covers creating a tech pack from your sketch, along with the CAD pattern or print across all its colourways.

    Bust Support and Construction Options

    We match bust support to your silhouette, your fabric and the support your customers need. We don't add every option available.

    • Padding and cups

      You can choose sewn-in or removable padding, moulded cups in sizes A to DD, and foam padding to suit your style.

    • Support and construction

      You can choose from underwire channels, removable cup pockets, power mesh, metal back-strap adjusters, invisible seams, high-cut legs and your own branded accessories.

    • Fabric choices

      Our swimwear fabrics include nylon or polyester blended with spandex, such as 80% nylon 20% spandex, with matching linings. When you request them, we also offer chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL, REPREVE or Italian Carvico fabrics.

    • Materials before sampling

      Through Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing, you can order a USD 80 swatch pack delivered door to door by DHL Express. We credit the full amount against your first bulk purchase order.

    Pattern Making, Size Grading and Fit Approval

    Our in-house team takes care of pattern design, grading and fit development, so there's less back-and-forth over your design.

    1. Digital pattern drafting

      We draft your patterns to your size chart in 2D AutoCAD, accurate to the millimetre. They're ready 3 to 5 days after we receive your tech pack.

    2. Fabric-specific ease

      We calculate negative ease for the exact fabric you'll produce in, including how it behaves wet. Then we cut the fabric automatically so sample making stays fast.

    3. Style assessment

      We review every private label sample for fit, drape, coverage, measurements and appearance. We also control strap tension, leg openings, symmetry and stretch recovery.

    4. Structured style checks

      On structured styles, our sample review also covers underwires, cup shape and how the hardware is attached.

    5. Documented fit approval

      We sign off one base size in production fabric against a measurement sheet, not just photos. Your feedback goes into revisions to the pattern and construction.

    6. Full range confirmation

      Once the fit is approved, we grade the full range and sew and measure the remaining sizes. We confirm the grading of each size before production.

    Before we start drawing, we talk with you about your target customer, your requirements, the styles you need, your design goals and your brand guidelines.

    We support both OEM and ODM development, including projects that start with an idea instead of finished technical files. Your tech pack records the measurements, materials, construction and design specifications. The technical flat drawings show seam types, where the elastic goes and the closures.

    What the Service Covers and Who Owns the Patterns

    Development through production

    Our service covers pattern making, swimwear product development, size grading, marker making, sampling and bulk production. We work at a scale that keeps smaller launches affordable.

    Development without bulk

    You can book patterns and samples on their own without committing to bulk. If you book both with us, development and production stay together.

    Your custom files

    On fully custom projects, the tech packs and CAD patterns we develop become yours once you've paid the related fees.

    Certified production

    Our production credentials cover OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS, RCS, bluesign, ISO 9001 and WRAP.

    Colours, Custom Prints and Mockups

    Collection mockups

    We make custom prints in-house. Once you've picked your colours or prints, we prepare mockups for you to approve. If you ask, we also make a free 2D mock-up of your full collection before cutting.

    Physical colour approval

    Before production, we send you sample colour swatches. You sign off colours on physical swatches or a strike-off, not on screen mockups.

    Printed sample timing

    Samples in the actual printed fabric take about one week longer. You can check your print on an A4 strike-off instead.

    Artwork development

    Our Custom Printing service takes your artwork as it is, adjusts it or creates it for you, then prepares the production files. New styles and bulk runs go through digital design approval and a physical sample sign-off.

    Hardware, Labels and Packaging Alongside Your Samples

    With our Swimwear Hardware service, logo or custom-colour hardware samples are ready about 15 to 20 days after you approve the artwork.

    Standard colour-matched parts take 7 to 10 days. Bulk hardware starts only after you sign off, and we can fit the approved parts onto your garment sample.

    Through Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels, we develop and review your labels, hang tags, hygiene liners and packaging at the same time as your garment samples. Your branding is locked before bulk, and making those decisions early takes several weeks off your production schedule.

    Component Sourcing and Production Preparation

    • Full or partial sourcing

      We source fabrics, elastic, hardware and tags for you from trusted suppliers. Choosing components takes around 1 week, and longer with some suppliers.

    • Choices before costing

      We settle your components before the samples are finished. That way the consumption report and bulk costing match the materials that go into production.

    • Bulk component orders

      Before mass production, we report how much of each component your order uses and work out the production cost. Then we help you order the components you need.

    • Your supplied materials

      You can send us your own fabric, trims, hardware and labels for sampling. We can also import from your mill if you give us the supplier name and fabric code.

    • Sampling fabric quantities

      For sampling, send about 1.5 metres of each fabric and lining for each style in each colour.

    • Bulk fabric allowance

      For bulk, send 5% extra fabric. It has to reach our factory at least 7 days before cutting is scheduled.

    Fit Sampling, Wet Testing and Approval

    1. Plain first sample

      For a custom design, we get the fit right on a white or black sample first. Then we move on to the colours or prints you've chosen.

    2. Live model fitting

      A real person wears every fit sample. The base size is M for standard styles and 2XL for plus-size swimwear.

    3. Dry and wet testing

      We test the same suit on the fit model dry and then wet, checking its stretch, coverage and opacity.

    4. Correcting wet opacity

      If the fabric goes see-through when wet, we add or replace the lining or switch to a heavier fabric. Then we wet-test it again before we send you your approval sample.

    5. Revising failed fit

      If you don't approve the fit, we amend the patterns and make new samples after each fitting. Most samples are approved in the first round.

    6. No-charge corrections

      Minor digital adjustments take minutes and are free. If a sample differs significantly from the approved handover, we remake it at no extra cost.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    If I send another brand's swimsuit, will you copy it exactly, or do you only use it as a reference for fit and construction?

    We use your reference swimsuit for fit, construction and sizing. We don't copy another brand's logos, trademarked prints or protected design details. We measure the reference and turn it into a spec sheet with tolerances, then build the pattern and make the changes you've asked for. Your finished style carries your own branding and artwork.

    If I launch with ready-to-go styles, could other brands sell the same designs, and how does that compare with the custom route?

    Yes. Our ready-to-go designs are non-exclusive and stay available to every buyer, so other brands can sell the same base styles. Your prints, artwork and branding are used only for you, though. Any custom or ODM design we develop for your brand is exclusive to you, and we'll sign an NDA if you ask.

    What measurement tolerances do you work to when you check samples and graded sizes against my spec sheet?

    We check every sample and every graded size against your spec sheet. Straps, bindings and any measurement under 10 cm can be off by no more than 0.5 cm either way (-0.5 to 0.5 cm). Body measurements can be off by no more than 1 cm either way (-1 to 1 cm).

    Can you show my styles as 3D virtual samples before you sew a physical prototype, or do you only offer 2D mockups?

    Yes. Before we sew your first physical prototype, we make 3D virtual samples free of charge, on top of our free 2D collection mockups. You check the proportions, where your artwork's print sits and the colours on screen, so you need fewer rounds of physical samples.
