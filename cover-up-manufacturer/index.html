Our Cover-Up Factory in China for Kaftans, Sarongs and Kimonos

We make cover-ups for you to layer over your bikinis and swimsuits, from kaftans, sarongs and kimonos to tunics and beach dresses.

We also manufacture swimwear ourselves in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. Your range covers maxi and mini lengths and mesh, crochet and printed pieces. Silhouettes can be fitted, oversized, draped, softly structured or asymmetrical, and the style direction can be minimalist, boho, sporty, luxury or high-fashion.

We pick each fabric first for how well it covers when wet and where you'll wear it. Its look and colour come after that.

Documented sun protection

For a sun-protection line, we use tested UPF 50+ fabric in closed-weave or knit long-sleeve shirts, tunics and kaftans. The AS/NZS 4399 documentation rates the fabric dry and relaxed.

UPF claim boundaries

Sheer, open mesh and crochet pieces are fashion layers, so they carry no UPF claim. On mixed-panel styles, the rating covers only the areas made from tested UPF fabric.

Shrinkage and creasing

We pre-shrink cotton, linen and rayon before cutting, which keeps finished shrinkage within 3%. Softening or enzyme washes reduce hard creasing, and linen-rayon or linen-cotton blends wrinkle less.

Colour and care

We use dyes and prints rated grade 4 or higher for fastness to light, salt water and washing. Your care instructions say to rinse in fresh water after beach wear and dry in the shade.

Stretch knit mesh

Machine-knitted nylon or polyester mesh contains 5–10% spandex and stretches about 30–50%. It springs back to shape and grows very little when wet.

Hand-crochet growth

Cotton or rayon crochet has little stretch and lengthens about 5–10% when damp. We pre-wash the yarn and build that drop into the pattern, or we switch to a polyester/nylon blend yarn, which grows less.

Secure delicate construction

Chiffon gets French or narrow-overlocked seams, wider allowances and clean hems to stop slippage. On mesh and crochet, we secure yarn ends, bind or tape cut edges and apply an anti-fray finish to stop snagging and unravelling.

Crochet cost trade-offs

Dense crochet uses about 1.5 times the yarn of open mesh and takes longer to make. Joined squares, floral medallions and 3D shells add handwork. Open stitches, larger motifs and woven panels bring the cost down.

Custom Cover-Up Prints, Colours and Trims

First we choose the printing method that suits your fabric. Then we adapt the artwork and embellishment to match it.

Print designs and effects

We develop leopard, floral and other custom prints for you using digital, screen or sublimation printing, including gradient and ombré effects.

Soft natural-fibre prints

On cotton voile, linen and rayon challis, water-based screen or digital inks soak into the fabric, so it keeps its soft hand and fluid drape.

Sublimation and block limits

Sublimation needs polyester and doesn't work on those natural fibres. On sheer voile, we keep heavy screen-print blocks small so the fabric doesn't stiffen.

Decorative finishing options

We finish your designs with embroidery, lace insertions, metallic accents and beading, and we adapt the embellishment to each fabric.

Sheer-fabric embroidery limits

On chiffon, voile and mesh, we keep light, open motifs within about 10 × 10 cm and around 5,000–8,000 stitches. For larger areas we use lace appliqués, so the fabric doesn't pucker or sag.

Removable embroidery support

We stitch over a water-soluble or tear-away stabiliser and remove it afterwards, so no stiff backing shows through the sheer fabric.

Your colour references

For custom colour matching, we work from a picture you send, our own colour chart or your Pantone references.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which choices make a cover-up cost more, such as maxi length, hand crochet, beading or linen instead of polyester?

Longer maxi and kaftan cuts cost more than simpler polyester styles. So do linings, linen and other natural fibres, and branded or recycled fabrics. If you choose a branded or recycled fabric, we list the price difference as a separate line on your quote. Crochet, beading and embroidery cost more as the handwork gets denser and covers more of the garment. Screen printing adds a setup charge for each colour. To bring the price down without lowering fabric quality, cut back the handwork, shorten the length, or use digital printing for short multicolour runs.

Can you cut and sew cover-ups from fabric we supply, including delicate woven and openwork materials?

Yes. We cut and sew the delicate wovens, lace, crochet and other openwork fabrics you send us. For cover-ups, all we need from you is the fabric's composition and care data.

How much longer do hand-crochet or hand-beaded cover-ups take to sample and produce than plain or printed styles?

A standard sample takes 10 to 15 days. Hand-crochet or hand-beaded samples take about 5 to 7 days more, and embroidered samples take about 2 to 3 days more. In bulk production, hand beading, sequins, rhinestones and hand crochet add 1 to 2 weeks, depending on how dense the work is. Our in-house embroidery adds about 5 to 7 days. We sew every bead and sequin on your cover-ups by hand. None are glued or heat-pressed.

Which of your cover-up materials are suitable for wearing into the water rather than only layering over swimwear on shore?

You can wear nylon or polyester-spandex swim fabrics, quick-dry polyester mesh, knit mesh and synthetic-yarn crochet into the water, and they dry fast. If your pieces are for the pool, we can supply chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life on request. Chiffon, cotton voile, linen, rayon and bamboo blends are layers for the shore. Once they're soaked, they get heavy, cling and lose their shape.

Can your cotton, linen and rayon cover-up fabrics come with certification such as OEKO-TEX or organic certification?

Yes. Selected cotton, linen and rayon fabrics fall under our OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification for tested fabrics and trims. We buy organic cotton from GOTS- or OCS-certified mills and can send you their certificates on request. Our organic cotton styles are GOTS certified. We don't hold OCS or FSC certification ourselves, so your finished garments can't carry those labels in our name. FSC-certified paper hang tags are available on request.

Can you put my swimsuit print on a chiffon or linen cover-up so the set matches, and how close will the colours be?

Yes. We print your swimsuit artwork on chiffon, rayon challis or linen and adjust the colour setup for each fabric. We match both runs to your Pantone references, and you approve strike-offs placed side by side. The same file looks different on swim knit than on a light woven fabric. On sheer or natural-fibre grounds, the print reads slightly softer, which the side-by-side strike-offs show before bulk.
