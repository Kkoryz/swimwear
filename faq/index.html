    Strandmill FAQ: Our Background and How to Work With Us

    We answer your questions plainly, from the first sketch to the carton at your door.

    Ask us a question

    • 4 to 7 days Customs and dispatch
    • 3 to 4 sizes Size split per minimum

    Strandmill at a Glance

    We make your swimwear ourselves, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. Production and export are handled within one company, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control. Your brief goes through sampling and approval, then into production, inspection and shipment.

    We reply to every enquiry within one business day.

    How a typical order runs

    1. Brief or tech pack

      You send your brief or tech pack.

    2. Sample

      Sample ready in 10 to 15 days.

    3. Approval and deposit

      You approve the sample and pay the deposit.

    4. Bulk production

      35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

    5. Full final inspection

      Every order gets a full final inspection.

    6. Shipment

      Shipped on FOB, CIF or DDP terms.

    Our Background & How We Sell

    We sell only to brands that hold and ship their own stock, so your warehouse or third-party fulfilment partner needs to be ready before delivery.

    We don't dropship, and we don't fulfil marketplace or web-store orders. We sell only through our website and our team. We have no online or marketplace shop, and we agree every order with you directly. Our factory background goes back to 2011.

    We keep our private-label clients anonymous. To give you a client reference, we need that client's written consent. If they consent, you get the reference within 5 business days of your request.

    While you wait, we offer you our catalogue and available garments from earlier production runs, so you can handle and test them yourself.

    OEM, ODM & Design Confidentiality

    Confidential design files

    We sign your NDA or cooperation agreement before we receive any files. We keep those files restricted to your company account, and we protect your designs, prints, patterns, brand and ideas.

    Your patterns and brand

    We use the patterns you supply only for your company, and we never reveal your brand without your approval.

    OEM and ODM development

    A style starts from your design, a picture or an idea. Our in-house team handles pattern design, grading and fit development, which cuts down the design back-and-forth.

    Stock Styles & Wholesale Buying

    • Stock wholesale styles

      As well as making to order, we keep some styles in stock for wholesale buyers.

    • Check before branding

      Before you pick ready-to-brand styles, you check fabric hand-feel and fit on up to three garments from earlier runs. Any preprinted samples we have ship within 1 to 2 business days.

    • Wholesale access

      Send your enquiry through the form on our website or by email, and we agree every order with you directly.

    Pricing & Quotes

    • Individual style pricing

      We don't publish prices online. We quote from your spec sheets, detailed photos or reference images.

    • Construction and materials

      Your price depends on the style's complexity, coverage, fabric, lining, print method and any custom printing.

    • Branding and quantity

      Labels, branding, packaging and order quantity also set the cost. We confirm bulk pricing once we've reviewed your design and the fabric you've chosen.

    • Factory-level costs

      Our private-label and wholesale prices reflect direct production, buying materials in bulk and efficient manufacturing.

    • Volume discounts

      The more pieces you order, the lower your per-unit cost, and that includes custom embroidered pieces. For 3,000 pieces or more, you get a custom quote at our best pricing.

    • Quote before sampling

      With our quotation process, we agree the price with you before we make your sample.

    Fabrics & Materials

    Our swimwear fabrics and sourcing range covers nylon or polyester blended with spandex, including 80% nylon / 20% spandex with matching linings. On top of that there's chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, recycled ECONYL nylon, recycled REPREVE polyester and Italian Carvico fabrics, which we source on request.

    Samples: Timing, Fees & Delivery

    Your standard sample takes 10 to 15 days, and DHL delivers it to your door in 5 to 7 days. Each sample costs USD 50, plus a USD 50 design fee per design that covers its pattern or print in all colourways.

    Neither fee is refunded or credited against bulk. Courier delivery is another USD 50.

    Minimum Order Quantities

    • Starting minimum

      Swimwear orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. That suits small and growing labels launching a capsule or testing their market.

    • Sizes and bikini counting

      You split each style-and-colour minimum across 3 to 4 sizes. A bikini top and bottom together count as one piece.

    • Fabric minimums

      Imported or custom-dyed fabrics carry their own mill minimums in metres, which we check before costing; the garment minimum stays 50 pieces per style in each colour. We agree first-trial quantities with you case by case, within our production limits.

    • Matching accessories

      Bucket hats, scrunchies and headbands also start at 50 pieces per style per colour. We cut them from your swimwear fabric or print.

    • First collection planning

      If you build your first range around a few core styles with matching accessories, it stays within our low per-colour minimums.

    • Ready-made and couple styles

      You can order our ready-made designs without a tech pack. Matching couple sets also start at a low minimum.

    • Resort piece minimums

      The same minimum applies even when a piece has embroidery, special washes, unusual construction, mixed fabrics and prints, or private-label details.

    • Fabric and print support

      We help you balance fabric stretch against durability, and we guide the printing on swimwear that carries your own label and logo.

    Order Sizes, Mixed Sizes and Colours

    • Growing production volumes

      We take on boutique batches, small team orders and large retail programmes, and your sizing stays consistent as your volumes grow.

    • Combined order planning

      You can mix sizes and colours within one order. Your quantities decide which styles we combine, and we plan production around your requirements.

    • Mixed carton ratios

      We pack your size split at no extra cost, including mixed age ratios for children's swimwear.

    • Standard size split

      Our S:M:L:XL ratio is 1:2:2:1, for example 2 S, 4 M, 4 L and 2 XL.

    • Even carton quantities

      If you order each size in multiples of 5 or 6, your cartons pack evenly.

    • Larger size split

      For a larger-size assortment, we propose 4 each of L, XL and 2XL per carton.

    Bulk Production Lead Times

    Once you've paid your deposit, we schedule bulk production based on your signed-off sample and specifications. Order size and season affect timing. So do design complexity, sample revisions, material availability, washing, customisation, our production schedule and holidays.

    If you lock your specifications early and allow time for fabric sourcing and holidays, all of this fits into your launch plan.

    From sign-off to bulk production

    1. Sign off sample and specs

      You approve the sample, fit, fabrics and technical details.

    2. Lead time confirmed

      We confirm your exact lead time with the order.

    3. Deposit received

      Bulk starts once the deposit is received.

    4. Bulk production

      Usually 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

    Order Confirmation & Scheduling

    1. Agree commercial details

      Before you confirm the order, our sales team goes over price, quantity and delivery time with you and gives you a detailed production schedule.

    2. Confirm production details

      Once your sample is approved, we confirm specifications, colours, size ratio, packaging, quantity and schedule with you before bulk starts.

    3. Confirm and queue

      When you give final approval on the prototype, you can confirm the bulk order. We then handle purchase orders strictly in the order we receive them.

    4. Control subsequent changes

      Your approved sample and confirmed specification sheet are the only references we use for bulk. Any change after confirmation becomes a revision with a new schedule.

    5. Track delivery dates

      Our merchandisers track progress against your confirmed delivery date, and if a delay comes up, they tell you within 2 business days.

    Priority & Rush Orders

    If you produce with us every month, you get scheduling priority.

    If your launch is time-critical, give us an early forecast so we book your production slot instead of relying on rush production. We accept a rush order when the product, quantity, artwork and our available workload capacity allow it.

    We quote the surcharge as a percentage of order value; you pay it in full with your 50% deposit.

    Repeat Orders & Restocks

    We keep your approved designs, patterns, artwork and production specifications, so your restocks match earlier deliveries.

    Reorder under the same style references and list any changes in writing. We store leftover components free and use them first on your next run. Approved repeat styles skip sampling: bulk takes 35 to 40 days after deposit.

    A style needing a new mould or print adds 2 to 3 weeks.

    Quality Control & Inspections

    • In-house quality checks

      We check your incoming fabric, then cutting and sewing, then run random checks and a full final inspection.

    • Your chosen inspector

      Before shipment, you can inspect the goods yourself or appoint an independent inspector such as SGS or BV.

    • Inspection arrangements

      Book any third-party inspection before you pay the balance, and agree the inspection standard and checklist with us in advance.

    Test Reports & Certifications

    Check your market's and retailers' testing requirements before sampling.

    Our available reports name the fabric, trim or hardware that was tested. They don't cover your specific style unless that style was tested. When your ordered styles, colours and trims need reports, we arrange testing at an accredited lab and share the results with you before bulk.

    Lab fees are charged at cost on your balance invoice. Our production certifications cover OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

    Payment Terms & Shipping Options

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm your order and the other 50% before we ship, both by T/T bank transfer in USD or EUR. Put your order number in the transfer reference, and once you've paid, send us the bank remittance slip.

    For samples and courier charges, you can also pay by PayPal.

    We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. Our DDP service covers destinations worldwide: you pay one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle the duties, customs and freight for you.

    We can arrange shipping and logistics for your order, or you can arrange them yourself. Freight pricing depends on your destination and order size.

    If you ask for freight alongside your goods quote, you get the full delivered cost to compare, and it's clear who pays each charge under the delivery terms we agree.

    We measure your packed cartons, then choose sea, air, rail or express based on actual weight, volume and urgency.

    Our planning guide puts air express quickest below 100 kg and sea freight economical at around 300 kg. We ship by express carriers such as DHL and FedEx. We ship regularly to the US, EU, UK and Australia.

    We email you the courier tracking link within a few hours of collection.

    Customs & Export Documents

    Before dispatch, send us your consignee details and certificate requirements, so our export documents match what you need at import.

    Our shipping department handles the customs declaration, and declaration plus dispatch takes 4 to 7 days. We prepare your commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and the other customs documents your shipment requires. That includes compliance paperwork for children's products when your order needs it.

    You get complete export documentation for every freight mode.

    Dispatch & Delivery to Your Door

    Before we pack, confirm your discharge port, delivery address or fulfilment centre, so the carton labels, marks and pallet builds meet your receiver's requirements.

    We film-wrap your cartons to protect them in transit. As soon as production and packing are finished, we dispatch straight from our factory on the schedule you agreed and give you an estimated delivery timeline. Every shipment is tracked from departure through to arrival.
