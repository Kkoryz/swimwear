Strandmill at a Glance

We make your swimwear ourselves, in our own single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. Production and export are handled within one company, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control. Your brief goes through sampling and approval, then into production, inspection and shipment.

We reply to every enquiry within one business day.

How a typical order runs

  1. Brief or tech pack

    You send your brief or tech pack.

  2. Sample

    Sample ready in 10 to 15 days.

  3. Approval and deposit

    You approve the sample and pay the deposit.

  4. Bulk production

    35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season.

  5. Full final inspection

    Every order gets a full final inspection.

  6. Shipment

    Shipped on FOB, CIF or DDP terms.

Our Background & How We Sell

Do you sell to brands that hold their own stock, or do you dropship?

We sell only to brands that hold and ship their own stock, so your warehouse or third-party fulfilment partner needs to be ready before delivery. We don't dropship, and we don't fulfil marketplace or web-store orders.

Can I buy from an online shop or marketplace store of yours?

No. We sell only through our website and our own team. We have no online or marketplace shop, and we agree every order with you directly. Our factory background goes back to 2011.

Can you put me in touch with one of your existing clients?

We keep our private-label clients anonymous, so a reference needs that client's written consent. If they consent, you get the reference within 5 business days of your request. While you wait, we offer you our catalogue and available garments from earlier production runs, so you can handle and test them yourself.

OEM, ODM & Design Confidentiality

Will you sign an NDA before we send our design files?

Yes. If you need an NDA or cooperation agreement first, tell us in your first message, and we sign it before you send any files. We keep those files restricted to your company account, and we protect your designs, prints, patterns, brand and ideas.

Will you use our patterns for other clients or reveal our brand?

No. We use the patterns you supply only for your company, and we never reveal your brand without your approval. We also keep our private-label clients anonymous.

Do you offer both OEM and ODM development for new styles?

Yes. A style can start from your own design, a picture or just an idea. Our in-house team handles pattern design, grading and fit development, which cuts down the design back-and-forth.

Stock Styles & Wholesale Buying

Do you keep any swimwear styles in stock for wholesale buyers?

Yes. As well as making to order, we keep some styles in stock for wholesale buyers. Send your enquiry through the form on our website or by email, and we agree every order with you directly.

Can I check fabric and fit before I brand your ready-made styles?

Yes. Before you pick ready-to-brand styles, you can check fabric hand-feel and fit on up to three garments from earlier runs. Any preprinted samples we have ship within 1 to 2 business days.

Pricing & Quotes

Do you publish prices, and what do you need to quote a style?

We don't publish prices online. We quote each style from your spec sheets, detailed photos or reference images. With our quotation process, we agree the price with you before we make your sample, so you know your cost before sampling starts.

Which choices set the unit price of my swimwear?

Your price rests on the style's complexity, coverage, fabric, lining, print method and any custom printing, plus your labels, branding, packaging and order quantity. We confirm bulk pricing once we've reviewed your design and the fabric you've chosen.

Do larger orders get a lower price per piece?

Yes. The more pieces you order, the lower your per-unit cost, and that includes custom embroidered pieces. For 3,000 pieces or more, you get a custom quote at our best pricing. Because you deal directly with our production floor, private-label and wholesale orders are priced at factory level.

Fabrics & Materials

Which swimwear fabrics do you work with, and what can you source on request?

Our swimwear fabrics and sourcing range covers nylon or polyester blended with spandex, including 80% nylon / 20% spandex with matching linings. On request, we also source chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, recycled ECONYL nylon, recycled REPREVE polyester and Italian Carvico fabrics.

Samples: Timing, Fees & Delivery

How long does a sample take, and how is it delivered?

Your standard sample takes 10 to 15 days to make. DHL then delivers it to your door in 5 to 7 days. We quote first, and sampling starts once you agree the price.

What do samples cost, and are the fees refunded against bulk?

Each sample costs USD 50, plus a USD 50 design fee per design that covers its pattern or print in all colourways. Courier delivery is another USD 50. Neither the sample fee nor the design fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

Minimum Order Quantities

What is your minimum order quantity for swimwear?

Swimwear orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour. That suits small and growing labels launching a capsule or testing their market. We agree first-trial quantities with you case by case, within our production limits.

Can I split the minimum across sizes, and how do bikinis count?

Yes. You can split each style-and-colour minimum across 3 to 4 sizes. A bikini top and its bottom together count as one piece toward the minimum.

Do fabric minimums or special finishes change the garment minimum?

Imported or custom-dyed fabrics carry their own mill minimums in metres, which we check before costing. The garment minimum stays 50 pieces per style in each colour, even when a piece has embroidery, special washes, unusual construction, mixed fabrics and prints, or private-label details.

Do matching accessories and ready-made styles have their own minimums?

Bucket hats, scrunchies and headbands also start at 50 pieces per style per colour, cut from your swimwear fabric or print. You can order our ready-made designs without a tech pack, and matching couple sets also start at a low minimum.

Order Sizes, Mixed Sizes and Colours

Can I mix sizes and colours within one order?

Yes. You can mix sizes and colours within one order, and we pack your size split at no extra cost, including mixed age ratios for children's swimwear. Your quantities decide which styles we combine.

Which size ratio do you use for a standard carton?

Our S:M:L:XL ratio is 1:2:2:1, for example 2 S, 4 M, 4 L and 2 XL. If you order each size in multiples of 5 or 6, your cartons pack evenly. For a larger-size assortment, we propose 4 each of L, XL and 2XL per carton.

Do you take small batches as well as large retail programmes?

Yes. We take on boutique batches, small team orders and large retail programmes, and your sizing stays consistent as your volumes grow. We plan production around your requirements.

Bulk Production Lead Times

How long does bulk production take after sample approval?

Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production usually takes 35 to 40 days, depending on order size and season. We confirm your exact lead time with the order, based on your signed-off sample and specifications.

What else can lengthen the bulk production lead time?

Beyond order size and season, timing depends on design complexity, sample revisions, material availability, washing, customisation, our production schedule and holidays. If you lock your specifications early and allow time for fabric sourcing and holidays, all of this fits into your launch plan.

Order Confirmation & Scheduling

What do we agree before you confirm my bulk order?

Before you confirm the order, our sales team goes over price, quantity and delivery time with you and gives you a detailed production schedule. Once your sample is approved, we confirm specifications, colours, size ratio, packaging, quantity and schedule with you before bulk starts.

Can I change my order after it has been confirmed?

Your approved sample and confirmed specification sheet are the only references we use for bulk. Any change after confirmation becomes a revision with a new schedule. We handle purchase orders strictly in the order we receive them.

How will I know if my delivery date is at risk?

Our merchandisers track progress against your confirmed delivery date. If a delay comes up, they tell you within 2 business days, so you can plan around it.

Priority & Rush Orders

Can you take a rush order for a time-critical launch?

We accept a rush order when the product, quantity, artwork and our available workload capacity allow it. We quote the surcharge as a percentage of order value; you pay it in full with your 50% deposit. If you produce with us every month, you get scheduling priority, and an early forecast lets us book your production slot instead of relying on rush production.

Repeat Orders & Restocks

How long does a repeat order of an approved style take?

Approved repeat styles skip sampling: bulk takes 35 to 40 days after deposit, and a style needing a new mould or print adds 2 to 3 weeks. We keep your approved designs, patterns, artwork and production specifications, so your restocks match earlier deliveries. Reorder under the same style references and list any changes in writing. We store leftover components free and use them first on your next run.

Quality Control & Inspections

How do you check quality while my order is in production?

Quality is checked at every stage of your order. We check your incoming fabric, then cutting and sewing, then run random checks and a full final inspection before your order ships.

Can we send our own inspector, such as SGS or BV?

Yes. Before shipment, you can inspect the goods yourself or appoint an independent inspector such as SGS or BV. Book any third-party inspection before you pay the balance, and agree the inspection standard and checklist with us in advance.

Test Reports & Certifications

Which certifications does your swimwear production hold?

Our production certifications cover OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Do your test reports cover my specific styles?

Our available reports name the fabric, trim or hardware that was tested. They don't cover your specific style unless that style was tested. When your ordered styles, colours and trims need reports, we arrange testing at an accredited lab and share the results before bulk. Lab fees are charged at cost on your balance invoice.

Payment Terms & Shipping Options

What are your payment terms for bulk orders?

You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm your order and the other 50% before we ship, both by T/T bank transfer in USD or EUR. Put your order number in the transfer reference, and once you've paid, send us the bank remittance slip.

Can I pay for samples and courier charges by PayPal?

Yes. For samples and courier charges, you can also pay by PayPal. Deposits and balances on bulk orders are paid by T/T bank transfer in USD or EUR.

Which shipping terms do you offer, and is DDP available worldwide?

We ship on FOB, CIF or DDP terms, and our DDP service covers destinations worldwide. Under DDP, you pay one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle the duties, customs and freight for you through our forwarding partners.

Can you arrange the freight, or can I use my own forwarder?

Both work. We can book shipping through our forwarding partners, or you can arrange it yourself. Freight pricing depends on your destination and order size, so ask for freight alongside your goods quote to get the full delivered cost and see who pays each charge under the delivery terms we agree.

Which shipping modes and carriers do you use for orders?

We measure your packed cartons, then choose sea, air, rail or express based on actual weight, volume and urgency. Our planning guide puts air express quickest below 100 kg and sea freight economical at around 300 kg. We ship by express carriers such as DHL and FedEx, and regularly to the US, EU, UK and Australia.

When do I get the tracking link for a courier shipment?

We email you the courier tracking link within a few hours of collection, so you can follow your goods from the moment the carrier picks them up.

Customs & Export Documents

Which customs and export documents do you prepare for my shipment?

Our shipping department handles the customs declaration, and for a bulk order, declaration plus dispatch takes 4 to 7 days. We prepare your commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and the other customs documents your shipment requires, including compliance paperwork for children's products when your order needs it. Send us your consignee details and certificate requirements before dispatch.

Dispatch & Delivery to Your Door

Can you deliver straight to our port, address or fulfilment centre?

Yes. Before we pack, confirm your discharge port, delivery address or fulfilment centre, so the carton labels, marks and pallet builds meet your receiver's requirements. We film-wrap your cartons, dispatch straight from our factory on the schedule you agreed, give you an estimated delivery timeline and track every shipment through to arrival.

Send us your brief, tech pack or reference images. We reply within one business day, review your requirements and quote before moving to sampling once you agree the price.
