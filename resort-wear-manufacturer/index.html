From Poolside to Evening: Our Full Resort Wear Range

We make resort wear for every part of the holiday day in your range, from poolside cover-ups and beach dresses to smart-casual evening looks.

Our dresses include casual sundresses, maxi and midi styles, wraps, skirt sets and evening designs. To build complete looks, you can add kaftans, sarong sets, co-ords, shirts, printed and embroidered tops, shorts, wide-leg and palazzo trousers, other pants, lightweight woven styles and layering pieces.

We make plus size collections too.

Custom Resort Wear Details, Luxury Fabrics and Seam Finishes

  • Luxury fabric choices

    If you sell to upscale holiday retail or boutique hotels, we offer linen and silk-blend resort styles as well as embroidered designs.

  • Visible seam finishes

    For luxury styles, we specify French seams or bias binding on every visible seam before cutting. This gives loose and sheer garments a finished interior.

  • Fray-resistant construction

    Linen and viscose fray with washing and wear. We enclose the edges in French seams or 1 cm self-fabric or cotton bias binding so the seams last longer.

  • Covered overlocking

    We use 4-thread overlock only on seams that a lining hides.

  • Construction trade-offs

    French seams and binding take longer to sew and cost more than overlocking. Very thick seams and tight curves limit where we can use them, and some of those styles need a lining.

  • Sheer fabric coverage

    Under sheer fabrics, we add a full or half lining in cotton voile or viscose, or a separate slip.

  • Waist and closure options

    We build waistbands with 3 cm elastic at the back or all the way round, drawcords, concealed side zips or button-and-loop closures.

Custom Resort Wear Trims, Prints and Washes

Signature detail selection

Choose a small set of details for each style: a wash, a trim and a construction feature. This gives your collection its identity and keeps costs predictable.

Trims and fastenings

We add crochet lace, tassel and fringe edging, smocked bodices and waists, shell or wooden buttons, rope ties and drawcords.

Prints and washed finishes

You can choose block, screen or digital prints. Enzyme washes, garment washes and garment dyeing give linen and cotton a softer, lived-in hand.

Shape and construction

Tiered skirts, ruffles, pin-tucks, side slits and tie-front closures are construction details you can combine with the trims and finishes you choose.

Hand-applied decoration

Our Embroidery service decorates bikinis and cover-ups with beads, sequins, stones, rhinestones, beaded chains, sequin appliqués, embroidered patches and crochet trims. The crochet is handmade, and we sew every bead and sequin onto garments individually, never gluing or heat-pressing them.

Private Label Resort Wear With Your Own Prints and Branding

  • Your collection route

    You can adapt our ready resort designs with your own prints, details and branding, or develop a complete custom private label collection with us.

  • Fabric-specific printing

    We digitally print linen, organic cotton and viscose with pigment or reactive inks. We keep sublimation printing for selected polyester resort fabrics.

  • Swim range coordination

    Send us your swimwear artwork or a reference swatch. We match the print and colour on the resort fabric itself so your two ranges go together.

  • Print matching costs

    If we match large motifs across seams or coordinated pieces, each garment uses 10% to 20% more fabric and needs extra time for single-layer cutting, so your unit cost goes up. If we match only at the centre front and key seams, you keep most of the visual impact and waste less fabric.

  • Engineered placement prints

    With engineered prints, we don't have to match the full repeat on every panel, but you need artwork for each panel and size. The higher setup cost pays off only on large enough orders.

  • Matching knit pieces

    Our Custom Printing service digitally prints soft polyester knit to match your swimsuit fabrics. We then make it into cover-ups, dresses or short suits.

  • Retail-ready brand presentation

    Our Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels service gives you industrially compostable packaging, branded accessories and heat-pressed logos inside the garment. Connected cardboard tags and individual hand-wrapping are optional. We source labels and packaging through our trim suppliers.

  • New label support

    Our Launch service gives you three finished samples, twenty fabric swatches, a catalogue of our stocked styles and a short guide to launching a swimwear brand. We also offer separate design, development and manufacturing startup packages, and new labels combine these with consulting.

Patterns, Size Grading and Fit Approval Before Bulk

We work out your dress proportions from your measurements, a reference garment, a sketch or a full technical specification.

Resort dresses and trousers come in XS to 3XL; their plus-size collections stop at 4XL, an exception to the 5XL our swimwear reaches. We fit every style on a size M model and check it against your measurement sheet.

For non-stretch wovens, we grade in fixed 5 cm steps at the bust, waist and hip, and we use a separate 6 cm rule for plus sizes. Finished body measurements stay within ±1 cm.

Plus sizes get their own grading rather than a stretched version of the core grade, so the proportions still work as body shape changes. The separate plus-size body fit check adds time to sampling.

Our in-house development team handles pattern design, grading and fit development, so you go through fewer rounds of design back-and-forth.

We fit-approve every women's resort style before bulk production.

Non-stretch fitted dresses and trousers worn over swimwear get about 6–8 cm of ease at the bust and 8–10 cm at the hip. Relaxed cover-ups get 15–20 cm. We check this extra room on a model wearing a swimsuit.

Then we shape it with darts, side slits, ties or elasticated back waists so the garment doesn't look oversized.

A swimsuit adds bulk at the bust and hip, so a cover-up fitted only over bare skin or underwear ends up tight or gaping once it goes over a swimsuit. Stretch fabrics and standalone styles don't get this extra ease. We fit them closer to the body.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you make men's resort shirts and shorts, or matching family resort pieces, to go with my women's collection?

Yes. We make men's shirts and shorts and kids' pieces alongside your women's styles. We use the same fabric and print so your men's, women's, kids' and swim looks all match. We can also put your logo on our ready-to-brand men's collection.

Which choices in a resort style, such as fabric, embroidery, length or lining, raise or lower the unit price?

Silk and linen blends cost more than viscose or cotton, and hand embroidery costs more than machine embroidery. Maxi lengths, full skirts and full linings use more fabric and sewing time. Midi or mini lengths, partial linings and unlined styles cost less. If you take out the lining, that changes the opacity and how we finish the seams. On natural fibres, digital printing suits small runs with many colours, and screen printing costs less per piece on larger runs with fewer colours. If you switch printing methods, your colour options change. We quote fabric, length, lining, embroidery and printing separately so you can compare them.

Can the organic cotton and viscose come with recognised certification such as GOTS, OCS or FSC for my sustainability claims?

We buy organic cotton from GOTS- or OCS-certified mills and viscose from mills that use FSC-certified wood pulp, and we send you the mill certificates on request. We don't hold GOTS, OCS or FSC certification ourselves, so finished garments can't carry those labels in our name. You can ask for FSC-certified paper hang tags. Which of our own certificates applies, including OEKO-TEX Standard 100, depends on the fabric you choose.

Are any of your ready resort designs in stock for wholesale, or are they all made to order, and can I see a resort catalogue?

We make most resort designs to order, but we keep some styles in stock for wholesale. Ask for our current Resort Wear line sheet to see what's available, and we'll send you the catalogue within 1 to 2 business days.

Which of your resort fabrics support machine-washable garments, and which require hand washing, dry cleaning or special drying and ironing instructions?

Our organic cotton, linen and bamboo can go on a gentle 30°C machine cycle. We pre-wash linen, which keeps shrinkage within 3%. Viscose sateen needs a cold hand wash or a delicate cycle, line or flat drying and ironing on low heat. We label silk blends hand wash or dry clean only. Every care label carries the care instructions you approve.

Can you manufacture resort garments using fabric and trims that we supply, and what material limitations apply?

Yes. We use your fabric, trims, labels and packaging, or we import fabric from your mill using its supplier name and fabric code. For resort fabrics, all we need is the composition and care data. Sublimation works only on polyester. We inspect your materials when they arrive and tell you about any shortages or defects within 3 business days, before we cut.
