A Full Men's Swimwear Range from One Supplier

You can source your full men's swimwear collection from us, with bottoms and tops all made by one supplier. We make swim trunks, swim shorts, board shorts, briefs, jammers, boxers and casual beach shorts. We also make rash guards, rash guard T-shirts, tank tops and casual swimwear.

Men's Swim Trunk Fabrics and Inseam Styles

Regular pool use

Chlorine and sun break down standard stretch fibres fastest, so our 85/15 polyester-spandex shell resists both chlorine and UV. It dries fast and keeps its colour, print clarity and shape.

Beach and casual wear

Fast drying matters more than chlorine resistance for beach trunks, so for these we offer UPF 50+ quick-dry polyester blends with mesh-lined pockets.

Inseam choices

Our swim trunks range covers classic elastic-waist, drawcord styles in 5, 7 and 9 inch inseams. It also includes a retro short trunk with a 3 inch inseam.

Our Own Men's Swimwear Factory in Guangzhou, China

We make your swimwear in our own factory, a single site in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

Our checks follow each order from production through to shipment. We have been operating since 2011. Our factory and in-house team keep production and export under our direct control.

We run around the clock with a capacity of about 100,000 pieces a month, and our 65-person sampling studio completes about 55 custom samples a day. We reply to every enquiry to our team within one business day.

From Tech Pack to Delivery: Lead Times and Shipping Terms

For new labels, our Launch service starter pack gives you three finished samples, twenty fabric swatches, a stock-style catalogue and a launch guide.

  1. 01

    Send your brief

    To get a free quote, email or WhatsApp us your design, fabrics, colours, quantity, sizing and packing needs. We check feasibility and send back estimated pricing and timing.

  2. 02

    Develop the sample

    We turn your brief into a sample for you to approve, so the product is settled before you commit to bulk production.

  3. 03

    Confirm order details

    Before the order is placed, we agree quantities, colours, customisation, availability and lead times with you.

  4. 04

    Run bulk production

    Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days. Your order size and the season set where it falls in that range.

  5. 05

    Supply your packaging

    For Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels, send your labels, tags and bags, plus 3% extra, at least 7 days before cutting. We report any shortages or defects within 3 business days.

  6. 06

    Ship your order

    Choose FOB, CIF or worldwide DDP. With DDP, you pay one all-in price for delivery to your door, and we handle duties, customs and freight through our forwarding partners.

Construction Options: Linings, Waistbands and Trims

We match fabrics, linings and trims to the way your garments are worn and washed.

Lining and panel options

You can choose Sport Mesh, Powernet or compression liners. Reinforced seams and cut-out panels are also available for your construction.

Waistband structures

For trunks, we use either full elastic with an internal drawcord or a flat front with back elastic. Board shorts get a fixed waistband with a lace-up fly.

Secure fly closures

Choose marine-grade hook-and-loop backed with soft tape, snaps, or flat woven lace-up cords with sealed tips running through metal-reinforced eyelets. These secure closures keep the fly shut in surf.

Pocket layouts

Specify mesh-lined side pockets, key pockets, cargo pockets or back pockets with drainage eyelets. Trapped water makes trunks drag and balloon.

Higher-spec fastening

On higher-spec builds, we fit corrosion-resistant pocket zips under storm flaps, so you get secure fastening along with drainage for swimming and surf.

Matched hardware

Our Swimwear Hardware service supplies colour-matched straps, rings, sliders, hooks and buckles in metal, plastic, resin or eco-friendly materials, as well as drawstrings, eyelets and zippers.

Shell Fabrics, Linings and Supplied Materials for Men's Swimwear

Your fabric choice comes down to your print method, style and target price.

  • Printing compatibility

    Sublimation needs polyester, because the dye bonds with that fibre. If you want a softer nylon shell, we switch it to pigment inks, printed in our own factory.

  • Fabric choices

    We offer solid, printable and sustainable options, including polyester-spandex, nylon-spandex, PBT, jacquard, compression, UV-protective, chlorine-resistant and athleisure fabrics.

  • Your supplied materials

    We sew the shells and linings you supply, or we import from your mill using its supplier name and fabric code.

  • Specialist fabric sourcing

    Through our fabric sourcing, we offer 80% nylon 20% spandex with matching linings. On request, we also source chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL, REPREVE and Italian Carvico.

  • Boardshort shell options

    The standard shells for our boardshorts are 100% polyester microfibre in peach twill or 95% polyester / 5% spandex 4-way stretch.

  • Production certifications

    Our production certifications cover OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics/trims, GRS/RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Custom Prints and Colour Matching

Before we start bulk printing, you approve a printed strike-off on the production fabric, with placement set across seams and panels.

Colours shift between screens, paper and stretch fabric, and seam alignment becomes visible once garments are cut and sewn. We match colours and align patterns so your whole order stays consistent.

Our Custom Printing service works from your AI or PDF artwork to produce high-resolution all-over or placement designs. The prints resist chlorinated and salt water. Our Embroidery service gives you machine or hand decoration in colours from our thread library.

Embroidery and screen printing start at 50 pieces per design, and custom woven labels start at 1,000 pieces.

Quality Checks from Approved Sample to Bulk Order

We check fit, construction and finishing against your approved sample. We test wet opacity, liner seams, drawcord anchoring and ballooning before grading and again during bulk production.

  • Liner seam strength

    We join soft mesh liners with flat-lock seams and add bar-tacks at the crotch and leg stress points so they don't tear.

  • Drawcord anchoring

    We stitch drawcords to the waistband at centre back and finish them with sealed or silicone tips so they cannot pull out.

  • Water drainage

    We add mesh drainage panels or eyelets to liners and pockets so water drains out and the garment doesn't balloon.

  • Light-colour coverage

    For light colours, we use fabric of 190 to 220 GSM or a full lining to keep coverage.

  • Print base colour

    We sublimate onto fabric pre-dyed close to the main print colour, so white doesn't show through when the fabric stretches.

  • Wet fit testing

    Before grading, we fit each style on a base-size model and wear-test it wet for stretch, coverage and opacity.

  • Independent inspection

    We welcome independent pre-shipment inspections by SGS or BV, so your appointed inspector can check the goods before dispatch.

  • Defect remedies

    Under our quality terms, you report defects with photos/videos within 7 days of receipt. After we assess them, we repair, remake, replace or credit the pieces. Shipping and handling charges are not refunded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are any of your men's jammers or racing suits World Aquatics-approved for competition, or are they supplied only as training and recreational styles?

We don't hold World Aquatics approval, so our jammers and racing suits are training and recreational styles and aren't labelled as competition-approved. If you apply for approval for your own design, we supply the samples and fabric data the application needs.

How do you construct swim-short liners to prevent inner-thigh chafing and liner ride-up during prolonged swimming or walking?

We keep flat-lock seams away from the inner thigh and secure the leg openings with soft elastic or a 1 cm silicone gripper. We also tack the liner to the shell's side seams so it can't twist or ride up. For casual shorts we use soft four-way-stretch sport mesh, and for active wear we use brief-style compression liners with a 2 to 3 inch leg.

Can you build contoured support pouches or separate front-support panels into men's briefs, jammers and swim-short liners?

Yes. In briefs and jammers we sew double-lined front pouches shaped with 2 to 3 seams, or we add separate support panels in powernet or compression fabric. Swim-short liners get the same pouch construction, in brief or boxer-brief cuts made to your measurements.
