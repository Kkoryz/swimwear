Swimwear Hardware for Every Strap and Closure

We supply swimwear sliders with matching rings and metal hooks in the same sizes, for adjustable straps, removable straps and closures.

Our metal rings run from 6 mm to 50 mm: 6 mm, 8 mm, 10 mm, 12 mm, 15 mm, 19 mm, 25 mm, 32 mm, 44 mm and 50 mm. You can also choose rust-resistant D-rings and O-rings, decorative buckles, grommets, stoppers, resin O-rings and metal tags.

We also supply hook and eye sets, front clasps, snap buttons, plastic buckles and magnetic closures.

Swimwear Hardware Types, Sizes and Materials

We size strap hardware by its inner opening and the finished width of your strap. Resin O-rings are sized differently, by band thickness and outer diameter.

Metals, Plating and Surface Finishes

Default metal

Our rings, sliders, hooks, buckles, clasps, stoppers and tags are made from high-grade zinc alloy. They're electroplated in nickel-free gold or silver, with no chrome or lead.

Forming and preparation

We die-cast zinc alloy rings, sliders and clasps in steel moulds and stamp flat tags from sheet. Then we deburr, tumble and polish the parts before plating and anti-rust coating.

Water protection

Our anti-rust topcoat stops seawater and pool chlorine from causing corrosion, rust, tarnishing, pitting or fabric stains. We test every piece for long-term wear.

Stainless steel grades

If you want stainless steel instead, you can specify grade 304 for general use or marine-grade 316L for long exposure to seawater and chlorinated pools.

Brass alternatives

For brass, we offer lead-free H62 brass with 62% copper and 38% zinc, or H65 with 65% copper and 35% zinc.

Skin-contact testing

We test every metal grade against the EN 1811 nickel-release limit. If you need hypoallergenic trims, we specify nickel-free plating tested to EN 1811.

Colours and surfaces

Rings, sliders, hooks and stoppers come in gold, silver, rose gold or gunmetal, each in gloss or matte, with a polished, brushed or powder-coated surface.

Production credentials

Our sustainability credentials include OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Custom Colours and Logo Branding on Metal Parts

  • Colour selection

    We give you Pantone references for most hardware items, and larger orders qualify for custom colour matching to your palette.

  • Logo application

    We engrave or emboss your logo on metal buckles, sliders and charms. We check your artwork against each part before we develop the mould.

  • Small-part artwork

    On stoppers and sliders, your logo needs to be at least 3 mm high, with lines at least 0.3 mm wide. Below that size, we use initials or a monogram instead of detailed artwork so the mark stays sharp after plating.

  • Physical approval samples

    Logo or custom-colour hardware samples take about 15 to 20 days after you approve the artwork. Standard parts colour-matched to your fabric take 7 to 10 days.

  • Bulk release

    We start bulk hardware only after you sign off the sample. If you ask, we fit the approved parts onto your garment sample.

  • Garment branding service

    Our Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels service supplies standard heat-transfer labels with your logo, care and size information, along with hygiene liners and hang tags. You can also add woven labels, silicone logos, fabric tags, logo cards and branded packaging.

Choosing and Fitting Hardware to Your Design

  1. Mark your design

    Send us a sketch, photo or tech pack showing where each piece of hardware goes, the finished strap width, and whether the part carries load or is decorative.

  2. Select the hardware

    We recommend the type, size and placement for your style, balancing function, appearance and budget.

  3. Secure strap fittings

    During production, we loop rings and sliders onto the strap ends, then fold the fabric back, stitch it and bar-tack it.

  4. Reinforce closure positions

    We sew hooks and clasps into reinforced fabric channels, and we press or rivet snaps through reinforced layers.

  5. Prepare grommet supports

    We back each hole with fused interfacing or double fabric that extends 10 mm around it. We place grommets at least 15 mm from seams and edges and press them in with matching washers.

  6. Test before bulk

    Before bulk production begins, we pull-test the fitted grommets on your garment sample at 70 N.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which hardware choices raise or lower the cost per piece of my swimsuit?

Plastic and resin, standard sizes and stock gloss gold or silver finishes keep your costs lowest. Your unit cost goes up with stainless steel or brass instead of zinc alloy, larger sizes, rose gold, gunmetal, matte plating, custom Pantone matching and logo engraving or embossing. Each custom logo hardware item also carries a one-off USD 200 mould fee.

What load capacities are available for rings, sliders, front clasps and buckles used in supportive swimwear?

Our metal rings and front clasps hold 8 to 10 kg, sliders hold 5 to 8 kg before the strap slips, and plastic buckles hold 4 to 6 kg. For supportive and larger-cup swimwear, we specify 25 mm or 32 mm metal parts. These ratings cover the part on its own, so we confirm the closure holds in your sewn garment sample.

How many hours of salt-spray, chlorine and UV exposure does the hardware withstand without corroding or fading?

Our metal hardware passes 48 hours of neutral salt spray to ISO 9227, 72 hours soaking in chlorinated pool water and 100 hours of UV exposure. To pass, it has to show no corrosion, rust or colour change at the end.

Can you cover rings and sliders in the same fabric as my swimsuit?

Yes. We wrap rings, sliders and buckles from 15 mm to 50 mm in your swimsuit fabric, over plastic or metal cores. During production, we use fabric from your bulk run so each colourway matches.

What is the minimum quantity and the development time for custom-moulded, colour-matched beads or a custom rhinestone motif?

Custom-moulded, colour-matched beads start at 1,000 pieces per design and colour. Development takes about 2 to 3 weeks, including a colour-matched approval sample. Custom rhinestone motifs start at 300 motifs per design, and a sample takes about 7 to 10 days.

Are your metal rings cast closed, welded or split, and which constructions are available for load-bearing straps?

Our metal rings are die-cast as one solid closed piece, with no weld seams or splits. For load-bearing halter, neck and back straps, we use only closed cast metal rings of 15 mm and above, not split rings or light plastic parts. Split rings are for hanging charms and tags only.
