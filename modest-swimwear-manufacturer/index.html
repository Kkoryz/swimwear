    Modest Swim Sets and Full-Coverage Styles We Make

    We make burkinis and modest swim sets as two-piece or three-piece outfits.

    Each one pairs a long-sleeve tunic or swim dress with full-length leggings and either a hood or a separate hijab. Our swim dresses are cut to layer over leggings.

    If you want a single-garment range, we also make full-coverage one-piece swimsuits and integrated body suits with leg coverage built in.

    Custom Modest Swimwear Styles We Produce

    Coordinated core range

    Start with a core burkini and add matching extensions. Fabric, colourway and fit block stay consistent, so the range is easier to reorder than a collection of unrelated pieces.

    Training and water sports

    We make custom sport burkinis for training, sportswear with two-tone panels, long-sleeve rash guards, and swim leggings for surfing and other water sports.

    Headwear and cover-ups

    We make swim hijabs and caps, as well as beach and poolside cover-ups, kaftans and robes in fast-drying fabric.

    Coverage Levels, Sleeves and Swim Hijab Constructions

    Choose coverage and headwear together. Tunic length, neckline and headwear work as a unit to control comfort and security. If you choose them separately, you end up with combinations that shift while the wearer swims.

    FeatureConstruction choices
    CoverageA mid-thigh tunic, a tunic with an added skirt or cape, or a knee-length swim dress with an overlapping or envelope hem. The swim dress is our longest style.
    Hijab constructionPull-on full-neck, tie-back or adjustable, or a two-piece cap and drape. Sets can also take a hood or a separate cap.
    Face and earsSoft elastic binding keeps the face opening in shape without digging in, and extra room around the ears makes it more comfortable.
    Neck and chinA long, coverstitched drape tucks inside the tunic. A shaped chin panel covers the chin and neck, and an internal drawcord within easy reach adjusts the fit.
    Headwear fabricA lighter swim knit breathes better. We dye it in the same dye lot as the tunic and leggings, and all three get approved together before bulk.
    Sleeves and necklinesFull-length sleeves, with or without thumb-loop cuffs, or three-quarter sleeves. Necklines come as a small stand collar, a high round neck or a hood set into the shoulder seam.
    Legging shapesStraight, tapered or flared legs, with an elastic or drawcord waistband.

    Fabric Matched to Pool or Open Water

    • Standard fabric weight

      Our 200–210 gsm fabric is rated UPF 50+. It absorbs little water and dries fast, so garments stay light when wet. Sport burkinis also get chlorine resistance.

    • Pool or open water

      Where your customers swim sets the specification. For pools we focus on chlorine resistance and colourfastness. For open water we focus on UPF and how the fabric behaves when soaked.

    • Wet sample approval

      We swim in every prototype to check drag, shifting, cling and opacity, check it again dry, and show you how it stays down instead of floating up. Before you approve bulk, have your own swimmer try it wet, after washing, through full strokes. That trial shows behaviour that dry swatches don't.

    • Lining and silhouette

      Light shades need a front lining. Full or front-only tunic lining, a relaxed A-line with 4 to 6 cm of extra hip ease, and overlays at the chest and hips all reduce cling and hide the body's contours.

    • Surface and shade

      Matt or textured fabrics, a heavier 220 gsm tunic option, darker shades and all-over prints all show less of the body's outline when wet.

    • Fabric sourcing choices

      Our Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing service supplies nylon/polyester-spandex fabrics with matching linings, including 80% nylon 20% spandex. On request it also supplies LYCRA Xtra Life, ECONYL recycled nylon, REPREVE recycled polyester and Italian Carvico.

    • Standard UPF evidence

      Our Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing service provides documentation for UPF 50+ fabrics tested to Australia/New Zealand's AS/NZS 4399. The rating applies to the fabric when it's dry and relaxed.

    • Additional laboratory testing

      Through Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing you can order wet/stretched tests, tests on selected colourways, or testing to EU EN 13758-1 or US AATCC 183. You pay the accredited lab's fees at cost.

    Construction That Stays Put in the Water

    To stop floating and ride-up, we don't depend on a single fix. We combine the cut, hems, inner layers and elastic finishes.

    • Tunic float control

      Elasticated or weighted hems, vents, a fitted inner layer and a close-cut waist keep the tunic from floating. Split or shaped hems stop it twisting around the legs during strokes.

    • Legging security

      Stirrups or cuffs keep leggings from riding up. Leggings sold separately get a wide, encased waistband that doesn't roll while they're worn.

    • Elastic and adjustments

      We control the elastic tension at the neck, cuffs and ankles and add silicone-backed elastic at the cuffs and ankles. Ties and drawcords stay within easy reach, and hoods are shaped to stay in place without pressing on the throat.

    • Detachable garment parts

      Detachable sleeves and skirts fasten with resin snaps or concealed zips, and each fastening is attached at a reinforced point.

    • One-piece access

      You can choose pull-on, full-length front zip, centre-back zip or half neck zip. Zipped styles have a fabric garage at the top and corrosion-resistant swim zips that work easily when wet.

    • Built-in light support

      For light shaping, we add a shelf bra with wire-free, removable contour cups inside your tunic or swim dress.

    • Integrated lower lining

      An inner brief or short in matching lining means the wearer doesn't need separate underlayers under the garment.

    • Loose coverage cuts

      Loose tunics skim the body instead of hugging it. Putting the volume through the shoulders, bust and hips instead of the hem controls drag and keeps full coverage.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does a complete two-piece or three-piece modest swim set count as one piece toward your 50-piece minimum, or is the minimum applied separately to each component?

    If you sell a complete two-piece or three-piece set as one unit, it counts as one piece toward our minimum of 50 pieces per style in each colour. If you sell tunics, leggings and hijabs as separates, each one counts on its own. Decide on sets or separates before you order, because that choice sets your minimum quantities and how mixed sizes are packed.

    Will burkinis made to your spec be accepted at public pools that only allow swim-specific fabric and close-fitting garments?

    Our burkinis use swim-specific nylon or polyester-spandex, with close-fitting leggings and a fitted inner layer in the tunic. They meet the swim-garment rules at most public pools, but not at every pool. For pools with stricter rules, we offer close-fitting mid-thigh tunics and chlorine-resistant fabric such as LYCRA Xtra Life. The fabric composition goes on the label so pool staff can check it.

    Can one retail set combine different tunic, legging and hijab sizes for customers whose measurements do not fit a single set size?

    Yes. We cut each component separately, so your retail set can mix tunic, legging and hijab sizes. We make these to order or pack them from separates. Each set is packed together, and every piece carries its own label.

    Can you connect the tunic to the leggings to prevent separation in the water, and what attachment constructions can you offer?

    Yes. We join them at the inner waist with concealed ties, fabric loops or low-profile resin snaps. Before bulk production, you approve the attachment position, holding strength and comfort on the complete sample set.

    Which hijab ties, hem weights and detachable fastenings do you modify or exclude from children's modest sets to reduce snagging and small-part risks?

    On children's sets, we use pull-on hijabs with no loose ties, we replace hem weights with elasticated hems, and we attach sleeves and skirts permanently. Every snap, ring, slider and trim is sewn or riveted on and passes a 90 N pull test.
