Swimwear Fabric and Sourcing from Our Guangzhou Factory

Our core 80/20 nylon-spandex comes with matching linings, and on request we import Italian fabrics or any fabric you name by its supplier and code, including from the mill you already use.

Swim Fabrics Imported Direct from Italian Mills

Most of the swimwear we make uses 80% nylon 20% spandex at 180–200 GSM, and on request we import Italian fabrics such as Carvico directly from the knitting mills.

If you want a fabric we don't stock, send us its name, or the supplier name and fabric code. We can also import from the mill you already use. Just give us the supplier name and code.

Fabric Blends, Branded Fabrics and Mill Minimums

Swimwear hardware

Our Swimwear Hardware range includes power mesh, adjustable bra straps, braided cords and chlorine-resistant elastics. Our nylon/spandex or polyester straps and elastics come in widths from 3 to 25 mm.

They recover at least 95% of their stretch after 100 cycles and hold up to more than 200 hours of chlorine exposure.

Blends and specialist fabrics

We make nylon-spandex and polyester-spandex swimwear with matching linings. On request, we source chlorine-resistant elastane, recycled nylon, recycled polyester and Italian fabrics.

Other mills’ minimums

If you use your own supplier or a mill other than Carvico, the minimum is 50 to 100 m per fabric and colour, set by each mill.

We check it before costing, and we store any leftover metres free of charge for your later runs.

Component selection and costing

Our quotation process starts with choosing your fabric, ribbon, hardware and tags. This takes about a week, or longer depending on the suppliers.

We then use the component quantities per piece and your bulk order size to set your estimated production cost and total product cost.

Order Minimums, Deposits and Shipping Terms

  • Order minimum

    Orders start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, which gives small and growing labels a way in.

  • First trial run

    We agree your first trial quantity with you case by case. We're flexible up to the limits of our production requirements.

  • Payment terms

    You pay a 50% deposit when you confirm the order and the other 50% before shipment.

  • Shipping terms

    We ship FOB, CIF or DDP. With worldwide DDP, you get one all-in price delivered to your door, and we handle the duties, customs and freight.

From Sample to Bulk: Our Production Timeline

  1. Brief to sample

    We turn your brief or tech pack into a standard sample in 10 to 15 days.

  2. Approval to production

    Once you approve the sample and we receive your deposit, bulk production takes 35 to 40 days, depending on your order size and the season.

  3. Inspection to shipment

    When bulk production is finished, we do a full final inspection and then ship your order.

Our Own Guangzhou Factory for Fabric Sourcing

We make your swimwear in our own factory. There is just one, and it is in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. As a factory-owning company, we handle both production and export ourselves, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control.

To contact our team, use email, WhatsApp or the contact form on our website.

80/20 Nylon-Spandex and Our Stock Fabric Options

Our main blend

Most of the swimwear we make uses 80% nylon and 20% spandex, in weights of 180–200 GSM.

Collection fabric choices

For print-led collections, we use polyester-spandex. We also offer nylon-spandex and recycled options. We choose between nylon, polyester, spandex and Lycra for stretch, UV resistance, performance and long wear.

Stock and imported colours

Use stock colours for your core shades and save imports for the colours that define your collection. Imported Carvico minimums are 70 m for Vita and Sumatra and 50 m for Pantelleria.

Which Fabrics Take Digital Prints

If your collection is printed, our nylon-based recycled micro-terry, recycled crinkle, recycled metallic and post-consumer recycled nylon all take digital printing. Recycled polyester corduroy rib takes sublimation, which gives sharp, clean detail. Post-consumer recycled nylon also works for your plain-colour styles.

Chlorine, Salt Water and UPF Performance

Additional claim testing

If you want to claim more than the dry, relaxed rating, you need extra testing. We can arrange wet, stretched, selected-colourway, EU EN 13758-1 or US AATCC 183 tests through an accredited lab, and you pay the fees at cost.

Pool and training

If your styles are for regular use in chlorinated pools and for training, we choose recycled performance fabric or a fabric built on chlorine-resistant elastane such as LYCRA Xtra Life.

Beach and fashion

Metallic knits, high-shine charmeuse, micro-terry, crinkle and soft-touch fabrics are for beachwear and fashion styles, not for regular pool training.

Standard UPF testing

Our UPF 50+ fabrics are tested to AS/NZS 4399, the Australia and New Zealand standard, and we give you the documentation. The rating applies to the fabric when it's dry and relaxed.

Recycled Content and Fabric Certifications

Our recycled materials are GRS certified.

All of the recycled content comes from fishing nets, plastic bottles and post-consumer waste, and we give you the GRS documentation to back up your claims.

Our production certifications are OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Swim Fabric Swatch Kits and Samples

Order our real-fabric swatch kit before sampling so you can compare hand, sheen, stretch and print quality side by side.

The kit has five Italian nylon swatches, two recycled lustre swatches, three high-shine charmeuse swatches, one recycled micro-terry swatch, four recycled nylon digital prints and three recycled polyester sublimation prints. It also includes samples of recycled performance, recycled metallic, recycled sparkle and mesh fabric.

Each garment sample costs USD 50. The design fee is USD 50 per design, which covers its pattern or print in every colourway. We don't refund either fee, and neither one is credited against bulk orders.

Pantone Colour Matching and Custom Dyeing

Included colour matching

Pantone matching is included for fabric, screen printing and sublimation. Send us a Pantone reference or a physical swatch, and that includes team or club colours.

Approval before bulk

During sampling we confirm whether your colour can be achieved. We then make one round of lab dips on the actual production fabric for you to approve.

Custom dyeing requirements

Custom-dyed fabric takes about 4 weeks from your deposit. It has a 100-metre minimum and a USD 110 surcharge. For small runs, use our in-stock colour cards instead.

Colour and accessory timing

Send us your requirements for custom colours and branded accessories, and we'll confirm the timing for those parts of your order.

Wet Opacity, Linings and Fabric Care

White and pale swimwear risks turning see-through when it's wet.

For light colours, we build your styles with 190 to 220 g/m² shell fabric plus a lining, a double layer or a lined front panel, and we use a matching 140 g/m² lining. We confirm opacity on a wet sample.

Then we check the finished garment wet and stretched before you approve bulk production.

Chlorine Resistance and Fabric Testing

Before we cut your bulk fabric, we test chlorine resistance and colour fastness after repeated chlorine exposure. We also test stretch and recovery to check that the fabric returns to shape after wear and time in the water. Strength, softness and durability get tested too.

The tests you request depend on how your product will be used.

What Drives Your Fabric Cost

Standard nylon- or polyester-spandex blends cost less, so they suit your basics.

Branded or recycled LYCRA Xtra Life, Carvico, ECONYL and REPREVE fabrics cost more and suit your hero styles. Your quotation shows the fabric difference separately so you can compare. Italian fabric adds the most when your order can't use up a full mill minimum.

Metallic yarns, textured surfaces and custom dyeing also cost more than stock colours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy sourced swimwear fabric without a garment order?

No. We only source fabric for garments we make for you. We import or source the cloth you choose, then cut and sew it into your styles.

Can several styles jointly meet a mill minimum?

Yes. When styles share the same fabric and colour, we combine them into one fabric order: 70 m for Carvico Vita and Sumatra, or 50 m for Pantelleria. Each style still needs 50 pieces per colour. If you confirm shared fabrics and colours early, we start imports before sampling and production.

How much time does importing fabric add?

Importing from Italy or from your own mill adds about 7 to 10 days before sampling, because the sample yardage is couriered. It adds about 3 to 5 weeks before bulk for knitting or dyeing and shipping. Our 10 to 15 days for sampling and 35 to 40 days for bulk start once the fabric arrives. Bulk also needs your sample approval and your deposit.

Can you reserve fabric for repeat orders?

Yes. Once you confirm a repeat collection, we book fabric and colour against your forecast up to 6 months ahead and keep your leftover metres for your next run. If the mill discontinues the fabric, we tell you at least 8 weeks ahead and send two or three matched alternatives for you to approve before bulk.

Which linings are available, and when do you match the shell or use nude?

We offer nylon-spandex or recycled-nylon-spandex linings at about 110 to 140 g/m² in white, black, nude or colours matched to your shell. Your recycled-fabric styles get recycled lining. We match the shell for dark, bright, reversible and visible-edge styles. Nude goes under white, pale colours and light prints so the lining doesn't show through.
