Sustainability at Strandmill

We match recycled nylon and polyester to the stretch, hand feel, print and colour your designs need within your budget, in production holding OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS, RCS, bluesign and WRAP certification.

Recycled Fabrics and Low-Impact Production

We start by matching recycled fabrics to the stretch, hand feel, print and colour your swimwear needs. Then we source options that suit your design and budget.

  • Regenerated Fibre Performance

    Waste such as plastic items and old carpets is cleaned, sorted, liquefied and reformed, then made into fabric. Regenerated fibre performs much like new fibre. Any difference in performance comes from the fibre type and the way the fabric is constructed, not from the fibre being recycled.

  • Sustainable Fabric Choices

    On request, we source ECONYL regenerated nylon, including Italian Carvico ECONYL fabrics, REPREVE recycled polyester and 84/16 recycled polyester-spandex. We also offer organic and other sustainable fabrics, and we source these only from certified material suppliers.

  • Requested Fabrics And Trade-offs

    If you ask, we source imported GRS- or OEKO-TEX-certified recycled fabrics, regenerated Lycra, and waste-regenerated fabrics coloured with OEKO-TEX-certified dyes. Branded or imported certified options cost more and come in fewer colours with less stock, so a tight budget or deadline narrows your choice.

  • Swimwear Fabric Construction

    We make your swimwear in nylon or polyester blended with spandex, such as 80% nylon 20% spandex, with matching linings. Chlorine-resistant LYCRA Xtra Life is available if you ask for it.

  • Production And Waste

    We use water-saving, low-carbon production processes, manage production waste strictly and follow carbon-neutral packaging strategies. Choosing recycled polyester helps your line cut plastic waste.

  • Animal-free Production

    Our production carries PETA's 'Cruelty Free' stamp and the 'PETA-Approved Vegan' logo, and our process involves no animal testing.

  • Retail-ready Packaging Service

    Through our Swimwear Packaging & Custom Brand Labels service, you get retail-ready biodegradable packaging, branded accessories and logos heat-pressed inside, plus optional cardboard tags, connectors and individual hand-wrapping. We make labels and packaging in-house wherever we can.

  • Sample Development Costs

    A standard development sample is ready in 10 to 15 days and costs USD 50 each. On top of that, you pay USD 50 per design, meaning each pattern or print, with all its colourways included. Neither fee is refunded or credited against your bulk order.

Our Certifications, Audits and Transaction Certificates

Our ordering terms start at 50 pieces per style in each colour, with 35 to 40 days of bulk production and FOB, CIF or DDP shipping.

  • Production Standards

    We hold OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

  • Fabric-specific Certification

    We hold GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, and we supply GRS documentation, including for recycled polyester. Certified options are available on request, and your fabric determines which certificates apply.

  • Shipment-level Recycled Claims

    If your order uses GRS- or RCS-certified fabric, ask for a transaction certificate when you confirm the order. We then apply for the shipment-specific evidence of recycled-content percentage that retailers and labelling rules require. This goes beyond what mill certificates show.

  • Transaction Certificate Costs

    We pass on the certification body's fee at cost on your balance invoice. The extra paperwork takes time, so if your line makes no recycled-content claims, you don't need to pay for this.

  • Fibre-brand Trademark Approval

    Before you put ECONYL or REPREVE hangtags and logos on your labels, the fibre brand has to give trademark approval. Starting early gives the approval time to come through, and we provide the mill application documents.

  • Certificate Copies

    Our factory is BSCI certified. When you ask, we email you certificate copies within 1 business day, each showing the certificate number and validity date.

  • Buyer-appointed Factory Audits

    We accept factory and social-compliance audits by any auditor you appoint, as long as you give us 14 days' notice. You pay the audit fee.

  • Audit Summary Access

    Under an NDA, we share summaries of our latest WRAP and BSCI audits with you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you measure the carbon footprint of our order and share it with us?

Yes, we can prepare a carbon footprint estimate per piece and for your whole bulk swimwear order. It covers fabric, the energy used for cutting and sewing in our factory, packaging, and freight to your destination under your FOB, CIF or DDP terms. We calculate it with GHG Protocol emission factors, and we use fabric mill data wherever the mill provides it. You get a PDF report within 10 business days after shipment. Please note that this is our own calculation, not a figure verified by an outside party.

Can you take back fabric offcuts or old swimwear for recycling?

Yes. We sort the nylon, polyester and spandex offcuts from your orders by fibre and send them to a textile recycling partner, who turns them into fibre or filling. If you ask, we report the recycled weight for each order. We also take old swimwear from your brand's collection programme. It has to arrive at our factory in batches of at least 20 kg, and we pass it on to the same recycling partner.

Do you use plastic-free or recycled packaging and polybags when you ship our goods?

We offer biodegradable and recyclable bags. Our biodegradable corn-starch bags compost in industrial composting facilities within about 180 days. They don't break down in home compost or landfill. Our eco zip-lock bags with biodegradable cornstarch can be recycled wherever soft-plastic (LDPE 4) collection is accepted. Which claim applies depends on how your customers can dispose of the bag. We supply material supplier data for both bags. That data covers only the bag material, not the printed finished pack. You need to check any claim about disposing of the whole pack against your market's rules. We only print wording that you've approved in writing.

Do you subcontract any sewing or printing, and are those workshops held to the same standards as your own factory?

No. We do all sewing, printing and other production under one roof, and no stage goes to an outside workshop.
