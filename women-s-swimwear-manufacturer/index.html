Bikinis, One-Pieces and Tankinis From Our Factory in Guangzhou, China

We make your women’s swimwear ourselves, in our single factory in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

That includes bikinis in a wide choice of cuts and colours, one-pieces, tankinis, performance athletic swimsuits and long-sleeve styles designed for sun protection. We’ve run our factory and export business since 2011, so quality, cost and delivery stay under our direct control.

We check quality all the way through each order. We agree every B2B order directly with you through our website and team, never through online or third-party shops.

Inclusive Fits for Every Body: Extended Sizes, Cup Grading and Long Torsos

Extended sizing

For your collection, we make large-bust OEM/ODM swimwear in a range of colours, in sizes XS to 3XL, or up to 5XL in our plus-size range.

Band-and-cup grading

We grade tops to your own size chart, for example 32D to 38G. Our structured underwire tops cover D to DD+ and go up to G cup.

Size allocation

Each band-and-cup combination counts as one size. A wider cup range splits your order across more sizes and needs more fit approvals than letter sizing does.

Dedicated fit blocks

Stretching one letter-size pattern across different bust and torso shapes leaves cups gaping and straps digging in. We use band-and-cup grading and dedicated torso lengths instead.

Long-torso adjustment

You can have long-torso grading on every one-piece and tankini. It adds roughly 5 cm in total above and below the waist, and the bust and leg lines stay where they are.

Measurements to send

Send us your customer’s bust, waist, hip, height and torso girth. If the girth is more than 4 cm over our chart, we switch to the long-torso block.

Measuring torso girth

Stand straight. Run the tape from one shoulder down the front, between the legs and up the back to where it started, keeping it snug against bare skin or a thin layer.

Detail-specific samples

Any style with cut-outs, cross-back straps or placed prints gets its own fit sample, so those details stay in the right place.

Tummy Control, Body Shaping and Maternity Cuts

  • Shaping construction

    Our wholesale tummy-control one-pieces and bikinis, high-waisted cuts included, use power mesh front panels, double front linings or full compression bodies, either alone or combined.

  • Control levels

    You choose the level: light control with 160 GSM mesh, medium with a 200 GSM panel, or firm with a 240 GSM panel plus a double lining.

  • Control by use

    We use firm compression for structured shaping one-pieces and lighter control for everyday styles and high-waisted bottoms. If a shaping piece has too little compression, it won’t give the smoothing it was designed for.

  • Compression trade-offs

    Too much compression makes comfort styles hard to put on and uncomfortable to wear all day. Firmer control also adds layers, warmth, drying time and manufacturing cost.

  • Maternity silhouettes

    We develop maternity one-pieces, tankinis and bikinis. They have high-waisted bottoms that sit above or below the bump, ruched side panels that expand, and fronts cut 4 to 6 cm longer.

  • Bust and nursing support

    Shelf bras or soft cups provide maternity support. For nursing, we add pull-aside or clip-down cups to your design.

  • Maternity one-piece comfort

    Our maternity one-pieces have adjustable straps and a full soft lining, and they’re made without underwire.

  • Contoured shaping

    In our Plus Size Swimwear range, we shape the silhouette for your collection with contoured seam lines.

Performance Features Built for Comfort, Support and Durability

  • Functional options

    We add UPF 50+ sun protection, underwire support, quick-dry fabric and chlorine-resistant stitching to match your swimwear specification.

  • Construction compatibility

    Underwire needs the right channel and padding. Chlorine-resistant stitching needs chlorine-resistant elastic underneath it, or the construction won’t stand up to chlorine.

  • Sample confirmation

    We confirm all the features together on your fit sample, checking the padding system, sewing method and adjustable straps for fit and comfort.

  • Development trade-offs

    Each feature you add brings more materials to check and test, so it raises the cost and adds sampling time for those checks.

Premium and Recycled Fabrics With Water-Based Digital Prints

We cut custom bikinis and one-pieces from premium fabrics. For sustainable lines, we pair recycled nylon with water-based digital printing.

Actual-fabric approval

Before bulk production, we get your approval on a printed strike-off. Recycled and virgin bases take ink differently, so the actual fabric is what sets the colour and sharpness.

Cost and timing

Recycled fabric and strike-off approval cost more and take longer than stock fabric and screen-printed colours.

Fabric sourcing

Our Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing service supplies lined nylon/polyester-spandex options, including 80% nylon 20% spandex. Branded or recycled fabrics, textures, metallic yarns and custom dyeing are each quoted as a separate premium.

Italian fabric carries the largest premium when you order below a full minimum run.

Production credentials

Our sustainability credentials cover OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

Custom print development

Our Custom Printing service gives you chlorine-resistant, high-resolution sublimation, digital printing and nearly any colour or print that can be sourced. Exclusive patterns add cost and lead time.

Custom Swimwear Embellishment: Rhinestones, Beads and Trims

  • Decoration placement

    We place rhinestones, handcrafted beading and built-in hardware away from spots where they would rub skin or catch on fabric. Dense hand-beading is kept for statement styles.

  • Design-specific pricing

    Embellishment raises the unit price and the lead time. We quote each design on its stone count, beading density and choice of hardware, and we put most of the decoration on styles where looks come first.

  • Custom bead minimums

    Custom-moulded, colour-matched beads start at 1,000 pieces per design and colour. This minimum is separate from the garment order minimum.

  • Hand-beading schedule

    Hand-beading adds 1 to 2 weeks to bulk production. Denser work takes longer.

  • Hand-finished care

    Hand-finished pieces need gentler care, so we recommend them for resort and statement styles rather than active swimming.

  • Branded metal hardware

    Our Swimwear Hardware service supplies branded metal components that resist saltwater and chlorine. Standard hardware starts at 100 to 500 pieces per colour or size. Logo or embossed stoppers, clasps, hooks, sliders and rings start at 1,000 pieces per item.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you grade women's plus-size swimwear above 3XL, and up to what size and cup size?

Yes. Our standard grading runs from XS to 3XL, and our Plus Size Swimwear range goes past that, up to 5XL. Our structured underwire tops go up to G cup.

How do you stop removable cups from moving or folding inside the suit after washing?

We cut the cup pockets to the exact shape of the cup and give them side openings with a 1 cm overlap. The cup edges are rounded and tapered, so they sit flat against the lining. If you ask, we add a stitched tack point or a small hook-and-loop tab at the base of each pocket. We then wash the sample 5 times to confirm the cups stay flat and don't shift or fold.

Which outer-fabric and lining combinations do you use to keep white and pale swimwear opaque when wet and stretched?

We pair a dense 190 to 220 GSM warp-knit tricot shell with a self-fabric double layer, a 140 GSM stretch tricot lining or a lined front panel. Being opaque when dry doesn't prove a fabric covers when it's wet. That's why we check your sample wet and stretched, along with the removable-cup wash test.
