Rash Guards for Women, Men, Swim Schools and Teams

We make rash guards in compression and relaxed cuts for surfing, swimming, watersports, swim camps and custom team ranges, and you can order collections for women, men, swim schools and clubs.

For women, we offer long sleeves for full UV coverage, short and cap sleeves, zip fronts, fitted or relaxed cuts, and plus sizes that are graded accurately.

For men, there are long and short sleeves, compression shirts for training and relaxed styles for surfing, and we add custom branding for your team.

Factory ownership and checks

With our factory ownership and quality controls, production and export sit within one company. We check at fabric arrival, cutting and sewing, then carry out random checks and a full final inspection.

Enquiry response time

Rash Guard Fabrics, UPF Ratings and Certifications

Through Swimwear Fabrics & Sourcing, we source 80/20 nylon-spandex with matching linings, and we supply AS/NZS 4399 UPF 50+ documentation; extra lab testing is charged at cost.

Performance fabric range

Our four-way-stretch, UV-protective polyester-spandex weighs 180gsm to 320gsm. Water temperature and activity decide the weight first. Board contact and sun exposure decide the fibre.

Lightweight warm-water fabrics

At 180 to 200gsm, these fabrics hold little heat and air-dry in about 30 minutes. They suit warm-water surfing, swim lessons and long days in the sun.

Heavier supportive fabrics

At 260 to 320gsm, these fabrics keep wearers warmer in cooler water and give firmer compression and muscle support for training and watersports. The trade-off is that they take about 60 minutes to dry.

Repeated board contact

For repeated board contact, we recommend tightly knitted 200–240 GSM polyester-spandex or 80/20 nylon-spandex. Nylon stands up to rubbing best, and polyester holds its colour better in sun and salt water.

Surface wear

For frequent surfing, we recommend fabrics of 190 GSM or more; mesh and brushed fabrics wear faster against boards and wax.

Recycled fabric option

For eco-focused ranges, we offer on request a 190 g/m² fabric made of 78% recycled nylon and 22% spandex. It's rated UPF 50+ and holds GRS and OEKO-TEX certification.

Training and surf protection

You can choose moisture-wicking, four-way-stretch, UV-protective blends. We use quick-drying, moisture-wicking fabric for training and UPF 50+ fabric for surf and watersports styles.

Production certifications

Our production certifications cover OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for tested fabrics and trims, GRS and RCS for recycled fabrics, bluesign for chemistry, ISO 9001 for quality management and WRAP for social compliance.

  • Sleeves and necklines

Flatlock and Bonded Seams Built for Board and Water

Every seam gets durable flatlock stitching by default.

It works with every fabric we use and comes on every order at standard cost. On compression-fit styles, we place the seams ergonomically. On orders of 300 pieces or more, you can choose bonded body seams for a cleaner, flatter finish, at a higher cost.

Bonding needs a smooth polyester-spandex or nylon-spandex knit at 180–240 GSM. Mesh, brushed thermal, textured fabrics and anything over 240 GSM don't qualify, because the tape can't grip them evenly.

Even on bonded styles, we keep flatlock stitching at the high-stress joins: zip openings, thumb loops, hood joins and underarm gussets.

Custom Rash Guard Printing, Logos and Names

Full sublimation printing

For multi-colour, detailed all-over designs, we print edge to edge in unlimited colours. Sublimation dyes the polyester fibres, so the print doesn't crack or peel and the fabric keeps its stretch.

Simple logo methods

Screen printing suits designs with up to three colours. Screen printing or heat transfer puts simple logos onto pre-dyed fabric efficiently, and we help you choose the method.

Brand colour matching

We match sublimated rash guards to your exact brand colours and specifications, and we explain the dark-shade limitation before you approve the design.

Dark shade limitation

Sublimated black and very dark shades look less deep than fabric dyed in those colours before printing, and that difference stays in the finished garment.

Individual team personalisation

We personalise each piece with its own name or number, and we offer full sublimation for your custom team rash guards.

Private Label Rash Guards and Design Customisation

  • Private label services

    With our OEM and private label services, you customise the colours, prints, logos, branding and fabric, and add private labels and custom packaging.

  • Design starting points

    Our OEM and ODM development starts from your design, picture or idea. Our team either creates the garment or works from your artwork.

  • Panels and stitching

    We offer colour-blocked or contrast body panels and contrast flatlock thread. Fix these choices, and any mesh inserts, in your spec sheet before sampling.

  • Breathable mesh inserts

    Mesh at the underarms, sides or back makes the garment more breathable but reduces sun coverage in those areas. That makes it right for training and warm-water styles rather than sun-protection ranges.

  • Changes after sampling

    Changing panels or inserts alters the pattern, print layout and fabric use, so any change after sampling needs a new sample.

How Sleeves, Details and Seams Shape Your Quote

Long sleeves take more fabric than short or cap sleeves, so they cost more per unit, and thumb loops, zips and extra panels add sewing time. We quote from your spec sheet, fabric and quantity, so list sleeve length, neckline, trims, panels and seam type from the start.

A complete specification up front saves repricing later.

Washing and Drying Printed and Bonded Rash Guards

We write the care label for each style around how it's built and decorated, and we add specific warnings for bonded seams.

Rinsing, washing gently and drying in the shade protects sublimation, screen prints and heat-transfer logos. Heat, wringing, bleach and chlorine left in the fabric damage prints, elastane and seam adhesive faster than ordinary wear does.

On programme orders, passing these instructions on to whoever does the laundry keeps the garments cared for the same way. Longer instructions need a bigger label. If comfort matters more, we print the care information straight onto the garment instead.

Care steps from our rash guard labels.

  1. 01

    Rinse in fresh water

    Rinse the rash guard in fresh water after each use.

  2. 02

    Wash cold, inside out

    Hand wash, or machine wash cold and inside out on a gentle cycle, with no bleach.

  3. 03

    Dry flat in shade

    Dry flat in the shade, with no tumble drying or ironing. For bonded seams: no water above 30°C, no wringing and no fabric softener.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you make thermal or lined rash guards for colder water?

Yes. We make fleece-lined thermal rash guards in brushed polyester-spandex for swimming and surfing in cool water. Fleece-lined fabrics hold warmth, but they breathe less and dry more slowly. We build the construction you choose into a pre-production sample so you can wear-test it before approving bulk.

Which fabric options keep white or light-coloured rash guards opaque when wet and stretched?

For opaque coverage, a soft hand and strong stretch, we use a dense 220 GSM fabric of 85% polyester and 15% spandex. You can also add a lining, a double layer or a lined front panel. You judge opacity on the pre-production sample, wet and stretched on a body, not on a flat swatch. Dense fabrics breathe less and dry more slowly, and added layers and sample rounds add cost and lead time.

What anti-ride-up options can you make for surf rash guards, such as gripper hems or board-short attachment loops?

On any surf style, we can add a silicone grip hem, a lockdown loop that ties to your board-short drawcord, or both. They keep the shirt down while you paddle and duck-dive. You wear-test your chosen option in the pre-production sample before approving bulk.

What zipper backing or chin-guard construction can you provide to prevent rubbing on zip-front rash guards?

We sew a soft 2 cm backing strip along the full length of the zipper and fold a fabric chin guard over the collar zip pull, so the teeth and slider stay off your skin. For extra comfort at the neck, we also offer a soft coil zip with a 3 cm zip garage at the top. You check comfort by wearing the pre-production sample.

How do you limit the white base showing through dark sublimation prints when a fitted rash guard stretches?

We print on polyester that's pre-dyed in a matching dark shade, so when it stretches, that colour shows through instead of white. Very light artwork colours can't be printed on this dark base. We also raise ink density by about 15% in the dark areas and check the sample print stretched by 30% before bulk approval. You judge the garment wet and stretched on a body.
